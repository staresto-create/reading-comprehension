109 指考閱讀測驗：現代廢水處理流程

Modern Wastewater Treatment Process
指考 109年第 36–39 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Early civilizations often built drainage systems in urban areas to handle rainwater that ran down the street during a storm. The Romans constructed elaborate systems that also drained wastewater from the public baths. However, as the population of the cities grew, the old drainage systems became overloaded. During the Industrial Revolution, manufacturing waste was added to sewage, which increased the need for more efficient sewage treatment. In the mid-19th century, the first steps were taken to treat wastewater.

第 2 段Sewage or wastewater treatment in modern times is the process of removing harmful physical, chemical, and biological elements from wastewater and house sewage. The whole process starts with screening out large objects such as paper and wood, and removing heavy materials like dirt. The screened wastewater is then ready to go through a series of concrete tanks for further treatment. In the second step, the sewage passes into the primary tanks. Here, human waste, called sludge, settles to the bottom while oils and grease float to the top, where they are collected. At the same time, organic matter like eggshells or coffee grounds in the sewage is broken down into smaller substances. The remaining sewage then enters the secondary tanks for the third stage of treatment. The solids that were not treated in the primary tanks are removed here through decomposition, which digests the material. Then, the liquid sewage is filtered through sand. This filtering process gets rid of almost all bacteria, as well as other solid particles that remain in the water. Finally, the wastewater flows into the last tanks, where the chemical chlorine is added to kill the remaining bacteria. After the bacteria are destroyed, the chlorine is eliminated from the water, and the treated clean water is discharged into a river or the ocean.

逐段中譯

第 1 段早期的文明常在都市地區興建排水系統，處理暴風雨時順著街道流下的雨水。羅馬人建造了精密的排水系統，也排放公共浴場的廢水。然而，隨著城市人口增加，舊的排水系統變得不堪負荷。工業革命時期，製造業的廢棄物加進了汙水，處理汙水更需要有效率的方法。到了 19 世紀中期，人類開始踏出處理廢水的第一步。

第 2 段現代的汙水（廢水）處理，是把廢水和家庭汙水中有害的物理、化學和生物成分去除的過程。整個過程從篩除紙張、木頭等大型物品開始，同時去除泥土等重物。篩過的廢水接著要流經一連串的混凝土池，做進一步的處理。第二步，汙水流進初級池。在這裡，人類的排泄物，也就是所謂的汙泥，沉到池底，油和油脂則浮到上面，收集起來。同時，汙水裡蛋殼、咖啡渣等有機物，會分解成比較小的物質。剩下的汙水接著進入二級池，做第三階段的處理。初級池沒有處理到的固體，在這裡經過分解，消化掉。然後，液態汙水用沙過濾。這道過濾程序可以去除幾乎所有的細菌，以及水中殘留的其他固體微粒。最後，廢水流進最後的池子，加入氯這種化學物質，殺死剩下的細菌。細菌消滅以後，再把水中的氯除去，處理乾淨的水就排進河川或海洋。

字詞搭配 配對與接法

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
  • add A to B把 A 加進 B第 1 段原文：manufacturing waste was added to sewage
  • the need for + N對……的需求第 1 段原文：increased the need for more efficient sewage treatment
  • take steps to + V採取步驟……第 1 段原文：the first steps were taken to treat wastewater
  • remove A from B從 B 去除 A第 2 段原文：removing harmful physical, chemical, and biological elements from wastewater
  • screen out + N篩除……第 2 段原文：screening out large objects
  • a series of + N一連串的……第 2 段原文：a series of concrete tanks
  • settle to the bottom沉到底部第 2 段原文：settles to the bottom
  • float to the top浮到上層第 2 段
  • break down + sth + into + N把……分解成……第 2 段原文：is broken down into smaller substances
  • get rid of + N除掉……第 2 段原文：gets rid of almost all bacteria
  • discharge + sth + into + 地方把……排放到……第 2 段原文：is discharged into a river or the ocean
