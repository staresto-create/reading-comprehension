108 學測閱讀測驗：「腦中神曲」現象

Earworms
學測 108年第 49–52 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Music has a tendency to get stuck in our heads. Sometimes a tune intrudes on our thoughts and then plays, and replays, in a never-ending loop. This interesting phenomenon becomes a subject explored by many scientists. They use a range of terms to describe it—stuck-song syndrome, sticky music, cognitive itch, or most commonly “earworm.” Earworms can run around our heads for several minutes to numerous hours. As the melody repeats, it becomes embedded into our mind. Even though our ears do not hear the tune, our brain continues to play it.

第 2 段Earworms often take the form of song fragments rather than entire songs, and the song is usually a familiar one. Researchers are not sure why some songs are more likely to get stuck in our heads than others, but everyone has their own tunes. Often those songs have a simple, upbeat melody and catchy, repetitive lyrics, such as popular commercial jingles and slightly annoying radio hits. Recent or repeated exposure to a song or even a small part of a song can also trigger earworms, as can word associations, such as a phrase similar to the lyrics of a song.

第 3 段While earworms might be annoying, most people who experience them nevertheless report that they are pleasant or at least neutral. Only a third of people are disturbed by the song in their heads. How people cope with their earworms seems to depend on how they feel about them. Those who have positive feelings about their stuck songs prefer to just “let them be,” while those with negative feelings turn to more behavioral responses, which include coping strategies such as singing, talking, or even praying.

第 49 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 1 段Sometimes a tune intrudes on our thoughts and then plays, and replays, in a never-ending loop.

第 1 段As the melody repeats, it becomes embedded into our mind.

原文 → 正確選項
As the melody repeats → melody repeating
第 1 段說一段旋律闖進思緒後不停重播，旋律一再重複就嵌進腦海；選項的 a melody repeating in our heads 說的就是這件事。(A) 照抄 ears，原文說的是耳朵沒聽到、大腦還在播放，earworm 不是真的蟲；(D) 照抄第 2 段的 commercial，廣告歌曲只是容易變成耳蟲的一種歌；(B) 第 2 段說每個人都有自己的耳蟲歌，但沒有說耳蟲是用個人方式記住的旋律。
第 50 題 詞義題 Vocabulary in context

第 1 段This interesting phenomenon becomes a subject explored by many scientists.

subject 有好幾個意思：學科、受試者、主題。這一句說這個現象成了許多科學家探究（explored）的 subject，所以是研究的主題，也就是 a matter being studied。(B) 課程、(C) 實驗的受試者，都是 subject 的其他常見意思，放進這一句說不通；(A) 文章說的是一種常見的現象，不是精神疾病。
第 51 題 細節題（未提及） Supporting details

第 2 段Often those songs have a simple, upbeat melody and catchy, repetitive lyrics, such as popular commercial jingles and slightly annoying radio hits.

第 2 段Recent or repeated exposure to a song or even a small part of a song can also trigger earworms, as can word associations, such as a phrase similar to the lyrics of a song.

三個有提到的都換了說法：第 2 段的 repeated exposure to a song 是 (A) Tunes repeatedly encountered，recent exposure to a small part of a song 是 (C) Fragments of songs recently heard，a phrase similar to the lyrics of a song 是 (D) Words similar to the lyrics of a song。原文說的是 simple, upbeat melody（簡單輕快的旋律），沒有提到節奏流暢，所以選 (B)。
第 52 題 主旨題 Main idea

第 3 段How people cope with their earworms seems to depend on how they feel about them.

原文 → 正確選項
cope with → react to
第 3 段先說多數人覺得耳蟲愉快或無所謂，接著點出重點：人們怎麼應付耳蟲，取決於他們對耳蟲的感受，再分別說明有正面、負面感受的人怎麼做，所以這一段講的是人們對耳蟲的反應；cope with 在選項換成 react to。(A) 照抄 annoying，但這一段說的是大多數人並不覺得煩；(C) 唱歌、說話、祈禱只是部分人應付耳蟲的方法，原文沒說要消滅耳蟲；(D) 什麼時候開始注意到耳蟲，這一段沒有提。

逐段中譯

第 1 段音樂很容易卡在腦子裡。有時候一段旋律闖進我們的思緒，接著不停播放、重播，沒完沒了。這個有趣的現象成了許多科學家研究的主題，他們用各種名稱來描述它：卡歌症候群、黏人音樂、認知搔癢，最常用的是「耳蟲」。耳蟲可以在腦中盤旋幾分鐘到好幾個小時。旋律一再重複，就深深嵌進我們的腦海；即使耳朵沒有聽到這段旋律，大腦還是繼續播放。

第 2 段耳蟲常常是歌曲的片段，而不是整首歌，而且通常是熟悉的歌。研究人員不確定為什麼有些歌比其他歌更容易卡在腦中，不過每個人都有自己的耳蟲歌。這些歌常常旋律簡單輕快，歌詞朗朗上口、一再重複，像是流行的廣告歌曲，以及有點煩人的電台熱門歌曲。最近聽過或反覆聽到一首歌，甚至只是歌曲的一小段，都可能引發耳蟲；字詞的聯想也會，例如聽到和某首歌歌詞相似的句子。

第 3 段耳蟲雖然可能惱人，大部分有過這種經驗的人卻表示，感覺是愉快的，至少無所謂，只有三分之一的人會因為腦中的歌感到困擾。人們怎麼應付耳蟲，似乎取決於他們對耳蟲的感受。對卡在腦中的歌有正面感受的人，寧願「隨它去」；有負面感受的人則會採取比較多的行動，像是唱歌、說話，甚至祈禱，來應付耳蟲。
