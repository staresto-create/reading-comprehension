106 指考閱讀測驗：札哈·哈蒂的藝術生涯與建築理念

Zaha Hadid's Artistic Career and Architectural Philosophy
指考 106年第 36–39 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Often named as the most prominent contemporary female architect, Zaha Hadid, an Iraqi-born British woman, is significant for her intellectual toughness and her refusal to compromise on her artistic ideas. For many years, her designs filled the pages of architecture journals but were dismissed as impractical or too radical. Also, being female in a male-dominated field didn’t help her succeed.

第 2 段Despite these setbacks, her star began to rise when her design for Cincinnati’s new Center for Contemporary Art was selected and built, earning her worldwide acclaim. The New York Times described the building as “the most important new building in America since the Cold War.” Once her talent was recognized, commissions started coming in to design a variety of projects, including public transportation, libraries, and opera houses. In 2004, Hadid became the first woman to win the prestigious Pritzker Prize. She also won the Stirling Prize in 2010 and 2011.

第 3 段Hadid’s interest in architecture had roots in a trip her family took to the ancient Sumer region in southern Iraq, the site of one of the world’s oldest civilizations, when she was a teenager. She recalled: “The beauty of the landscape—where sand, water, reeds, birds, buildings, and people all somehow flowed together—has never left me. I’m trying to discover—invent, I suppose—an architecture, and forms of urban planning, that do something of the same thing in a contemporary way.”

第 4 段Nature’s forms appear as a recurrent source of inspiration for Hadid’s architecture. Her designs are daring and visionary experiments with space and with the relationships of buildings to their urban surroundings. She consistently pushes the boundaries of architecture and urban design in the pursuit of a visionary aesthetic that expresses her ideals.

逐段中譯

第 1 段札哈·哈蒂是在伊拉克出生的英國女性，常有人稱她是當代最傑出的女性建築師。她之所以重要，是因為她思想強韌，又絕不在藝術理念上妥協。多年來，她的設計常常登上建築期刊，卻也常常讓人覺得不切實際、太過激進而不予理會。此外，在男性主導的領域裡身為女性，對她的成功也沒有幫助。

第 2 段儘管遇到這些挫折，她設計的辛辛那提新當代藝術中心獲選並且落成，讓她贏得全世界的讚譽，她從此開始走紅。《紐約時報》說這棟建築是「冷戰以來美國最重要的新建築」。她的才華一旦獲得肯定，各式各樣的設計委託就接踵而來，包括大眾運輸、圖書館和歌劇院。2004 年，哈蒂成為第一位獲得聲望崇高的普立茲克獎的女性。她也在 2010 年和 2011 年獲得斯特林獎。

第 3 段哈蒂對建築的興趣，源自她青少年時期全家到伊拉克南部古蘇美地區的一趟旅行，那裡是世界上最古老的文明之一的所在地。她回憶說：「那裡的風景很美，沙、水、蘆葉、鳥、建築和人，都以某種方式融為一體，這片風景一直留在我心裡。我想找出──或者說創造出──一種建築和都市規劃的形式，用當代的方式做到同樣的事。」

第 4 段大自然的形態一再成為哈蒂建築的靈感來源。她的設計是大膽而有遠見的實驗，探索空間，也探索建築和周圍城市環境的關係。她不斷突破建築和都市設計的界限，追求一種有遠見、能表達她理想的美學。
