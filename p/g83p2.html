83 學測閱讀測驗：徒手攀爬世貿中心的George Willig

George Willig's World Trade Center Climb
學測 83年第 34–36 題3 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段On May 26, 1977, George Willig traveled to the top of the 1,350-foot-tall World Trade Center in New York City. He did not take the elevator, however. Instead, like a human fly, George Willig climbed the outside of the immense 110-story building. His climb, which began at 6:30 a.m., took three and a half hours to finish.

第 2 段At the top, Willig was greeted by both police and reporters. Some of the police officers asked him for his autograph. Then they wanted to put him in jail and charged him with violation of law for climbing the building without a permit. Furthermore, the city also planned to sue him for a quarter of a million dollars. The amount, the police department reported, would pay the costs of rushing special equipment and eighty police officers in order to save him.

第 3 段By the next day, city officials had reevaluated the situation. At City Hall, Mayor Abraham Beame hosted a news conference that turned into a ceremony in George Willig's honor. Beame announced that the city would not punish "The Human Fly." Instead, he would be fined a penny for each of the 110 floors he had climbed. Instead of punishing Willig, the city treated him as a hero who attempted the impossible and met the challenge.

題目

34.George Willig reached the top of the World Trade Center in New York at

  1. (A)six thirty in the morning of May 26, 1977.
  2. (B)six thirty in the evening of May 26, 1977.
  3. (C)ten o'clock in the morning of May 26, 1977.
  4. (D)ten o'clock in the evening of May 26, 1977.

35.This passage is a story about

  1. (A)a man who enjoys dangers and risks.
  2. (B)a human being who can fly like a fly.
  3. (C)a man who has escaped from the jail.
  4. (D)a fly which climbs like a human being.

36.According to the passage, which of the following statements is true?

  1. (A)Willig was fined $250,000.
  2. (B)Willig was sent to prison for a year.
  3. (C)Willig was honored in a party and fined only $1.10.
  4. (D)Willig climbed the World Trade Center with permission.

每一題的依據句

第 34 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段His climb, which began at 6:30 a.m., took three and a half hours to finish.

第 1 段說 Willig 早上 6 點 30 分（6:30 a.m.）開始爬，花了三個半小時才爬完。6 點 30 分加上三個半小時是 10 點，所以他在早上 10 點到達頂端，是 (C)。(A) 6 點 30 分是他開始爬的時間；(B)、(D) 是晚上，原文寫的是 a.m.，指的是上午。
第 35 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 1 段說 Willig 不搭電梯，貼著 110 層樓的外牆爬上世界貿易中心，第 2 段說他爬到頂樓後，警方要控告他未經許可攀爬大樓，市政府也打算向他求償，第 3 段說市政府隔天改變態度，把他當成敢於挑戰不可能的英雄。這是一個喜歡冒險、不怕危險的人的故事，所以是 (A)。三個錯誤選項都偏離主題：(B) 照抄 fly，human fly 是形容他像蒼蠅一樣爬牆，他並不會飛；(D) 也照抄 fly，故事的主角是人，不是蒼蠅；(C) 照抄 jail，第 2 段說警方想把他關進監獄，但他沒有坐牢，更沒有越獄。
錯誤選項：(B) 偏離主題；(C) 偏離主題；(D) 偏離主題
第 36 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 3 段a ceremony in George Willig's honor

第 3 段Instead, he would be fined a penny for each of the 110 floors he had climbed.

原文 → 正確選項
honor → honored
第 3 段說市長舉行的記者會變成了表揚 Willig 的典禮（a ceremony in George Willig's honor），而且只照他爬的 110 層樓，每層罰一美分。110 美分就是 1.10 美元，所以是 (C)。(A) 25 萬美元是市政府原本打算向他求償的金額，第二天就改了；(B) 警方原本想把他關進監獄，但原文沒有說他坐了一年牢；(D) 第 2 段說他是未經許可（without a permit）攀爬大樓。

逐段中譯

第 1 段1977 年 5 月 26 日，George Willig 登上了紐約市 1,350 英尺高的世界貿易中心頂端。不過，他不是搭電梯上去的，而是像蒼蠅一樣，貼著這棟 110 層樓的巨大建築外牆往上爬。他從早上 6 點 30 分開始爬，花了三個半小時才爬完。

第 2 段到了頂樓，迎接 Willig 的有警察，也有記者。有些警察還向他要簽名。接著警方想把他關進監獄，控告他未經許可攀爬大樓，觸犯法律。不只這樣，市政府還打算向他求償 25 萬美元。警察局說，這筆錢是用來支付緊急調派特殊裝備和 80 名警察去救他的費用。

第 3 段到了第二天，市府官員重新評估了整件事。市長 Abraham Beame 在市政廳舉行記者會，結果記者會變成了表揚 George Willig 的典禮。Beame 宣布市政府不會處罰這位「人蒼蠅」（The Human Fly），只會照他爬的 110 層樓，每一層罰他一美分。市政府沒有處罰 Willig，反而把他當成敢於挑戰不可能、而且成功完成挑戰的英雄。
