100 學測閱讀測驗：紅杉的生態

The Ecology of Redwood Trees
學測 100年第 53–56 題4 題

文章

第 1 段Redwood trees are the tallest plants on the earth, reaching heights of up to 100 meters. They are also known for their longevity, typically 500 to 1000 years, but sometimes more than 2000 years. A hundred million years ago, in the age of dinosaurs, redwoods were common in the forests of a much more moist and tropical North America. As the climate became drier and colder, they retreated to a narrow strip along the Pacific coast of Northern California.

第 2 段The trunk of redwood trees is very stout and usually forms a single straight column. It is covered with a beautiful soft, spongy bark. This bark can be pretty thick, well over two feet in the more mature trees. It gives the older trees a certain kind of protection from insects, but the main benefit is that it keeps the center of the tree intact from moderate forest fires because of its thickness. This fire resistant quality explains why the giant redwood grows to live that long. While most other types of trees are destroyed by forest fires, the giant redwood actually prospers because of them. Moderate fires will clear the ground of competing plant life, and the rising heat dries and opens the ripe cones of the redwood, releasing many thousands of seeds onto the ground below.

第 3 段New trees are often produced from sprouts, little baby trees, which form at the base of the trunk. These sprouts grow slowly, nourished by the root system of the “mother” tree. When the main tree dies, the sprouts are then free to grow as full trees, forming a “fairy ring” of trees around the initial tree. These trees, in turn, may give rise to more sprouts, and the cycle continues.

逐段中譯

第 1 段紅杉是地球上最高的植物，可以長到 100 公尺高。紅杉也以長壽聞名，通常能活 500 到 1000 年，有時甚至超過 2000 年。一億年前的恐龍時代，北美洲比現在潮濕得多，屬於熱帶氣候，當時紅杉在森林裡很常見。後來氣候變得比較乾冷，紅杉就退縮到北加州太平洋沿岸的一條狹長地帶。

第 2 段紅杉的樹幹非常粗壯，通常長成一根筆直的柱子。樹幹外面覆蓋著漂亮、柔軟、像海綿一樣的樹皮。這層樹皮可以相當厚，比較成熟的樹，樹皮厚度超過兩英尺。樹皮讓老樹在某種程度上不受昆蟲侵害，但最主要的好處是，樹皮夠厚，遇到中等規模的森林火災時，能保護樹心完好無損。這種耐火的特性，就是巨大的紅杉能活那麼久的原因。大部分其他種類的樹木會毀於森林火災，巨大的紅杉卻反而因為森林火災長得更好。中等規模的火災會清除地面上和紅杉競爭的植物，上升的熱氣也會烘乾成熟的紅杉毬果，讓毬果張開，把成千上萬顆種子撒在下方的地面上。

第 3 段新的樹常常從嫩芽長出來，嫩芽就是樹幹基部長出的小樹苗。這些嫩芽長得很慢，靠「母樹」的根系提供養分。母樹死了以後，嫩芽就能自由長成大樹，在原來那棵樹的周圍形成一圈「仙女環」。這些樹接著又可能長出更多嫩芽，就這樣循環下去。
