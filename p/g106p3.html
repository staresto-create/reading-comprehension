Winslow Homer

文章

第 1 段Winslow Homer (1836-1910) is regarded by many as the greatest American painter of the nineteenth century. Born and raised in Boston, he began his career at age eighteen in his hometown, working as an apprentice at a printing company. Skilled at drawing, he soon made a name for himself making illustrations for novels, sheet music, magazines, and children’s books.

第 2 段He then moved to New York City, where he worked as a freelance illustrator with Harper’s Weekly, a popular magazine of the time, and began painting. Homer was assigned to cover the inauguration of President Lincoln and, later, the Civil War. His pictures of the Union troops won international recognition. Homer moved to England and, after a two-year stay, returned to America. He settled permanently in Maine in 1883.

第 3 段From the late 1850s until his death in 1910, Winslow Homer produced a body of work distinguished by its thoughtful expression and its independence from artistic conventions. A man of multiple talents, Homer excelled equally in the arts of illustration, oil painting, and watercolor. Many of his works—depictions of children at play and in school, farm girls attending to their work, hunters and their prey—have become classic images of nineteenth-century American life. Others speak to more universal themes such as the primal relationship of humans to nature.

第 4 段This two-week exhibition highlights a wide and representative range of Homer’s art. It shows his extraordinary career from the battlefields, farmland, and coastal villages of America, to the North Sea fishing village of Cullercoats, the rocky coast of Maine, the Adirondacks, and the Caribbean. The exhibition offers viewers an opportunity to experience and appreciate the breadth of his remarkable artistic achievement.

第 49 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 4 段說「這次為期兩週的展覽」呈現霍默的創作生涯，讓觀眾有機會欣賞他的作品；前三段介紹畫家的生平和作品特色，正是美術館網站介紹展覽的寫法，所以是 (B)。(D) 印刷只在第 1 段提到霍默當過印刷公司的學徒，過於狹隘；(A) 霍默是 19 世紀的畫家，不是當代藝術，(C) 霍默是美國畫家，只在英國住過兩年，都偏離主題。
錯誤選項：(A) 偏離主題；(C) 偏離主題；(D) 過於狹隘（只講第 1 段）
第 50 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 1 段Born and raised in Boston, he began his career at age eighteen in his hometown, working as an apprentice at a printing company.

第 1 段Skilled at drawing, he soon made a name for himself making illustrations for novels, sheet music, magazines, and children’s books.

原文 → 正確選項
made a name for himself → established his reputation
hometown → hometown
第 1 段說霍默在家鄉波士頓的印刷公司當學徒，很快就以繪製插畫出名（made a name for himself），選項換成 established his reputation as an illustrator。(A) 照抄第 2 段的 England，但讓他贏得國際肯定的是描繪北軍的畫作，不是描繪英國的畫；(B) 照抄 greatest，原文說他是最偉大的畫家（painter），不是最偉大的插畫家；(C) 照抄第 3 段的 watercolor，原文說他的插畫、油畫和水彩都同樣出色（excelled equally），沒說水彩比較有名。

第 3 段Many of his works—depictions of children at play and in school, farm girls attending to their work, hunters and their prey—have become classic images of nineteenth-century American life.

原文 → 正確選項
nineteenth-century American life → life of nineteenth-century Americans
第 3 段說霍默的許多作品，像是孩子遊戲和上學、農家女孩工作、獵人和獵物，都成了 19 世紀美國生活的經典畫面，選項把 classic images of nineteenth-century American life 換成 vividly portrayed the life of nineteenth-century Americans。(B) 照抄 thoughtful，原文說他的作品表達深刻，沒說是替受戰爭之苦的人發聲；(C) 照抄 artistic，原文說他不受藝術傳統束縛（independence from artistic conventions），選項說他遵循傳統，方向正好相反；(D) 文章沒說他想逃離社會。
第 52 題 詞義題（指代詞） Vocabulary in context

第 3 段Many of his works

第 3 段Others speak to more universal themes such as the primal relationship of humans to nature.

原文 → 正確選項
works → works
第 3 段先說他的許多作品（Many of his works）成了美國生活的經典畫面，下一句接著說 Others 觸及更普遍的主題。Many of his works…Others… 前後對照，Others 是他的其他作品，所以是 (C)。(B) 照抄 themes，主題是這些作品觸及的內容，不是 Others 本身；(D) 照抄 images，經典畫面是前一句對那批作品的評價；(A) 這一段沒有提到其他藝術家。

逐段中譯

第 1 段許多人認為溫斯洛·霍默（1836–1910）是 19 世紀最偉大的美國畫家。他在波士頓出生長大，十八歲在家鄉展開職業生涯，在一家印刷公司當學徒。他擅長繪畫，很快就以替小說、樂譜、雜誌和兒童書繪製插畫而出名。

第 2 段後來他搬到紐約市，替當時很受歡迎的雜誌 Harper’s Weekly 擔任自由插畫家，也開始作畫。霍默受派報導林肯總統的就職典禮，後來又報導南北戰爭。他描繪北軍的畫作贏得國際肯定。霍默後來搬到英國，住了兩年後回到美國，1883 年在緬因州定居。

第 3 段從 1850 年代後期到 1910 年去世，霍默創作了大量作品，特色是表達深刻，而且不受藝術傳統束縛。霍默多才多藝，插畫、油畫和水彩都同樣出色。他的許多作品，像是描繪孩子遊戲和上學、農家女孩忙著做事、獵人和獵物的畫，都已經成為 19 世紀美國生活的經典畫面。其他作品則觸及更普遍的主題，例如人和大自然之間最原始的關係。

第 4 段這次為期兩週的展覽，展出霍默藝術作品中範圍廣泛又具代表性的一批。展覽呈現他非凡的創作生涯，從美國的戰場、農田和海邊村落，到北海的漁村卡勒科茨、緬因州的岩岸、阿第倫達克山脈和加勒比海。觀眾可以藉這次展覽，體會並欣賞他卓越藝術成就的廣度。

綱要
  1. 介紹霍默是許多人眼中 19 世紀最偉大的美國畫家，在故鄉波士頓當印刷學徒，以插畫成名。

  3. 旅居英國兩年後回國，1883 年定居緬因州。
