The Lipstick Effect
指考 99課綱參考試卷（102年施測）第 40–43 題4 題

文章

If you’ve been following the news recently, you’ve probably heard about the “lipstick effect.” It refers to the tendency for consumers, during an economic recession, to purchase smaller, cheaper items such as lipstick rather than large luxury items to cheer themselves up. The theory was first identified in the Great Depression in the 1930s, when industrial production in the US was sharply reduced but sales of cosmetics rose. However, it was Leonard Lauder, chairman of a cosmetics firm, Estée Lauder, who coined the phrase in the 2001 recession. The sale of lipstick in America during the recession increased by 11%. Recent sales figures from the world’s big cosmetic companies confirmed that the so-called “lipstick effect” has returned as the global economy is now in the middle of a downturn.

Though lipstick sales are red hot, not everyone is smiling. When consumer trust in the economy is dropping, consumers will buy goods that have less impact on their available funds. So women see lipstick, which can sell for as little as $1.99, as a reasonable indulgence and pick-me-up when they feel their budgets are squeezed and they can’t afford a whole new outfit. Obviously men will not be buying lipstick, but since they cannot afford to purchase large luxuries like cars, they might be tempted to buy a new type of beer or smaller and less costly gadgets such as an electronic razor.

題目

40.According to the passage, when may the “lipstick effect” occur?

  1. (A)When people’s budgets are tight.
  2. (B)When the economy is recovering.
  3. (C)When women want to look attractive.
  4. (D)When cosmetic companies put lipstick on sale.

41.Why are people unhappy when lipstick is selling well?

  1. (A)Because women are too wasteful.
  2. (B)Because it affects industrial production.
  3. (C)Because it is a sign of an economic recession.
  4. (D)Because Estée Lauder is making too much money.

42.Which of the following does pick-me-up most likely mean?

  1. (A)Something that sells very well.
  2. (B)Something that costs you a fortune.
  3. (C)Something that makes you feel better.
  4. (D)Something that you can buy anywhere.

43.According to the “lipstick effect” theory, which of the following items might be bought by men during an economic recession?

  1. (A)A gold watch.
  2. (B)A necktie.
  3. (C)A trip around the world.
  4. (D)A big house.

綱要
  1. 「口紅效應」指經濟不景氣時，消費者為了讓自己開心，會買口紅之類較小、較便宜的東西，(rather than) 而不買大件奢侈品。
  2. 這個現象最早在 1930 年代經濟大蕭條時期發現，當時美國工業生產銳減，(but) 化妝品銷量卻上升。
  3. (However) 不過「口紅效應」這個詞是雅詩蘭黛董事長 Leonard Lauder 在 2001 年經濟衰退時提出的，那次美國口紅銷量增加 11%；最近全球經濟低迷，口紅效應又出現了。
  4. (Though) 口紅賣得火紅，卻不是人人笑得出來：(When) 消費者對經濟沒信心時，會買不傷荷包的東西，(So) 所以買不起新衣的女性，就買一支口紅犒賞自己、提振心情。
  5. (Obviously) 男性當然不會買口紅，(but) 但買不起汽車這類大件奢侈品時，可能改買新口味的啤酒，或電動刮鬍刀之類比較便宜的小玩意。
