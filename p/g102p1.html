102 學測閱讀測驗：瑞士刀的起源

The Origin of the Swiss Army Knife
學測 102年第 41–44 題4 題

文章

第 1 段The Swiss army knife is a popular device that is recognized all over the world. In Switzerland, there is a saying that every good Swiss citizen has one in his or her pocket. But the knife had humble beginnings.

第 2 段In the late nineteenth century, the Swiss army issued its soldiers a gun that required a special screwdriver to dismantle and clean it. At the same time, canned food was becoming common in the army. Swiss generals decided to issue each soldier a standard knife to serve both as a screwdriver and a can opener.

第 3 段It was a lifesaver for Swiss knife makers, who were struggling to compete with cheaper German imports. In 1884, Carl Elsener, head of the Swiss knife manufacturer Victorinox, seized that opportunity with both hands, and designed a soldier’s knife that the army loved. It was a simple knife with one big blade, a can opener, and a screwdriver.

第 4 段A few years after the soldier’s knife was issued, the “Schweizer Offizier Messer,” or Swiss Officer’s Knife, came on the market. Interestingly, the Officer’s Knife was never given to those serving in the army. The Swiss military purchasers considered the new model with a corkscrew for opening wine not “essential for survival,” so officers had to buy this new model by themselves. But its special multi-functional design later launched the knife as a global brand. After the Second World War, a great number of American soldiers were stationed in Europe. And as they could buy the Swiss army knife at shops on military bases, they bought huge quantities of them. However, it seems that “Schweizer Offizier Messer” was too difficult for them to say, so they just called it the Swiss army knife, and that is the name it is now known by all over the world.

第 42 題 詞義題（指代詞） Vocabulary in context

第 2 段Swiss generals decided to issue each soldier a standard knife to serve both as a screwdriver and a can opener.

第 3 段It was a lifesaver for Swiss knife makers, who were struggling to compete with cheaper German imports.

原文 → 正確選項
issue each soldier a standard knife → needed a knife for every soldier

逐段中譯

第 1 段瑞士刀是全世界都認得的熱門工具。瑞士有一句話說，每個好公民口袋裡都有一把瑞士刀。不過這把刀的出身其實很平凡。

第 2 段19 世紀末，瑞士陸軍發給士兵一種步槍，拆卸和清理都需要特殊的螺絲起子。同一時期，罐頭食品在軍中越來越普遍。瑞士的將領於是決定發給每個士兵一把制式刀具，既能當螺絲起子，也能開罐頭。

第 3 段這對當時苦於和比較便宜的德國進口刀競爭的瑞士刀具業者來說，簡直是救命稻草。1884 年，瑞士刀具製造商 Victorinox 的負責人 Carl Elsener 緊緊抓住這個機會，設計出一款軍方很喜歡的士兵刀。這是一把簡單的刀，有一片大刀刃、一個開罐器和一支螺絲起子。

第 4 段士兵刀發放幾年後，Schweizer Offizier Messer，也就是「瑞士軍官刀」上市了。有意思的是，軍方從來沒有發軍官刀給軍人。瑞士軍方的採購人員認為，這款多了一支開葡萄酒用的軟木塞開瓶器的新刀，並不是「生存必需品」，所以軍官得自己掏錢買。但後來正是這種特殊的多功能設計，讓這把刀成了全球品牌。第二次世界大戰後，大批美軍駐紮在歐洲，他們可以在軍事基地的商店買到這種刀，於是大量購買。不過 Schweizer Offizier Messer 對美軍來說似乎太難唸了，他們乾脆叫它 Swiss army knife，現在全世界都用這個名字稱呼它。
