110 學測閱讀測驗：摩根奇幻樂園

Morgan's Wonderland
學測 110年第 53–56 題4 題

文章

第 1 段Morgan’s Wonderland, located in San Antonio, Texas, is a theme park geared mainly toward mentally or physically disabled children. The park was built by Gordon Hartman, a former real estate developer. The creation of the park was inspired by his daughter, Morgan, who suffers from severe cognitive delay and physical challenges.

第 2 段The world’s first ultra-accessible family fun park, Morgan’s Wonderland opened in the spring of 2010. Admission for guests with special needs is free, and fees for the general public are set at a much discounted price so that people of all ages and abilities can come together and play in a fun and safe environment.

第 3 段Completely wheelchair-accessible, the park features 25 acres of attractions including rides, playgrounds, a catch-and-release fishing lake, and picnic areas throughout the park. The rides are custom-designed to accommodate wheelchair riders so that every family member can enjoy the fun. The adapted rides include the Off-Road Adventure, where guests can test their driving skills in sporty vehicles. Moreover, each visitor is offered the option to wear a GPS Adventure Band, which allows them to keep track of each other while in the park. The band also enables them to take part in electronic activities. For example, when the riders scan the band at the Off-Road Adventure, a photo will be taken and sent to their email.

第 4 段In June 2017, Morgan’s Wonderland celebrated the opening of Morgan’s Inspiration Island. The new expansion is composed of five themed splash pads and a River Boat Adventure Ride. The wheelchair guests can transfer out of their chairs into unique, waterproof chairs and enjoy the splash park without risking damage to their personal wheelchairs.

第 5 段“Morgan taught me that there’s more to life in many ways than what I saw before,” said Hartman. “The blessing that Morgan has brought is beyond anything that I ever could have imagined and could explain.”

第 53 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 1 段講建園的緣由，第 2–4 段講樂園的特色和新開的園區，所以全文介紹一座特殊主題樂園的設立和特色。(A) 新園區只講第 4 段，(C) GPS 手環只講第 3 段，都過於狹隘；(D) 身心障礙者的需求範圍比這座樂園大得多，文章也沒有專門討論，太過廣泛。
錯誤選項：(A) 過於狹隘（只講第 4 段）；(C) 過於狹隘（只講第 3 段）；(D) 太過廣泛
第 54 題 詞義題 Vocabulary in context

第 2 段so that people of all ages and abilities can come together and play in a fun and safe environment

第 3 段Completely wheelchair-accessible, the park features 25 acres of attractions

第 3 段The rides are custom-designed to accommodate wheelchair riders so that every family member can enjoy the fun.

原文 → 正確選項
every family member can enjoy the fun → easy for all visitors to use the facilities
accessible 是「方便使用、可以到達」，ultra- 是「極度」；後文說全區輪椅都能通行、設施特別設計讓每一位家人都能玩，所以是所有遊客都能輕鬆使用設施。(B) 照抄 Admission，但免費的只有特殊需求的遊客；(C) 照抄 wheelchair，原文說設施能讓輪椅搭乘，沒說每項設施都提供輪椅；(D) 用的是 accessible「交通方便、容易到達」的意思，可是後文講的都是園區裡的設施人人能用，文章沒有談樂園的地點方不方便。

第 56 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 3 段Moreover, each visitor is offered the option to wear a GPS Adventure Band, which allows them to keep track of each other while in the park.

第 3 段The band also enables them to take part in electronic activities.

原文 → 正確選項
enables them to take part in electronic activities → enjoy the convenience of the electronic devices
GPS 手環能掌握彼此的位置，也能參加電子互動活動，就是讓遊客享受電子裝置的便利。(A) 照抄 waterproof chairs，但防水椅只用在戲水區；(B) 照抄 25 acres，那是整座樂園的面積，不是靈感島；(C) 文章只說建園的靈感來自 Morgan，沒說這是 Morgan 想要達成的。

逐段中譯

第 1 段摩根奇幻樂園位在德州聖安東尼奧，是一座主要為身心障礙兒童設計的主題樂園。樂園由前房地產開發商 Gordon Hartman 興建，建園的靈感來自他的女兒 Morgan，Morgan 有嚴重的認知發展遲緩和肢體障礙。

第 2 段摩根奇幻樂園是全世界第一座「超無障礙」的家庭遊樂園，2010 年春天開幕。有特殊需求的遊客免費入園，一般民眾的門票也大幅打折，讓各種年齡、各種能力的人都能聚在一起，在有趣又安全的環境裡玩樂。

第 3 段樂園全區都能讓輪椅通行，25 英畝的園區裡有遊樂設施、遊戲場、釣了再放生的釣魚湖，四處還有野餐區。遊樂設施都經過特別設計，坐輪椅也能搭乘，讓每一位家人都能同樂。這些經過改造的設施包括「越野冒險」，遊客可以開著跑車造型的車子測試駕駛技術。此外，每位遊客都可以選擇戴上 GPS 冒險手環，在園區裡隨時掌握彼此的位置。手環也能讓遊客參加電子互動活動，例如在越野冒險掃描手環，就會拍下照片，寄到遊客的電子信箱。

第 4 段2017 年 6 月，摩根奇幻樂園慶祝「摩根靈感島」開幕。這個新擴建的園區有五座主題戲水區和一項河船冒險設施。坐輪椅的遊客可以換坐特製的防水椅，盡情享受戲水樂園，不必擔心弄壞自己的輪椅。

第 5 段Hartman 說：「Morgan 讓我明白，人生在許多方面，比我以前所看到的還要豐富。」「Morgan 帶給我的福氣，遠遠超過我所能想像，也超過我能解釋的。」

字詞搭配 配對與接法

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。

  • suffer from + N患有……第 1 段原文：suffers from severe cognitive delay
  • special needs特殊需求第 2 段原文：guests with special needs
  • the general public一般大眾第 2 段
  • at a discounted price以折扣價第 2 段原文：set at a much discounted price
  • be custom-designed to + V專門設計來……第 3 段原文：The rides are custom-designed to accommodate wheelchair riders
  • keep track of + N隨時知道……的位置、動態第 3 段原文：keep track of each other
  • take part in + N參加……第 3 段原文：take part in electronic activities
  • enable + sb + to + V讓某人能夠……第 3 段原文：enables them to take part in
  • be composed of + N由……組成第 4 段原文：is composed of five themed splash pads
  • risk + N／V-ing冒……的風險第 4 段原文：without risking damage to their personal wheelchairs
  • there is more to life than + N人生不只是……第 5 段原文：there’s more to life in many ways than what I saw before
