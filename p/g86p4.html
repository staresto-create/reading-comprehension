86 學測閱讀測驗：俄羅斯父子澎湖獲救記

Russian Father and Son Rescued in Penghu
學測 86年第 52–55 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Penghu, Nov.24—Two Russians, a father and a son, were rescued Saturday near the Penghu Islands off southwestern Taiwan after their sailboat broke down and began drifting.

第 2 段Medvedev Vladimir, 43, and his son, Maxsine, 14, sailed from an unnamed eastern Russian seaport on August 18, to begin an ambitious around-the-world cruise.

第 3 段Their voyage, down the coastline of northeastern mainland China, through the East China Sea, then down the Taiwan Strait and into the South China Sea, had been smooth until four days ago when the mainsail of their twin-mast sailboat “Kawasaki” was smashed by strong winds on the high seas.

第 4 段The father-and-son team started the inboard engine. However, after less than two days, the engine broke down.

第 5 段Near the “black ditch” of Penghu, battered by winds of up to 70 knots, the rear sail was torn into shreds. The rudder also stopped responding.

第 6 段Surprised by the father and son’s “pale faces” and their battered vessel, the Penghu police and fishers in Paisha rescued them. Speaking only limited English, the Vladimirs spent five hours trying to explain their presence.

第 7 段It is not immediately known whether the “Kawasaki” can be repaired on the fishing island of Penghu, or when the Vladimirs will be able to set off again and continue their around-the-world voyage.

逐段中譯

第 1 段澎湖 11 月 24 日電：兩名俄羅斯人，一對父子，帆船故障、開始漂流以後，週六在台灣西南外海的澎湖群島附近獲救。

第 2 段43 歲的 Medvedev Vladimir 和 14 歲的兒子 Maxsine，8 月 18 日從俄羅斯東部一個未透露名稱的港口出航，展開一趟雄心勃勃的環球航行。

第 3 段他們的航程先沿著中國大陸東北部的海岸線南下，穿過東海，再南下台灣海峽，進入南海。一路都很順利，直到四天前，強風在公海上打碎了他們那艘雙桅帆船「Kawasaki」的主帆。

第 4 段這對父子發動了船上的引擎。可是不到兩天，引擎就故障了。

第 5 段在澎湖的「黑水溝」附近，風速高達 70 節的強風猛烈吹打著帆船，後帆撕成了碎片，舵也失靈了。

第 6 段澎湖的警察和白沙的漁民看到這對父子「臉色蒼白」、船隻破損不堪，都嚇了一跳，於是救起了他們。Vladimir 父子只會說一點英文，花了五個小時，才解釋清楚他們為什麼會出現在這裡。

第 7 段「Kawasaki」能不能在澎湖這個漁業島嶼上修好，Vladimir 父子什麼時候能再出發、繼續環球航行，目前都還不知道。
