87 學測閱讀測驗：機器人擔任畢業典禮演講者

Robot as a Commencement Speaker
學測 87年第 41–43 題3 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段One of the most unusual graduation speakers of the 1983 commencement season was not a person at all. The speaker was Robot Redford, a robot who delivered the commencement address at a community college in suburban Maryland.

第 2 段The speaker, carefully programmed, arrived on the stage under its own power and proceeded to tell the audience of 658 graduates, their families, and friends that they would have to learn to work with robots and technology to solve society’s problems. The robot described itself as an extension of a person to help humans increase the workload. Although this particular robot can be programmed to speak, the voice heard wasn’t that of the robot, because it was feared that the robot’s voice was not loud enough to carry throughout the hall. An amplified voice of a human was used instead, while the listeners watched the robot.

第 3 段Robot Redford was followed by a person, William Bakaleinikoff, who spoke about the need for cooperation between people and technology. Mr. Bakaleinikoff’s experience with his topic included the fact that he had created Robot Redford and had provided the voice for the robot’s words to the graduates.

第 4 段There was quite a bit of controversy over the choice of a robot as a commencement speaker. Some students thought it was insensitive and degrading to use a robot. Others thought the idea was clever and innovative.

第 5 段“I’ll never forget this speech,” said one graduate. “You forget a political leader, but you won’t forget a robot.”

題目

41.Robot Redford’s speech was discussed in this article mainly because ______.

  1. (A)it was political
  2. (B)it was psychological
  3. (C)it was controversial
  4. (D)it was insensitive

42.Which of the following statements is NOT true?

  1. (A)Robot Redford was a talking machine.
  2. (B)Robot Redford walked to the stage by itself.
  3. (C)Robot Redford made a graduation speech in 1983.
  4. (D)Robot Redford graduated from a college in Maryland.

43.Which of the following statements can be supported by the passage?

  1. (A)A robot was designed to let humans decrease their productivity.
  2. (B)Robot Redford was the brainchild of William Bakaleinikoff.
  3. (C)Everybody in the commencement was pleased with the arrangement.
  4. (D)Robot Redford’s speech was too difficult to understand.

第 41 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 1 段說機器人當畢業演講者非常不尋常，第 4 段說選機器人當演講者引起不少爭議（controversy），有人覺得不妥，有人覺得有創意，第 5 段再用畢業生的話收尾。這場演講會寫成報導，主要是因為它引起爭議，所以是 (C)。(D) 照抄 insensitive，那只是第 4 段部分學生的看法，過於狹隘；(A) 照抄 political，第 5 段的畢業生只是拿政治領袖和機器人比較，演講內容和政治無關，偏離主題；(B) 文章沒有談到心理學，偏離主題。

第 42 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段a robot who delivered the commencement address at a community college in suburban Maryland.

第 1 段說 Robot Redford 是在馬里蘭州一所社區大學的畢業典禮上致詞（delivered the commencement address），它是演講者，不是畢業生，所以和原文不符的是 (D)。其他三個選項都和原文相符：第 2 段說這個機器人可以設定程式說話，是 (A)；它靠自己的動力走上講台（under its own power），是 (B)；第 1 段說這是 1983 年的畢業典禮季，是 (C)。
第 43 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 3 段he had created Robot Redford

原文 → 正確選項
created → brainchild
第 3 段說 Bakaleinikoff 製造了 Robot Redford（he had created Robot Redford），選項的 brainchild 指的是某人構想出來的心血結晶，所以是 (B)。(A) 第 2 段說機器人能幫助人類完成更多工作，不是降低生產力；(C) 第 4 段說這個安排引起爭議，有學生覺得不妥，不是每個人都滿意；(D) 原文沒有說演講太難懂。

逐段中譯

第 1 段1983 年畢業典禮季最特別的畢業演講者之一，根本不是人。這位演講者是 Robot Redford，一個機器人，它在馬里蘭州郊區一所社區大學的畢業典禮上致詞。

第 2 段這位演講者經過精心設定程式，靠自己的動力走上講台，接著告訴在場的 658 位畢業生和他們的家人、朋友，他們必須學會和機器人、科技合作，來解決社會的問題。機器人把自己形容成人的延伸，能幫助人類完成更多工作。雖然這個機器人可以設定程式說話，但大家聽到的並不是機器人的聲音，因為有人擔心機器人的聲音不夠大，沒辦法傳遍整個禮堂。所以改用擴音的人聲，觀眾則看著機器人。

第 3 段接在 Robot Redford 後面演講的是一個人：William Bakaleinikoff，他談到人和科技需要合作。Bakaleinikoff 先生對這個題目的經驗包括：Robot Redford 就是他製造的，機器人對畢業生說的話，也是他配的音。

第 4 段選機器人當畢業典禮的演講者，引起不少爭議。有些學生認為用機器人既不體貼，又有貶低人的意味。也有人認為這個主意很聰明、很有創意。

第 5 段「我永遠不會忘記這場演講。」一位畢業生說：「政治領袖你會忘記，但機器人你不會忘記。」
