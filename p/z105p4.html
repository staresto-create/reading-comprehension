What Makes Screams Unique and How to Use It
指考 105年第 48–51 題4 題

文章

第 1 段Screaming is one of the primal responses humans share with other animals. Conventional thinking suggests that what sets a scream apart from other sounds is its loudness or high pitch. However, many sounds that are loud and high-pitched do not raise goose bumps like screams can. To find out what makes human screams unique, neuroscientist Luc Arnal and his team examined a bank of sounds containing sentences spoken or screamed by 19 adults. The result shows screams and screamed sentences had a quality called “roughness,” which refers to how fast a sound changes in loudness. While normal speech sounds only have slight differences in loudness—between 4 and 5 Hz, screams can switch very fast, varying between 30 and 150 Hz, thus perceived as being rough and unpleasant.

第 2 段Arnal’s team asked 20 subjects to judge screams as neutral or fearful, and found that the scariest almost always corresponded with roughness. The team then studied how the human brain responds to roughness using fMRI brain scanners. As expected, after hearing a scream, activity increased in the brain’s auditory centers where sound coming into the ears is processed. But the scans also lit up in the amygdala, the brain’s fear center.

第 3 段The amygdala is the area that regulates our emotional and physiological response to danger. When a threat is detected, our adrenaline rises, and our body prepares to react to danger. The study discovered that screams have a similar influence on our body. It also found that roughness isn’t heard when we speak naturally, regardless of the language we use, but it is prevalent in artificial sounds. The most aggravating alarm clocks, car horns, and fire alarms possess high degrees of roughness.

第 4 段One potential application for this research might be to add roughness to alarm sounds to make them more effective, the same way a bad smell is added to natural gas to make it easily detectable. Warning sounds could also be added to electric cars, which are particularly silent, so they can be efficiently detected by pedestrians.

逐段中譯

第 1 段尖叫是人類和其他動物共有的原始反應之一。一般的想法認為，尖叫和其他聲音不同的地方，在於音量大或音調高。然而，許多又大聲、音調又高的聲音，並不像尖叫那樣讓人起雞皮疙瘩。為了找出人類的尖叫特別在哪裡，神經科學家 Luc Arnal 和他的團隊研究了一個聲音資料庫，裡面是 19 位成人說出或尖叫出來的句子。結果顯示，尖叫和尖叫出來的句子，都有一種叫做「粗糙度」（roughness）的特性，指的是聲音的音量變化得多快。一般說話的聲音，音量只有些微的差異，在 4 到 5 赫茲之間；尖叫的音量卻可以變化得非常快，在 30 到 150 赫茲之間，所以聽起來粗糙、刺耳。

第 2 段Arnal 的團隊請 20 位受試者判斷尖叫聲是平常的還是恐懼的，發現最可怕的尖叫，幾乎都是最粗糙的。團隊接著用功能性磁振造影（fMRI）掃描儀，研究人腦對粗糙度有什麼反應。和預期的一樣，聽到尖叫以後，大腦處理耳朵接收到的聲音的聽覺中樞，活動增加了。不過，掃描影像上，大腦的恐懼中心杏仁核也亮了起來。

第 3 段杏仁核是調節我們對危險的情緒和生理反應的區域。察覺到威脅的時候，我們的腎上腺素上升，身體準備對危險做出反應。研究發現，尖叫對身體也有類似的影響。研究也發現，不管我們說的是哪一種語言，自然說話的時候都聽不到粗糙度，但人工的聲音卻常常有粗糙度。最讓人煩躁的鬧鐘、汽車喇叭和火災警報器，粗糙度都很高。

第 4 段這項研究可能的一個應用，是在警報聲裡加入粗糙度，讓警報更有效，就像在天然氣裡加入難聞的氣味，讓人容易察覺一樣。也可以替特別安靜的電動車加上警示音，讓行人能有效地察覺。

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
  • share + sth + with + N和……共有……第 1 段原文：one of the primal responses humans share with other animals
  • set A apart from B讓 A 和 B 不一樣第 1 段原文：what sets a scream apart from other sounds
  • raise goose bumps讓人起雞皮疙瘩第 1 段原文：do not raise goose bumps
  • refer to + N指的是……第 1 段原文：which refers to how fast a sound changes
  • vary between A and B在 A 和 B 之間變動第 1 段原文：varying between 30 and 150 Hz
  • perceive A as B覺得 A 是 B第 1 段原文：perceived as being rough and unpleasant
  • judge A as B把 A 判斷為 B第 2 段原文：judge screams as neutral or fearful
  • correspond with + N和……相符第 2 段原文：corresponded with roughness
  • respond to + N對……做出反應第 2 段原文：how the human brain responds to roughness
  • have an influence on + N對……有影響第 3 段原文：screams have a similar influence on our body
  • regardless of + N不論……第 3 段原文：regardless of the language we use
  • add A to B把 A 加進 B第 4 段原文：to add roughness to alarm sounds
  • the same way + 子句就像……一樣第 4 段原文：the same way a bad smell is added to natural gas
