85 學測閱讀測驗：外國人入境台灣的簽證規定

Visa Regulations for Foreigners Entering Taiwan
學測 85年第 44–46 題3 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Generally speaking, all foreigners coming to Taiwan should apply for entry visas through the embassies or consulates of the Republic of China, or agencies authorized by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

第 2 段As of May 1, 1995, all nationals of the following 15 countries are exempted from securing visas for entry into the Republic of China: U.S.A., Japan, Canada, Belgium, Luxembourg, Sweden, Holland, New Zealand, Australia, Austria, Germany, Britain, France, Spain, and Portugal. The maximum duration of stay is 14 days. Requirements for a 14-day non-visa entry are: passport good for at least six months, confirmed onward ticket within 14 days, no violation of law recorded.

第 3 段If it is necessary to stay more than 14 days and less than 30 days, nationals of the specific countries holding a 14-day non-visa entry should in the meantime apply for a landing-visa with the authorized office in the CKS airport from the Bureau of Consular Affairs of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. The landing-visa fee is NT$1,500.

題目

44.When an American lady wants to enter Taiwan for a one-week sight-seeing trip, one of the requirements is

  1. (A)to obtain a landing-visa at the CKS airport.
  2. (B)to apply for a visa before leaving the U.S.A.
  3. (C)to make sure her passport was issued before May 1, 1995.
  4. (D)to make sure her passport is valid for more than six months on the date of arrival.

45.If a British businessman wants to visit some trade partners in Taiwan for about three weeks, what should he do?

  1. (A)He should obtain a visa for entry into the Republic of China before getting on board the flight to Taiwan
  2. (B)He should travel with a friend who is from any of the other 14 countries mentioned.
  3. (C)He must obtain a landing-visa at the CKS airport.
  4. (D)He just needs to apply for a non-visa entry when he arrives at the CKS airport.

46.Which of the following is needed for a landing-visa?

  1. (A)A passport that is valid for more than six months.
  2. (B)A 14-day non-visa entry.
  3. (C)An application fee of NT$1,500.
  4. (D)All of the above.

第 44 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 2 段passport good for at least six months

原文 → 正確選項
good for at least six months → valid for more than six months
美國在第 2 段的 15 國名單裡，停留一星期不到 14 天，可以免簽證入境，條件之一是護照效期至少六個月（good for at least six months），所以是 (D)。(A) 照抄第 3 段的 landing-visa，停留超過 14 天才要申請落地簽證；(C) 照抄 May 1, 1995，那是免簽證開始實施的日期，和護照什麼時候核發無關；(B) 美國國民免簽證，不必出發前先辦簽證。
第 45 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 3 段If it is necessary to stay more than 14 days and less than 30 days

第 3 段should in the meantime apply for a landing-visa with the authorized office in the CKS airport

英國在第 2 段的免簽證名單裡，但是三週大約 21 天，超過 14 天、不到 30 天。第 3 段說這種情況要向設在中正機場的辦事處申請落地簽證，所以是 (C)。(D) 照抄 non-visa entry，免簽證入境最多只能停留 14 天；(A) 照抄 entry into the Republic of China，第 2 段說的是英國國民入境中華民國免辦簽證，不必出發前先辦；(B) 和其他國家的朋友同行，原文沒有提到。
第 46 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 2 段passport good for at least six months

第 3 段nationals of the specific countries holding a 14-day non-visa entry

第 3 段The landing-visa fee is NT$1,500.

第 3 段說申請落地簽證的，是持 14 天免簽證入境的國民，所以要有 (B)；免簽證入境本來就要護照效期至少六個月，所以 (A) 也是條件；落地簽證的費用是新台幣 1,500 元，所以還要繳 (C)。三項都需要，所以是 (D)。

逐段中譯

第 1 段一般來說，所有要來台灣的外國人，都應該透過中華民國的大使館、領事館，或外交部授權的機構申請入境簽證。

第 2 段自 1995 年 5 月 1 日起，下列 15 國的國民入境中華民國免辦簽證：美國、日本、加拿大、比利時、盧森堡、瑞典、荷蘭、紐西蘭、澳洲、奧地利、德國、英國、法國、西班牙和葡萄牙。停留期限最長 14 天。14 天免簽證入境的條件是：護照效期至少六個月、持有 14 天內離境的確認機票，而且沒有違法紀錄。

第 3 段如果需要停留超過 14 天、不到 30 天，持 14 天免簽證入境的這些國家國民，應該在停留期間向外交部領事事務局設在中正機場的授權辦事處申請落地簽證。落地簽證的費用是新台幣 1,500 元。
