The Impact of Video Games on Decision-Making Skills
學測 99課綱（102年施測）第 53–56 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Video games have been blamed for many of the ills of the world. Parents complain that violent games make their children antisocial, violent, shallow, and overweight. Educators worry that video games are breeding a generation that cannot sustain their concentration.

第 2 段However, a recent study from cognitive science suggests that there may be a benefit in playing some action games. The research, published in Current Biology, reports that video gamers make faster and more accurate decisions. What was particularly interesting was that the faster reaction times could also be acquired by non-video gamers with 50 hours of training over a few weeks. But for the benefits to increase, they had to play a particular sort of game. Only those who played fast-moving action video games such as “Call of Duty 2” and “Unreal Tournament” saw an improvement in their decision-making skills. Rather ironically, players of “The Sims 2,” a game where one has to decide how to organize an entire simulated world, did not show the same benefit.

第 3 段The precise reason for this effect is still unknown. What is known, however, is that people make decisions based on probabilities, which are constantly being calculated and refined in their heads. The brain collects small pieces of information, eventually gathering enough to make an accurate decision. When driving a car, for example, these many probabilities will be compared to make decisions such as whether or not to brake. The more efficient a person is at collecting visual and auditory information, the faster he or she will become ready to make a decision.

  1. (A)Making decisions slowly.
  2. (B)Being unfriendly to people.
  3. (C)Becoming heavy and unfit.
  4. (D)Having difficulty concentrating.

  1. (A)To promote some new video games for children.
  2. (B)To discourage children from playing video games.
  3. (C)To distinguish healthful from harmful video games.
  4. (D)To report new findings on the effect of playing video games.

  1. (A)It takes years of practice for one to improve decision-making skills.
  2. (B)Players of all video games show the same effect in decision making.
  3. (C)Players of fast-moving video games tend to make less accurate decisions.

  4. (D)Decision making is based on information gathered and compared in the brain.

每一題的依據句

第 53 題 細節題（未提及） Supporting details

第 1 段Parents complain that violent games make their children antisocial, violent, shallow, and overweight.

第 1 段Educators worry that video games are breeding a generation that cannot sustain their concentration.

第 1 段提到的問題，選項換了說法：antisocial 是 (B) 對人不友善；overweight 是 (C) 過胖、身體不健康；cannot sustain their concentration 是 (D) 難以專心。文章沒說玩電玩會讓孩子做決定變慢，第 2 段反而說玩電玩的人做決定比較快，所以選 (A)。
第 54 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 1 段先說家長和教育工作者對電玩的批評；第 2 段用 However 轉折，報導一項新研究：玩某些動作遊戲能讓人做決定更快、更準確；第 3 段說明人怎麼做決定。全文在報導玩電玩有什麼影響的新發現，所以是 (D)。(C) 第 2 段比較了動作遊戲和《The Sims 2》，但只是研究結果的一部分，過於狹隘；(A) 文章沒有推銷新遊戲，(B) 第 1 段的批評只是研究的背景，文章並沒有勸孩子別玩電玩，兩個選項都偏離主題。
錯誤選項：(A) 偏離主題；(B) 偏離主題；(C) 過於狹隘（只講第 2 段）
第 55 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 2 段However, a recent study from cognitive science suggests that there may be a benefit in playing some action games.

第 2 段Only those who played fast-moving action video games such as “Call of Duty 2” and “Unreal Tournament” saw an improvement in their decision-making skills.

原文 → 正確選項
improvement in their decision-making skills → improve our decision-making skills
第 2 段說玩某些動作遊戲可能有好處，只有玩快節奏動作遊戲的人決策能力有進步（saw an improvement in their decision-making skills），選項換成 playing some video games may help us improve our decision-making skills。(C) 照抄 fast-moving，玩快節奏動作遊戲的人做決定更準確，選項說的正好相反；(B) 照抄 same，原文說玩《The Sims 2》的人沒有得到同樣的好處，可見不是所有遊戲都有這個效果；(A) 不玩電玩的人在幾週內接受 50 個小時的訓練就能看到效果，不需要好幾年。
第 56 題 主旨題 Main idea

第 3 段What is known, however, is that people make decisions based on probabilities, which are constantly being calculated and refined in their heads.

第 3 段The brain collects small pieces of information, eventually gathering enough to make an accurate decision.

原文 → 正確選項
collects small pieces of information → information gathered
第 3 段的重點在 however 之後：人根據腦中不斷計算的機率做決定，大腦蒐集零碎的資訊（collects small pieces of information），累積夠了就做出決定，開車要不要煞車是例子。選項換成 information gathered and compared in the brain，所以是 (D)。(A) 照抄 unknown，還不清楚的是電玩為什麼有這種效果，不是做決定的過程；(B) 照抄 driving a car，開車只是舉的例子，不是整段的主旨；(C) 原文說原因還不清楚，不能說已經證實電玩對決策有正面影響。

逐段中譯

第 1 段許多人把世上的種種問題怪到電玩遊戲頭上。家長抱怨暴力遊戲讓孩子變得反社會、暴力、膚淺，而且過胖。教育工作者擔心電玩遊戲正在養出無法持續專注的一代。

第 2 段然而，認知科學最近的一項研究指出，玩某些動作遊戲可能有好處。這項研究發表在《Current Biology》期刊，指出電玩玩家做決定比較快、也比較準確。特別有意思的是，不玩電玩的人在幾週內接受 50 個小時的訓練，也能練出比較快的反應時間。不過，要讓好處增加，他們必須玩特定類型的遊戲。只有玩快節奏動作遊戲的人，例如《Call of Duty 2》和《Unreal Tournament》，決策能力才有進步。頗為諷刺的是，《The Sims 2》要玩家決定怎麼安排整個模擬世界，玩這款遊戲的人卻沒有得到同樣的好處。

第 3 段這種效果的確切原因目前還不清楚。不過，已經知道的是，人做決定是根據機率，這些機率在腦中不斷地計算、修正。大腦會蒐集零碎的資訊，最後累積到足夠的資訊，做出準確的決定。例如開車時，大腦會比較這許多機率，決定要不要踩煞車。一個人蒐集視覺和聽覺資訊的效率越高，就能越快準備好做出決定。

綱要

  2. (However) 認知科學研究指出，動作電玩玩家決策更快、更準確，非玩家經過訓練也能獲益。
  3. (But) 只有快節奏的動作遊戲才有效，(Rather ironically) 模擬經營遊戲反而沒有效果。

  5. (for example) 以開車煞車為例，蒐集視聽資訊越有效率，決定就越快。
