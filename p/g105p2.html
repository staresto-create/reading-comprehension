105 學測閱讀測驗：小發明家的故事

The Story of a Young Inventor
學測 105年第 45–48 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Like many other five-year-olds, Jeanie Low of Houston, Texas, would use a stool to help her reach the bathroom sink. However, the plastic step-stool she had at home was unstable and cluttered up the small bathroom shared by her whole family. After learning of an invention contest held by her school that year, Jeanie resolved to enter the contest by creating a stool that would be a permanent fixture in the bathroom, and yet could be kept out of the way when not in use.

第 2 段Jeanie decided to make a stool attached to the bathroom cabinet door under the sink. She cut a plank of wood into two pieces, each about two feet wide and one foot long. Using metal hinges, Jeanie attached one piece of the wood to the front of the cabinet door, and the second piece to the first. The first piece was set just high enough so that when it swung out horizontally from the cabinet door, the second piece would swing down from the first, just touching the ground, and so serving as a support for the first piece of the wood. This created a convenient, sturdy platform for any person too short to reach the sink. When not in use, the hinges allowed the two pieces of wood to fold back up tightly against the cabinet, where they were held in place by magnets. Jeanie called her invention the “Kiddie Stool.”

第 3 段Jeanie’s Kiddie Stool won first place in her school’s contest. Two years later, it was awarded first prize again at Houston’s first annual Invention Fair. As a result, Jeanie was invited to make a number of public appearances with her Kiddie Stool, and was featured on local TV as well as in newspapers. Many people found the story of the Kiddie Stool inspiring because it showed that with imagination, anyone can be an inventor.

題目

45.Why did Jeanie Low invent the Kiddie Stool, according to the passage?

  1. (A)Many other five-year-olds had problems reaching the bathroom sink.
  2. (B)She did not think that plastic stools were tall enough for her.
  3. (C)The stool in her bathroom was not firm and often got in the way.
  4. (D)She was invited to enter an invention contest held by her school.

46.Which of the following statements is true about how the Kiddie Stool works?

  1. (A)The Kiddie Stool will swing out only when the cabinet door opens.
  2. (B)It uses hinges and magnets to keep the wooden pieces in place.
  3. (C)It swings from left to right to be attached to the cabinet door.
  4. (D)The platform is supported by two pieces of metal.

47.What are the characteristics of Jeanie’s Kiddie Stool?

  1. (A)Permanent and foldable.
  2. (B)Fragile and eye-catching.
  3. (C)Conventional and touching.
  4. (D)Convenient and recyclable.

48.Which of the following sayings best captures the spirit of Jeanie Low’s story?

  1. (A)Failure is the mother of success.
  2. (B)There’s nothing new under the sun.
  3. (C)Necessity is the mother of invention.
  4. (D)Genius is 1% inspiration and 99% perspiration.

第 45 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段However, the plastic step-stool she had at home was unstable and cluttered up the small bathroom shared by her whole family.

原文 → 正確選項
unstable → not firm
cluttered up → got in the way
第 1 段說 Jeanie 家裡的塑膠踏凳不穩（unstable），又讓小浴室很擠（cluttered up），選項換成 not firm 和 often got in the way。(D) 照抄 invention contest held by her school，學校的發明比賽是她動手做的契機，她要解決的是凳子不穩又占空間的問題，而且原文說她決定參加，不是受邀參加；(A) 照抄 five-year-olds，原文只說她和許多五歲小孩一樣要踩凳子，沒說其他小孩有困難；(B) 照抄 plastic，原文沒說塑膠凳不夠高。
第 46 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 2 段Using metal hinges, Jeanie attached one piece of the wood to the front of the cabinet door, and the second piece to the first.

第 2 段When not in use, the hinges allowed the two pieces of wood to fold back up tightly against the cabinet, where they were held in place by magnets.

原文 → 正確選項
held in place by magnets → magnets to keep the wooden pieces in place
第 2 段說 Jeanie 用金屬鉸鏈把兩片木板接起來，不用時鉸鏈讓木板摺回櫃門上，再用磁鐵固定（held in place by magnets），選項換成 uses hinges and magnets to keep the wooden pieces in place。(A) 照抄 cabinet door，凳子是從櫃門上往外展開，原文沒說要先打開櫃門；(C) 照抄 swing，原文說第一片木板水平展開、第二片往下擺，不是左右擺動；(D) 照抄 metal，金屬做的是鉸鏈，支撐平台的是兩片木板。
第 47 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段Jeanie resolved to enter the contest by creating a stool that would be a permanent fixture in the bathroom, and yet could be kept out of the way when not in use.

第 2 段When not in use, the hinges allowed the two pieces of wood to fold back up tightly against the cabinet, where they were held in place by magnets.

原文 → 正確選項
permanent → Permanent
fold back → foldable
第 1 段說 Jeanie 要做一張固定在浴室裡（permanent fixture）、不用時又能收起來的凳子；第 2 段說不用時兩片木板能摺回櫃門上（fold back），選項換成 Permanent and foldable。(D) 照抄第 2 段的 convenient，方便說對了一半，但 recyclable 可回收文章沒提；(B) fragile 脆弱，和原文說的 sturdy 穩固正好相反；(C) touching 感人說的是第 3 段大家對這個故事的感受，conventional 傳統的也和新發明接不上。
第 48 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 1 段說 Jeanie 因為家裡的凳子不穩又占空間，才想做一張更好的凳子；第 2 段說她怎麼做出兒童凳；第 3 段說兒童凳得獎、激勵許多人。整個故事是生活上有需要，才催生了發明，最貼切的是 (C) 需要為發明之母。(A) 失敗為成功之母，Jeanie 的發明一路得獎，文章沒有寫到失敗；(B) 太陽底下沒有新鮮事，和她做出新發明的故事不合；(D) 天才是 1% 的靈感加 99% 的汗水，強調的是苦幹，文章結尾強調的卻是想像力；三個選項都偏離主題。
錯誤選項：(A) 偏離主題；(B) 偏離主題；(D) 偏離主題

逐段中譯

第 1 段德州休士頓的 Jeanie Low 和許多五歲小孩一樣，要踩凳子才搆得到浴室的洗手台。不過，她家裡那張塑膠踏凳不穩，又讓全家人共用的小浴室顯得很擠。得知學校那一年要舉辦發明比賽之後，Jeanie 決定參加，做一張固定在浴室裡、不用時又能收起來不擋路的凳子。

第 2 段Jeanie 決定做一張裝在洗手台下方櫃門上的凳子。她把一塊木板鋸成兩片，每片大約兩英尺寬、一英尺長。她用金屬鉸鏈把第一片木板裝在櫃門正面，再把第二片裝在第一片上。第一片的位置裝得剛好夠高，從櫃門往外水平展開時，第二片會從第一片往下擺，剛好碰到地面，成為第一片木板的支撐。這樣就成了一個方便又穩固的平台，任何個子太矮、搆不到洗手台的人都能用。不用的時候，鉸鏈讓兩片木板能緊緊摺回櫃門上，再用磁鐵固定。Jeanie 把她的發明取名為「Kiddie Stool」（兒童凳）。

第 3 段Jeanie 的兒童凳在學校的比賽中得到第一名。兩年後，兒童凳在休士頓第一屆年度發明展又得到首獎。因此，Jeanie 受邀帶著兒童凳多次公開亮相，也登上地方電視和報紙。許多人覺得兒童凳的故事很激勵人心，因為它說明了只要有想像力，人人都可以當發明家。
