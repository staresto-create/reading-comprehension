Capoeira
學測 106年第 45–48 題4 題

文章

第 1 段Capoeira is a martial art that combines elements of fight, acrobatics, drumming, singing, dance, and rituals. It involves a variety of techniques that make use of the hands, feet, legs, arms, and head. Although Capoeira appears dancelike, many of its basic techniques are similar to those in other martial arts.

第 2 段Capoeira was created nearly 500 years ago in Brazil by African slaves. It is believed that the martial art was connected with tribal fighting in Africa, in which people fought body to body, without weapons, in order to acquire a bride or desired woman. In the sixteenth century, when the Africans were taken from their homes to Brazil against their will and kept in slavery, Capoeira began to take form among the community of slaves for self-defense. But it soon became a strong weapon in the life-or-death struggle against their oppressors. When the slave owners realized the power of Capoeira, they began to punish those who practiced it. Capoeiristas learned to camouflage the forbidden fights with singing, clapping, and dancing as though it were simply entertainment.

第 3 段At first, Capoeira was considered illegal in Brazil. However, a man known as Mestre Bimba devoted a great deal of time and effort to convincing the Brazilian authorities that Capoeira has great cultural value and should become an official fighting style. He succeeded in his endeavor and transformed the martial art into Brazil’s national sport. He and Mestre Pastinha were the first to open schools, and the Capoeira tree grew, spreading its branches across the world. Nowadays, it is performed in movies and music clips. Capoeira is also believed to have influenced several dancing styles like breaking and hip-hop.

逐段中譯

第 1 段卡波耶拉是一種武術，結合了格鬥、雜技、擊鼓、歌唱、舞蹈和儀式等元素，用到的技巧五花八門，會運用手、腳、腿、手臂和頭部。卡波耶拉雖然看起來像舞蹈，許多基本技巧卻和其他武術相似。

第 2 段卡波耶拉是將近五百年前，巴西的非洲奴隸創造出來的。一般認為這種武術和非洲的部落格鬥有關，人們赤手空拳、徒手搏鬥，為的是娶到新娘或心儀的女子。十六世紀，非洲人身不由己地離開家鄉，到巴西淪為奴隸，卡波耶拉就在奴隸群體中開始成形，用來自衛。但是它很快就成了奴隸對抗壓迫者、攸關生死的強大武器。奴隸主發現卡波耶拉的威力後，開始懲罰練習的人。練卡波耶拉的人於是學會用歌唱、拍手和舞蹈掩飾這些禁止的打鬥，讓它看起來只是娛樂。

第 3 段一開始，卡波耶拉在巴西是違法的。不過，一位叫 Mestre Bimba 的人花了大量時間和心力，說服巴西政府卡波耶拉有很高的文化價值，應該成為正式的格鬥技。他成功了，把這門武術變成巴西的國技。他和 Mestre Pastinha 最早開設學校，卡波耶拉這棵樹從此開枝散葉，傳遍全世界。現在，電影和音樂影片裡都看得到卡波耶拉。一般也認為卡波耶拉影響了霹靂舞、嘻哈舞等好幾種舞蹈風格。

字詞搭配 配對與接法

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
  • a martial art武術第 1 段原文：Capoeira is a martial art
  • make use of + N利用……第 1 段原文：make use of the hands, feet, legs, arms, and head
  • be connected with + N和……有關第 2 段原文：the martial art was connected with tribal fighting
  • against one's will違背某人的意願第 2 段原文：against their will
  • take form成形第 2 段原文：Capoeira began to take form
  • a life-or-death struggle生死存亡的搏鬥第 2 段原文：the life-or-death struggle against their oppressors
  • as though + 子句好像……一樣第 2 段原文：as though it were simply entertainment
  • devote time and effort to + V-ing投入時間心力去……第 3 段原文：devoted a great deal of time and effort to convincing the Brazilian authorities
  • convince + sb + that + 子句說服某人……第 3 段原文：convincing the Brazilian authorities that Capoeira has great cultural value
  • succeed in + N／V-ing成功做到……第 3 段原文：He succeeded in his endeavor
  • transform A into B把 A 轉變成 B第 3 段原文：transformed the martial art into Brazil’s national sport
