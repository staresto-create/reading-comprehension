106 指考閱讀測驗：國歌的歷史演變與文化影響

The Historical Evolution and Cultural Impact of National Anthems
指考 106年第 48–51 題4 題

文章

第 1 段During the past three hundred years, when a country gains its freedom or independence, one of the first things established is a national anthem. National anthems are generally played and sung at formal state occasions and other events which celebrate or support the country’s national identity.

第 2 段Holland’s 16th-century hymn “Het Wilhelmus” is widely considered the world’s oldest national anthem, followed by the U.K.’s “God Save the King/Queen”—also a hymn, popularized in the 1740s. As nationalism spread throughout Europe in the 18th and 19th centuries, so did anthems. Many countries, such as the independent states that are today part of Germany, took “God Save the King/Queen” as a model and adopted hymns (songs of prayer typically addressed to a deity or VIP). Others, notably Spain and France, chose marches (songs with a strong, regular rhythm often performed by military bands)—which expressed a martial rather than monarchic spirit. With imperialism, Europeans spread their musical taste. Even when former colonies gained independence, they often imitated the traditions of their former rulers. The result is that most anthems are either hymns or marches, played on European instruments.

第 3 段Japan’s anthem makes for a good case study of European influence. In the 1860s a British bandmaster living in Japan, John William Fenton, noted that the country did not have a national anthem. A local military officer, Ōyama Iwao, selected the lyrics from a Heian era poem and Fenton wrote the melody. About a decade later, a Japanese committee chose a replacement melody by a court musician—one that had been composed for traditional Japanese instruments, but in a mixed style influenced by Fenton’s arrangement. The version in use today was also altered by German Franz Eckert to fit a Western scale.

第 4 段In addition to hymns and marches, British composer Michael Bristow identifies a couple of more minor categories. National anthems in South and Central America are often operatic, with long, elaborate orchestral introductions. These were influenced by 19th-century Italian opera. Burma and Sri Lanka are both in a folk group, as they rely more on indigenous instruments.

逐段中譯

第 1 段過去三百年間，一個國家獲得自由或獨立的時候，最先確立的事情之一，就是國歌。國歌通常在國家正式的場合，以及其他慶祝或支持國家認同的活動上演奏和演唱。

第 2 段荷蘭 16 世紀的聖歌〈威廉頌〉（Het Wilhelmus）普遍公認是世界上最古老的國歌，其次是英國的〈天佑吾王／女王〉（God Save the King/Queen），這首也是聖歌，在 1740 年代開始流行。18、19 世紀，民族主義在歐洲各地擴散，國歌也跟著擴散。許多國家，例如今天屬於德國的那些獨立邦國，以〈天佑吾王／女王〉為範本，採用聖歌（向神明或重要人物祈禱的歌）。其他國家，特別是西班牙和法國，選擇了進行曲（節奏強而規律的歌，通常由軍樂隊演奏），表達的是軍人的精神，而不是君主的精神。隨著帝國主義擴張，歐洲人也把他們的音樂品味傳播出去。前殖民地即使獨立了，也常常模仿以前統治者的傳統。結果是大多數國歌不是聖歌就是進行曲，而且用歐洲的樂器演奏。

第 3 段日本的國歌，是研究歐洲影響的好例子。1860 年代，一位住在日本的英國軍樂隊隊長 John William Fenton 注意到，這個國家沒有國歌。當地的軍官大山巖從平安時代的一首詩選出歌詞，Fenton 譜了曲。大約十年後，日本的一個委員會選了宮廷樂師寫的曲子來取代原曲。這首曲子是為日本傳統樂器寫的，風格卻融合了 Fenton 編曲的影響。現在使用的版本，又經過德國人 Franz Eckert 修改，以配合西方的音階。

第 4 段除了聖歌和進行曲，英國作曲家 Michael Bristow 還歸納出兩種比較少見的類別。中南美洲的國歌常常帶有歌劇風格，前面有很長、很繁複的管弦樂前奏，這是 19 世紀義大利歌劇帶來的影響。緬甸和斯里蘭卡都屬於民謠類，因為這兩國比較依賴本土的樂器。
