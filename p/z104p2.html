104 指考閱讀測驗：六標準差管理

Six Sigma Management
指考 104年第 40–43 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Six Sigma is a highly disciplined process that helps companies focus on developing and delivering near-perfect products and services. The word “sigma” is a statistical term that measures how far a given process falls short of perfection. The central idea behind Six Sigma is that if a company can measure how many “defects” they have in a commercial production process, they can systematically figure out how to eliminate the problems and get as close to “zero defects” as possible.

第 2 段Training and teamwork are essential elements of the Six Sigma methodology. In other words, companies need to have their team leaders and team members trained to implement the Six Sigma processes. They must learn to use the measurement and improvement tools. They also need to learn communication skills necessary for them to involve customers and suppliers and to serve their needs.

第 3 段Six Sigma was developed in 1986 by Motorola, an American telecommunications company. Engineers in Motorola used it as an informal name for a plan to reduce faults in production processes. A few years later, Motorola extended the name “Six Sigma” to mean a general performance improvement method, beyond purely “defect reduction” in the production process. In 1995, Jack Welch, CEO of General Electrics (GE), decided to implement Six Sigma in GE; and by 1998 GE claimed that Six Sigma had generated over three-quarters of a billion dollars of cost savings.

第 4 段By 2000, Six Sigma was effectively established as an industry in its own right, involving the training, consultancy and implementation of Six Sigma methodology in all sorts of organizations around the world. Organizations as diverse as local governments, prisons, hospitals, the armed forces, banks, and multi-national corporations have been adopting Six Sigma for quality and process improvement.

逐段中譯

第 1 段六標準差（Six Sigma）是一套非常嚴謹的流程，幫助公司專心開發並提供近乎完美的產品和服務。sigma 這個字是統計名詞，衡量一個流程離完美還差多遠。六標準差背後的核心概念是：如果公司能算出商業生產流程中有多少「瑕疵」，就能有系統地想出消除問題的方法，盡可能接近「零瑕疵」。

第 2 段訓練和團隊合作，是六標準差方法不可或缺的要素。也就是說，公司要讓組長和組員接受訓練，才能執行六標準差的流程。他們必須學會使用測量和改善的工具，也要學會必要的溝通技巧，讓顧客和供應商參與，並且滿足他們的需求。

第 3 段六標準差是美國電信公司摩托羅拉在 1986 年發展出來的。摩托羅拉的工程師用這個名稱，非正式地稱呼一項減少生產流程錯誤的計畫。幾年後，摩托羅拉把「六標準差」這個名稱的意思擴大，指一般的績效改善方法，不再只是生產流程中的「減少瑕疵」。1995 年，奇異公司（GE）的執行長傑克·威爾許決定在奇異公司實施六標準差；到了 1998 年，奇異公司宣稱六標準差已經替公司省下超過七億五千萬美元的成本。

第 4 段到了 2000 年，六標準差已經實實在在地自成一個產業，在世界各地各種組織裡提供六標準差方法的訓練、諮詢和實施。地方政府、監獄、醫院、軍隊、銀行和跨國企業，這麼多不同的組織，都採用六標準差來提升品質、改善流程。
