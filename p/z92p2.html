92 指考閱讀測驗：麥可·傑克森的育兒爭議

Michael Jackson's Parenting Controversy
指考 92年第 50–53 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Michael Jackson provoked new concerns for his children’s welfare after he took them to the zoo covered in strange, bright-colored veils “to protect them from kidnappers.”

第 2 段The pop star was pictured walking through Berlin Zoo with his two elder children, Prince Michael, five, and Paris, four.

第 3 段Both youngsters had their faces completely covered in outlandish see-through burgundy-colored veils—a choice of dress which Jackson’s aides said was designed to disguise the youngsters to protect them from being kidnapped. But as they wandered through the zoo with their famous father, it seemed an unlikely explanation.

第 4 段The rare appearance came just 24 hours after the singer provoked outrage by dangling his youngest child, baby Prince Michael II, from the fourth-floor balcony of a hotel window.

第 5 段The nine-month-old boy’s face was covered with a white cloth, which was again said to be a precautionary measure taken by the star to protect his children from becoming kidnappers’ targets.

第 6 段As people across the world criticized Jackson as an unfit father, child protection groups called on German police to take action against the pop legend. However, Berlin prosecutors said they were not investigating the incident, and an inquiry could only be opened if a complaint was filed against Jackson.

題目

50.This passage is most likely taken from a ______.

  2. (B)travel brochure

51.What did Michael Jackson do at Berlin Zoo that caught people’s attention?

  1. (A)He was involved in the kidnapping of two children named Prince and Paris.
  2. (B)He covered his children in veils to prevent them from being kidnapped.
  3. (C)He tried to protect his children from kidnappers by hiding them in the zoo.
  4. (D)He wandered through the zoo with his face covered in a strange cloth.

52.What does “precautionary measure” mean in the passage?

  1. (A)A calculation done with great precision.
  2. (B)A plan carefully designed to achieve a goal.
  3. (C)A step taken to avoid some bad consequences.
  4. (D)A mathematical problem prepared with extreme care.

53.According to the passage, German police could take action against Michael Jackson only when ______.

  1. (A)Berlin prosecutors were investigated
  2. (B)more veils were used to cover the youngsters
  3. (C)an official complaint was made against him
  4. (D)child protection groups called the police again

每一題的依據句

第 50 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

文章報導一位名人最近的舉動：有人拍到他帶孩子逛柏林動物園，並交代 24 小時前發生的事件、各界的批評，以及檢察官的回應，還引述隨行人員和檢察官的說法。這是新聞報導的寫法，所以是 (D)。(A) 私人信件，文章沒有寫信人和收信人；(B) 旅遊手冊，文章雖然提到動物園，卻不是在介紹景點；(C) 研究報告，文章沒有研究方法或數據；三個選項都偏離主題。
錯誤選項：(A) 偏離主題；(B) 偏離主題；(C) 偏離主題
第 51 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段he took them to the zoo covered in strange, bright-colored veils “to protect them from kidnappers

原文 → 正確選項
to protect them from kidnappers → to prevent them from being kidnapped
第 1 段說傑克森帶孩子去動物園，讓孩子罩著鮮豔的面紗，說是為了防範綁匪；第 3 段隨行人員也說，面紗是為了替孩子變裝，免得有人綁架他們。選項把防範綁匪換成 prevent them from being kidnapped，所以是 (B)。(D) 照抄第 3 段的 wandered through the zoo，臉上罩東西的是孩子，不是傑克森；選項的 cloth 是第 5 段蓋在男嬰臉上的白布，而且那是在飯店；(C) 照抄第 5 段的 protect his children from，傑克森是讓孩子罩上面紗，不是把孩子藏在動物園裡；(A) 照抄第 2 段的 Paris，Prince 和 Paris 是他自己的孩子，原文沒有說他涉及綁架。
第 52 題 詞義題 Vocabulary in context

第 5 段which was again said to be a precautionary measure taken by the star to protect his children from becoming kidnappers’ targets.

原文 → 正確選項
to protect his children from becoming kidnappers’ targets → to avoid some bad consequences
這一題答對率只有 48%，判斷步驟：[1] 同一句說明這項 measure 的用途：保護孩子不成為綁匪的目標；[2] 事情還沒發生，先採取行動防範，measure 在這裡是措施，precautionary 是預防的；[3] 預防措施，就是為了避免壞結果而採取的步驟，所以是 (C)。(B) 照抄第 3 段的 designed，原文說的是面紗用來變裝，選項說成為了達成目標而精心設計的計畫，沒有預防的意思；(A) precision（精準）和 precautionary 字形相近，但意思無關；(D) measure 也有測量的意思，選項把它想成數學題目。
怎麼從前後文推出字義 →
第 53 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 6 段an inquiry could only be opened if a complaint was filed against Jackson.

原文 → 正確選項
a complaint was filed against Jackson → an official complaint was made against him
這一題答對率只有 45%，判斷步驟：[1] 第 6 段最後說，柏林檢察官表示目前沒有在調查；[2] 必須有人對傑克森提出告訴（a complaint was filed），才能展開調查（an inquiry could only be opened）；[3] 選項把 filed 換成 made，所以是 (C)。(A) 照抄 Berlin prosecutors，原文是檢察官調查別人，選項說成有人調查檢察官，方向弄反了；(D) 照抄 child protection groups called，兒童保護團體已經要求過警方，原文說的條件是提出告訴，不是再找一次警察；(B) 照抄第 3 段的 veils，面紗是引起擔憂的原因，不是警方採取行動的條件。

逐段中譯

第 1 段麥可·傑克森帶孩子去動物園，讓孩子罩著奇怪的鮮豔面紗，說是「為了防範綁匪」，又引起外界對他孩子福祉的擔憂。

第 2 段有人拍到這位流行樂巨星帶著兩個大孩子，五歲的 Prince Michael 和四歲的 Paris，走在柏林動物園裡。

第 3 段兩個孩子的臉完全罩在古怪的透明酒紅色面紗裡。傑克森的隨行人員說，這樣的打扮是為了替孩子變裝，免得有人綁架他們。可是看著他們和大名鼎鼎的父親在動物園裡閒逛，這個說法實在令人難以相信。

第 4 段這次難得的公開露面，距離他引發眾怒的事件才 24 小時：當時他把最小的孩子，還是嬰兒的 Prince Michael II，懸在飯店四樓窗外的陽台外面晃。

第 5 段這個九個月大的男嬰臉上蓋著一塊白布，據說這同樣是傑克森採取的預防措施，為了保護孩子不成為綁匪的目標。

第 6 段世界各地的人批評傑克森是不適任的父親，兒童保護團體也要求德國警方對這位流行樂傳奇人物採取行動。不過，柏林的檢察官表示，他們並沒有在調查這個事件，必須有人對傑克森提出告訴，才能展開調查。

第 1 段Michael Jackson provoked new concerns for his children’s welfare after he took them to the zoo covered in strange, bright-colored veils “to protect them from kidnappers.”

第 2 段The pop star was pictured walking through Berlin Zoo with his two elder children, Prince Michael, five, and Paris, four.

第 4 段The rare appearance came just 24 hours after the singer provoked outrage by dangling his youngest child, baby Prince Michael II, from the fourth-floor balcony of a hotel window.

第 5 段The nine-month-old boy’s face was covered with a white cloth, which was again said to be a precautionary measure taken by the star to protect his children from becoming kidnappers’ targets.

📌 提醒：不是每一段都有主旨句。開頭鋪陳的段落、承接前一段的延伸段落，還有敘事或描寫的段落，主旨常常藏在字裡行間，找不到一句可以直接畫線。遇到這種段落，與其硬找一句話，不如問自己「這一段在做什麼」（舉例？補充？延續前一段？），再用自己的話歸納出這一段的重點就可以了。
