The Regenerative Ability of Zebrafish

文章

第 1 段Zebrafish, named for their characteristic stripes, have been a popular test subject for researchers. Only a few centimeters in length, the fish breed easily in captivity, grow quickly, and their transparent body makes it easy to study their organs. Above all, they possess some amazing “self-healing” power. When part of their heart is removed, they can grow it back in a matter of weeks. When blinded, they can quickly regain the ability to see.

第 2 段Recent studies show that humans and zebrafish have the same major organs and share 70 percent of the genes. Moreover, 84 percent of human genes associated with disease find a counterpart in zebrafish. Scientists thus hope that understanding the self-healing mystery of the fish may one day allow humans to regenerate such organs as eyes, hearts, and spines.

第 3 段Researchers at Vanderbilt University are particularly interested in zebrafish retina regeneration. They have learned that damage of retina can cause blindness in zebrafish, yet it only takes about three to four weeks before vision is restored. The structure and cell types of zebrafish retinas are almost identical to those of humans. If the process can be replicated in humans, it may give rise to new treatments for blindness caused by retinal damage.

第 4 段In order to know exactly how zebrafish retina is regenerated, the team looked at the neurotransmitter gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), a chemical messenger in the brain that reduces the activity of neurons. They found that lowering GABA levels in zebrafish can trigger retina regeneration, while a high level of GABA concentration will suppress the regeneration process. This suggested that GABA plays an important role in the fish’s ability to regain their sight.

第 5 段The team is beginning to test the GABA theory on mice. If that works, human trials will be next on the agenda. If the research proves successful in humans, some of the nearly 40 million blind people worldwide may one day have a tiny, striped fish to thank.

第 43 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 2 段Scientists thus hope that understanding the self-healing mystery of the fish may one day allow humans to regenerate such organs as eyes, hearts, and spines.

第 3 段it may give rise to new treatments for blindness caused by retinal damage

原文 → 正確選項
new treatments → medical advancements
第 2 段說斑馬魚或許能讓人類的器官再生，第 3 段說可能帶來治療失明的新療法，這些都是醫學上的進展。(A) 第 1 段的 study 講的是斑馬魚身體透明、容易研究，說的是為什麼常拿斑馬魚做研究，文章沒有說應該怎麼研究；(B) 文章只說 GABA 在視網膜再生中扮演重要角色，沒說再生能力從哪裡來；(C) 只說基因有 70% 相同，沒說為什麼相同。
第 44 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 4 段They found that lowering GABA levels in zebrafish can trigger retina regeneration

原文 → 正確選項
trigger retina regeneration → stimulate retina regrowth
trigger 在選項換成 stimulate，regeneration 換成 regrowth。(A) 方向相反，原文說 GABA 降低神經元的活動；(D) 照抄同一段的 chemical 和 trigger，可是會啟動再生的是降低 GABA，不是 GABA 含有什麼成分；(B) 文章沒有比較斑馬魚腦中 GABA 的高低。
第 45 題 詞義題 Vocabulary in context

第 3 段If the process can be replicated in humans, it may give rise to new treatments for blindness caused by retinal damage.

原文 → 正確選項
replicated → Reproduced
把斑馬魚視網膜再生的過程在人類身上「再做一次」，replicate 和 reproduce 都是複製、重現。(B) Reassembled 是把拆開的東西重新組裝，(C) Recycled 是回收再利用，兩個都不是把同一個過程再做一次。(D) Restored 照抄同一段 vision is restored，但恢復的是視力，不是把過程複製到人身上。
第 46 題 推論題 Inference

第 2 段Scientists thus hope that understanding the self-healing mystery of the fish may one day allow humans to regenerate such organs as eyes, hearts, and spines.

原文 → 正確選項
may one day allow humans to regenerate such organs → may allow humans to regrow their organs
hope 和 may 表示的是科學家的期望和推測，還沒有證實，屬於意見。(A)、(B)、(C) 文章都當作研究結果或正在進行的事來陳述，是事實。

逐段中譯

第 1 段斑馬魚因身上特有的條紋而得名，一直是研究人員愛用的實驗對象。斑馬魚只有幾公分長，在人工飼養下容易繁殖、長得快，身體又是透明的，觀察器官十分方便。最重要的是，斑馬魚擁有驚人的「自我修復」能力：心臟切掉一部分，幾個星期就能長回來；眼睛看不見了，也能很快恢復視力。

第 2 段近來的研究顯示，人類和斑馬魚有相同的主要器官，基因有 70% 相同。而且與疾病相關的人類基因，有 84% 在斑馬魚身上找得到對應的基因。因此科學家希望，弄懂斑馬魚自我修復的奧祕，或許有一天能讓人類的眼睛、心臟、脊椎這些器官再生。

第 3 段范德堡大學的研究人員對斑馬魚視網膜的再生特別感興趣。他們發現，視網膜受損會讓斑馬魚失明，但只要大約三到四個星期，視力就能恢復。斑馬魚視網膜的結構和細胞種類幾乎和人類一模一樣，如果這個過程能在人類身上重現，或許能為視網膜受損造成的失明帶來新的療法。

第 4 段為了弄清楚斑馬魚的視網膜究竟怎麼再生，研究團隊觀察了神經傳導物質 GABA（γ-胺基丁酸）。GABA 是腦中的化學信使，會降低神經元的活動。研究團隊發現，降低斑馬魚體內的 GABA 濃度可以啟動視網膜再生，GABA 濃度高則會抑制再生的過程。這表示 GABA 在斑馬魚恢復視力的能力中扮演重要的角色。

第 5 段研究團隊開始在老鼠身上測試 GABA 的理論，如果成功，下一步就是人體試驗。要是研究在人體上也證實有效，全世界將近 4,000 萬名盲人當中，也許有些人有一天要感謝這種小小的條紋魚。
