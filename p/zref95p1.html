Sources of Stress in Modern Society

文章

Stress in modern society seems unavoidable. Most of the time, stress is the result of common day-to-day problems. How you react to stress is critical to becoming a good “manager” of your stress. Stress usually comes from three areas: our environment, our body, and our thought processes. Our environment contains such stressors as noise, crowding, time demands at home and work, pollution, and threats to our safety and well-being. Changes in our body can trigger a stress response. These changes include the physiological changes that occur in growing into adolescence, aging, accidents and serious illnesses, lack of exercise, poor nutrition and chronic lack of sleep.

Our thought processes can also be a source of considerable stress. Experts estimate that people can think up to 200 words per minute. Our brain interprets the “self-talk” about changes in our environment and determines whether or not to push the “panic button.” How you interpret, perceive or label events going on in your environment or your own “self-talk” will determine whether these perceptions will relax you or serve as a source of stress. Many people do not take the time to evaluate their “self-talk” or their perceptions from the outside world. Instead, they give a lot of power to unmediated, panic-inducing perceptions, thus intensifying their personal level of stress.

綱要
  1. 現代社會的壓力似乎無可避免，(Most of the time) 多半來自日常的問題，一個人怎麼回應壓力，決定他能不能好好管理壓力。
  2. 壓力主要有三個來源：環境、身體和思考過程。
  3. 環境中的壓力源有噪音、擁擠、時間壓力、汙染和安全威脅；身體的變化也會引發壓力，例如青春期、老化、重病、缺乏運動、營養不良和睡眠不足。
  4. 思考過程 (also) 也可能是很大的壓力來源：大腦解讀我們的「自我對話」，決定要不要按下「恐慌按鈕」；我們怎麼看待周遭的事，決定自己放鬆還是緊張。
  5. 許多人不花時間評估自己的「自我對話」和對外界的感受，(Instead) 反而任由未經過濾、引發恐慌的感受主導，(thus) 因此壓力越來越大。
