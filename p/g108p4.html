108 學測閱讀測驗：除蟲炸彈的無效性

The Ineffectiveness of Bug Bombs
學測 108年第 53–56 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Got a bug bite problem? Many people who are troubled by skin rashes caused by bug bites use “foggers,” or “bug bombs,” to get rid of the annoying crawlers in their homes. Many people think these bug killers or pesticides will penetrate every place where the insects hide. Actually, quite the opposite is true. Once the pests detect the chemical fog in the room, they’ll hide themselves in walls or other hideaways, where you’ll never be able to treat them effectively.

第 2 段Ohio State University researchers tested three commercially sold foggers in a study on the effect of foggers on bedbugs. After testing these brands on five different groups of live bedbugs for two hours, the scientists saw that the foggers had little—if any—effect on the insects. The researchers said bedbugs hide in cracks and crevices such as under sheets and mattresses, or deep in carpets where foggers won’t reach. Moreover, bugs that do come in contact with the mist may be resistant to the pesticide.

第 3 段Foggers, or bug bombs, should really be a measure of last resort. First of all, the gases used in bug bombs are highly flammable and thus pose a serious risk of fire or explosion if the product is not used properly. Second, once a bug bomb is used, every surface in your home will be covered with the toxic pesticide. When you use a bug bomb, a chemical mixture rains down on your counters, furniture, floors, and walls, leaving behind oily and toxic substances. Your health might thus be endangered. Therefore, it is suggested that people leave the problem to the professionals.

逐段中譯

第 1 段有蟲咬的困擾嗎？許多人因為蟲咬引起皮膚起疹子，就用「噴霧器」，也就是「除蟲彈」，來消滅家裡惱人的小蟲。很多人以為這些殺蟲劑能滲透到蟲子藏身的每個地方，事實上正好相反。害蟲一察覺室內的化學藥霧，就會躲進牆壁或其他藏身處，在那裡你永遠沒辦法有效地處理牠們。

第 2 段俄亥俄州立大學的研究人員研究除蟲彈對床蝨的效果，測試了三種市售的除蟲彈。他們用這幾個品牌對五組活的床蝨測試兩個小時後，發現除蟲彈對這些蟲子幾乎沒有效果，甚至毫無效果。研究人員說，床蝨會躲在縫隙裡，像是床單和床墊底下，或是地毯深處，這些地方除蟲彈都噴不到。此外，真的接觸到藥霧的蟲子，也可能對殺蟲劑有抗藥性。

第 3 段除蟲彈其實應該是最後的手段。首先，除蟲彈用的氣體非常易燃，使用不當就有起火或爆炸的嚴重危險。其次，一旦用了除蟲彈，家裡每一個表面都會蓋上一層有毒的殺蟲劑。使用除蟲彈時，化學藥劑會像雨一樣落在流理台、家具、地板和牆壁上，留下油膩、有毒的物質，可能因此危害你的健康。所以，建議大家把這個問題交給專業人員處理。
