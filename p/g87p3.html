87 學測閱讀測驗：巴黎千禧年木製艾菲爾鐵塔計畫

Paris Millennium Wooden Eiffel Tower Project
學測 87年第 47–50 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段PARIS, Dec. 9 -- Paris will be given a new Eiffel Tower--but made of wood--for the millennium, city mayor Jean Tiberi said Tuesday, presenting a program of celebration for the year 2,000 which would transform the “city of light” into the “capital of light.”

第 2 段The new structure will be 200 meters high, 100 meters shorter than the cast-iron Eiffel Tower built 100 years ago.

第 3 段It will be made of timber as an ecological symbol of “the right to a dignified life on a protected Earth,” said architects Normier, Henni and Lelievre, who were also responsible for the Europe section at the Seville exhibition.

第 4 段The tower, of pinewood, will be built on eight pillars and will be topped with a flower of five metal petals of 700 square meters each.

第 5 段The structure, to be erected in the redeveloped areas of the capital’s 13th district, will cost 250 million francs (US$42 million).

第 6 段Tiberi also said that Paris would be placed under the sign of the lights of “culture and life” and “intelligence.”

第 7 段“If, in 1900, Paris was consecrated the ‘city of light’, it will become the ‘capital of light’ for the millennium,” Tiberi said.

第 8 段The mayor said new buildings would be illuminated, notably along the banks of the Seine, which would become a luminous strip, while Notre Dame Cathedral would be lit from the inside to show off its stained glass windows.

第 9 段A laser clock would be installed on top of the Arc de Triomphe at the top of the Champs Elysees and three laser beams would pick out the hands showing the hours, minutes and seconds in the millennium countdown, Tiberi said.

第 10 段A giant egg-timer will be set up in the Place de la Concorde and its sand will begin running after a solar eclipse scheduled on April 11, 1999.

題目

47.Why is Paris building a new Eiffel Tower?

  1. (A)The old Eiffel Tower must be torn down.
  2. (B)The old iron Eiffel Tower needs to be re-built with wood.
  3. (C)The tower is built to celebrate the Seville exhibition.
  4. (D)The tower is built to celebrate the coming of the year 2,000.

48.According to Mayor Tiberi, Paris will be decorated mostly by ______.

  1. (A)flowers
  2. (B)lights
  3. (C)new buildings
  4. (D)towers

49.What is “the Seine” mentioned in the passage?

  1. (A)A tall monument.
  2. (B)The name of the new tower.
  3. (C)A river.
  4. (D)A crowded street.

50.Why is the new tower made of wood?

  1. (A)To serve as a symbol of environmental concerns.
  2. (B)To reduce the weight and ensure the safety.
  3. (C)Out of the consideration of the limited budget.
  4. (D)To form a contrast with the iron structure of the old tower.

每一題的依據句

第 47 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段presenting a program of celebration for the year 2,000

原文 → 正確選項
celebration for the year 2,000 → celebrate the coming of the year 2,000
第 1 段說巴黎要蓋一座木造的新艾菲爾鐵塔來迎接千禧年，市長同時提出 2000 年的慶祝計畫（a program of celebration for the year 2,000），所以是 (D)。(C) 照抄 Seville exhibition，第 3 段說建築師負責過塞維亞博覽會的歐洲區，蓋塔不是為了慶祝那次博覽會；(B) 照抄 wood，新塔用木頭蓋，但舊的鑄鐵鐵塔並沒有要改建；(A) 原文沒有說舊鐵塔要拆掉。
第 48 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段which would transform the “city of light” into the “capital of light.”

第 8 段The mayor said new buildings would be illuminated

原文 → 正確選項
light → lights
市長要把巴黎從「光之城」變成「光之首都」（capital of light），第 8 段說新建築和塞納河兩岸要打燈、聖母院從內部點燈，第 9 段還要在凱旋門上裝雷射時鐘，裝飾巴黎的主要是燈光，所以是 (B)。(A) 照抄第 4 段的 flower，花是新塔塔頂的造型；(C) 照抄第 8 段的 new buildings，新建築是打燈的對象，裝飾靠的是燈光；(D) 照抄 tower，新塔只有一座。
第 49 題 詞義題 Vocabulary in context

第 8 段notably along the banks of the Seine, which would become a luminous strip

第 8 段說 the banks of the Seine，bank 是河岸，塞納河兩岸要打上燈光，整條變成一條發光的帶子，所以塞納河是一條河，是 (C)。(B) 新塔的名稱，原文沒有提到；(A) 高聳的紀念碑、(D) 擁擠的街道，都不會有兩岸（banks）。
第 50 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 3 段It will be made of timber as an ecological symbol of

原文 → 正確選項
ecological symbol → symbol of environmental concerns
第 3 段說用木材蓋塔，是作為生態的象徵（an ecological symbol），選項換成 symbol of environmental concerns，所以是 (A)。(D) 照抄第 2 段的 iron，第 2 段拿舊鐵塔比的是高度，原文沒有說要和鑄鐵形成對比；(B) 減輕重量、(C) 預算有限，原文都沒有提到。

逐段中譯

第 1 段巴黎 12 月 9 日電：巴黎市長 Jean Tiberi 週二表示，巴黎將有一座新的艾菲爾鐵塔，不過是木造的，用來迎接千禧年。他同時提出 2000 年的慶祝計畫，要把這座「光之城」變成「光之首都」。

第 2 段新的建築高 200 公尺，比 100 年前建造的鑄鐵艾菲爾鐵塔矮 100 公尺。

第 3 段建築師 Normier、Henni 和 Lelievre 說，這座塔用木材建造，是要作為生態的象徵，代表「在保護良好的地球上，享有尊嚴生活的權利」。這三位建築師也負責過塞維亞博覽會的歐洲區。

第 4 段這座松木塔將建在八根柱子上，塔頂是一朵有五片金屬花瓣的花，每片花瓣 700 平方公尺。

第 5 段這座建築將豎立在巴黎第 13 區重新開發的地區，造價 2.5 億法郎（4,200 萬美元）。

第 6 段Tiberi 也說，巴黎將以「文化與生活」和「智慧」之光為主題。

第 7 段「如果說 1900 年巴黎獲尊為『光之城』，那麼到了千禧年，巴黎將成為『光之首都』。」Tiberi 說。

第 8 段市長說，新建築會打上燈光，特別是塞納河兩岸，塞納河將成為一條發光的帶子；聖母院則會從內部點燈，展示它的彩繪玻璃窗。

第 9 段Tiberi 說，香榭麗舍大道頂端的凱旋門上方會裝設一座雷射時鐘，三道雷射光分別標出時針、分針和秒針，為千禧年倒數計時。

第 10 段協和廣場將設置一座巨型沙漏，沙子會在預定 1999 年 4 月 11 日發生的日蝕之後開始流動。
