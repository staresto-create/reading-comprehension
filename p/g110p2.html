The History of Passports

文章

第 1 段The concept of a travel document, which shows a person is under a ruler’s protection while in a foreign land, has probably existed since rulers and states were first invented. But the earliest mention of an object which we might recognize as a passport appeared in about 450 B.C. The Hebrew Bible states that Nehemiah, an official serving King Artaxerxes of ancient Persia, asked permission to travel to Judah. The King agreed and gave Nehemiah a letter “to the governors of the province beyond the river,” requesting safe passage for him as he travelled through their lands.

第 2 段Later, in the medieval Islamic Caliphate, a form of passport was the bara’a, a receipt for taxes paid. Only people who paid their taxes were permitted to travel to different regions of the Caliphate. In medieval Europe, on the other hand, travel documents were issued by local authorities, and generally contained a list of towns and cities which the document holder was permitted to enter or pass through. On the whole, documents were not required for travel to seaports, which were considered open trading points, but documents were required to travel inland from seaports.

第 3 段King Henry V of England is credited with having invented the first true passport, as a way of helping his subjects prove who they were in foreign lands. The earliest reference to these documents is found in a 15th-century Act of Parliament, while the term “passport” came into use about a century later. Nevertheless, passports were not generally required for international travel until the First World War. It was at this time that passports as we would recognize them today began to be used.

逐段中譯

第 1 段旅行文件用來證明一個人身在異地時仍受統治者保護，這個概念大概從有統治者和國家以來就存在了。不過，最早提到一樣我們會認為是護照的東西，大約在西元前 450 年。希伯來聖經記載，古波斯國王亞達薛西的官員尼希米，請求准許他前往猶大。國王答應了，給了尼希米一封信，寫給「河那邊的各省省長」，請他們讓尼希米安全通過他們的領土。

第 2 段後來，在中世紀的伊斯蘭哈里發國，有一種護照叫做 bara’a，是一張繳稅的收據，只有繳了稅的人才能到哈里發國的其他地區旅行。另一方面，在中世紀的歐洲，旅行文件由地方當局核發，上面通常列出持有人可以進入或通過的城鎮。大致來說，前往海港不需要文件，因為海港視為開放的貿易據點，但從海港往內陸走就需要文件了。

第 3 段一般認為英王亨利五世發明了第一本真正的護照，用來幫助他的子民在國外證明自己的身分。最早提到這種文件的，是 15 世紀的一項國會法案，而 passport（護照）這個詞大約一百年後才開始使用。不過，一直到第一次世界大戰，國際旅行才普遍需要護照。我們今天所熟悉的護照，也是從那時候開始使用的。
