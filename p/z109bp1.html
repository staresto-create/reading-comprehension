109 指考補考閱讀測驗：羅傑·伊伯特影展歡迎詞補考

Welcome to Roger Ebert’s Film Festival
指考 109年補考第 36–39 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段I am thrilled to welcome you to the 20th annual Roger Ebert’s Film Festival, a special event in partnership with the University of Illinois College of Media, the greater Champaign-Urbana community, and movie lovers everywhere. Little did we think, back in 1999, that our experiment in reinventing the film festival—to make a festival that celebrated overlooked films, formats and genres, and the women and men who made those films—would become a landmark annual event in the life of our community and contribute to the renovation of the remarkable Virginia Theater, our home from the very beginning.

第 2 段In selecting the films for this year, Chaz Ebert and I looked to women filmmakers for our inspiration. Fully half of our films are either directed or co-directed by women, and several others have women in powerful producing roles. Films directed or co-directed by women include “13th,” directed by Ava DuVernay, “Belle,” directed by Amma Asante, and “American Splendor,” directed by Shari Springer Berman and Robert Pulcini. And women producers include Lynda Obst and Emma Thomas (“Interstellar”) and Ruth Ann Harnisch and Danielle Renfrew Behrens (“Columbus”).

第 3 段Our opening night film is “The Fugitive,” directed by University of Illinois alumnus Andrew Davis. Our Saturday night film is the classic “The Big Lebowski.” Our guest will be the Hollywood legend Jeff Dowd. And of course The Alloy Orchestra will be back, this year with “A Page of Madness,” the classic Japanese silent film.

第 4 段We would like to thank our sponsors, volunteers, festival pass holders, and individual ticket holders for their welcome participation in this endeavor. We look forward to your unwavering support as our festival continues to blossom each spring in central Illinois. We especially want to thank the University of Illinois for their loyal support year after year.

第 5 段This festival is Roger Ebert’s gift to his hometown, and for that we thank him and his wife Chaz. They continue to be a remarkable team, and it is an honor to work with them.

題目

36.Who is most likely the writer of this passage?

  1. (A)The President of the University of Illinois.
  2. (B)A movie critic who is a friend of the Eberts.
  3. (C)A member of the Festival’s organizing committee.
  4. (D)A journalist who reports stories about the Festival.

37.What was the purpose of reinventing Roger Ebert’s Film Festival in 1999?

  1. (A)To work closely with the University of Illinois.
  2. (B)To help restore a building associated with the Festival.
  3. (C)To establish a signature annual event in the community.
  4. (D)To honor people who made films that were not box-office hits.

38.What is the focus of this year’s film selection?

  1. (A)Films produced or directed by women.
  2. (B)Hollywood classics and legends.
  3. (C)Stories featuring female leading roles.
  4. (D)Diversity in formats and genres.

39.Which of the following is NOT included in the writer’s acknowledgement remarks?

  1. (A)The founder of Roger Ebert’s Film Festival.
  2. (B)A student who purchased a pass to the Festival.
  3. (C)The producer of the Festival’s award-winning film.
  4. (D)A company which provided financial support for the Festival.

每一題的依據句

第 36 題 推論題 Inference

第 2 段In selecting the films for this year, Chaz Ebert and I looked to women filmmakers for our inspiration.

第 4 段We would like to thank our sponsors, volunteers, festival pass holders, and individual ticket holders

作者在第 2 段說今年選片時，Chaz Ebert 和我（Chaz Ebert and I）以女性電影人作為靈感，可見作者負責挑選影片；第 4 段又代表影展感謝贊助者、志工和觀眾，全文也一直用 our festival、we 這樣的說法。會選片、會代表影展致謝的，最可能是影展籌備委員會的成員，所以是 (C)。(A) 照抄第 4 段的 University of Illinois，作者是在感謝伊利諾大學，不是代表大學說話；(D) 記者是報導影展的外人，不會說「我們的影展」；(B) 作者說能和伊伯特夫婦合作是一種榮幸，是合作的關係，原文沒有說作者是影評人。
第 37 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段to make a festival that celebrated overlooked films, formats and genres, and the women and men who made those films

原文 → 正確選項
overlooked films → not box-office hits
第 1 段說 1999 年重新打造影展，是要表揚少有人注意的電影（overlooked films），以及拍出這些電影的人。少有人注意的電影，就是票房不好、沒什麼人看的片子，所以是 (D)。這一句是倒裝：Little did we think（我們萬萬沒想到）後面接的，是當初沒有料到的結果，不是目的。(C) 照抄 annual event，成為社區的年度盛事，(B) 幫忙修復 Virginia 戲院，都是當時沒有料到的結果；(A) 照抄 University of Illinois，伊利諾大學媒體學院是合作單位，原文沒有說合作是重新打造影展的目的。
第 38 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 2 段Chaz Ebert and I looked to women filmmakers for our inspiration.

第 2 段Fully half of our films are either directed or co-directed by women, and several others have women in powerful producing roles.

原文 → 正確選項
women filmmakers → produced or directed by women
第 2 段說今年選片以女性電影人（women filmmakers）作為靈感，一半的影片由女性執導或共同執導，還有好幾部由女性擔任製片，所以是 (A)。(C) 照抄 roles，原文的 producing roles 是幕後的製片工作，不是片中的女主角；(B) 照抄第 3 段的 Hollywood，好萊塢傳奇人物 Jeff Dowd 是嘉賓，不是選片重點；(D) 照抄第 1 段的 formats and genres，那是 1999 年重新打造影展時要表揚的對象，不是今年選片的重點。
第 39 題 細節題（未提及） Supporting details

第 4 段We would like to thank our sponsors, volunteers, festival pass holders, and individual ticket holders

第 5 段for that we thank him and his wife Chaz.

作者在第 4、5 段致謝。感謝贊助者（sponsors），是 (D) 出錢支持影展的公司；感謝影展通行證持有人（festival pass holders），買了通行證的學生也在其中，是 (B)；第 5 段感謝羅傑·伊伯特，影展是他送給家鄉的禮物，他就是 (A) 影展的創辦人。第 2 段雖然提到幾位女性製片，但那是介紹今年的選片，不是致謝，原文也沒有提到哪一部片子得獎，所以選 (C)。

逐段中譯

第 1 段我非常興奮，歡迎各位來參加第 20 屆羅傑·伊伯特影展。這個特別的活動，由伊利諾大學媒體學院、香檳–厄巴納（Champaign-Urbana）一帶的社區，以及各地的影迷共同參與。回到 1999 年，我們嘗試重新打造影展，想辦一個表揚少有人注意的電影、形式和類型，也表揚拍出這些電影的男男女女的影展。我們當時萬萬沒想到，這個實驗會成為社區生活裡具有指標意義的年度盛事，還幫忙修復了了不起的 Virginia 戲院，這座戲院從一開始就是影展的根據地。

第 2 段今年選片的時候，Chaz Ebert 和我以女性電影人作為靈感來源。整整一半的影片由女性執導或共同執導，另外還有好幾部片子由女性擔任重要的製片。由女性執導或共同執導的影片，包括 Ava DuVernay 執導的《13th》、Amma Asante 執導的《Belle》，以及 Shari Springer Berman 和 Robert Pulcini 共同執導的《American Splendor》。女性製片則有《Interstellar》的 Lynda Obst 和 Emma Thomas，以及《Columbus》的 Ruth Ann Harnisch 和 Danielle Renfrew Behrens。

第 3 段開幕片是伊利諾大學校友 Andrew Davis 執導的《The Fugitive》。星期六晚上放映的是經典電影《The Big Lebowski》。我們的嘉賓是好萊塢傳奇人物 Jeff Dowd。當然，Alloy 管弦樂團今年也會回來，帶來的是日本經典默片《A Page of Madness》。

第 4 段我們要感謝贊助者、志工、影展通行證持有人和單場購票的觀眾，謝謝各位熱情參與。影展每年春天在伊利諾州中部持續茁壯，期待各位繼續堅定支持。我們特別要感謝伊利諾大學年年忠實的支持。

第 5 段這個影展是羅傑·伊伯特送給家鄉的禮物，為此我們感謝他和他的妻子 Chaz。他們兩位一直是了不起的搭檔，能和他們一起工作，是我們的榮幸。

全文中心思想與段落主旨句

兩者都是　全文中心思想　段落主旨句
藍底標示全文中心思想，底線標示段落主旨句，一句同時是兩者，就用藍底加底線。下面依段落列出這些句子。沒有列出的段落，就是沒有主旨句，請對照「這一篇的文章結構」裡的綱要，找出那一段的重點。

第 1 段I am thrilled to welcome you to the 20th annual Roger Ebert’s Film Festival, a special event in partnership with the University of Illinois College of Media, the greater Champaign-Urbana community, and movie lovers everywhere.

第 2 段In selecting the films for this year, Chaz Ebert and I looked to women filmmakers for our inspiration.

第 4 段We would like to thank our sponsors, volunteers, festival pass holders, and individual ticket holders for their welcome participation in this endeavor.

第 5 段This festival is Roger Ebert’s gift to his hometown, and for that we thank him and his wife Chaz.

📌 提醒：不是每一段都有主旨句。開頭鋪陳的段落、承接前一段的延伸段落，還有敘事或描寫的段落，主旨常常藏在字裡行間，找不到一句可以直接畫線。遇到這種段落，與其硬找一句話，不如問自己「這一段在做什麼」（舉例？補充？延續前一段？），再用自己的話歸納出這一段的重點就可以了。
