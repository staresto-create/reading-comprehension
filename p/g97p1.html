97 學測閱讀測驗：吼猴的行為與地域性

Howler Monkeys' Behavior and Territoriality
學測 97年第 41–44 題4 題

文章

Howler monkeys are named for the long loud cries, or howls, that they make every day. They are the loudest land animal and their howls can be heard three miles away through dense forests. Male howler monkeys use their loud voices to fight for food, mates, or territory. Everyone starts and ends the day by howling to check out where their nearest competitors are.

Interestingly, when there are few howler monkeys in an area, the howling routine takes on a different pattern. In Belize, where howler monkeys were newly reintroduced into a wildlife sanctuary, the howler monkeys were heard only a few times a week rather than every day. Apparently, with plenty of space and no other howler monkeys around, there was no need to check on the whereabouts of their competitors. At the sanctuary, keepers now use recorded howler sounds from a distance so that the monkeys feel the need to make the territorial calls as they would do in the wild. In the future when the population grows, there will be no need for the recording because the howler monkeys will have more reason to check in with the neighbors to define their own territories.

題目

41.Why do howler monkeys howl?

  1. (A)To claim their territory.
  2. (B)To check how popular they are.
  3. (C)To tell others they are going to leave.
  4. (D)To show friendliness to their neighbors.

42.Why did the howler monkeys in Belize howl less often?

  1. (A)They lived too close to each other.
  2. (B)There was enough food for all of them.
  3. (C)There were no other competitors around.
  4. (D)They were not used to the weather there.

43.Why do the keepers at the sanctuary use recorded howls?

  1. (A)To prevent the howler monkeys from getting homesick.
  2. (B)To help howler monkeys maintain their howling ability.
  3. (C)To trick the monkeys into the belief that there is plenty of space around.
  4. (D)To teach the monkeys how to make the loudest cries to scare people away.

44.According to the passage, which of the following is true about howler monkeys?

  1. (A)They howl most often at noon.
  2. (B)They originally came from Belize.
  3. (C)People can hear their howls three miles away.
  4. (D)Female monkeys howl to protect their babies.

綱要
  1. 介紹吼猴的名稱由來與其巨大聲量，說明公猴利用叫聲爭奪食物、配偶和地盤。
  2. (Interestingly) 提出有趣現象：當吼猴數量稀少時，嚎叫模式會改變。
  3. (Apparently) 解釋原因：在貝里斯的猴群因空間充足且無競爭者，無需頻繁嚎叫。
  4. 管理員現使用錄音來模擬吼叫聲，(so that) 以便猴子感受壓力而發出地域性叫聲。
