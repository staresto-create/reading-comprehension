99 指考閱讀測驗：鈞特·葛拉斯的多元創作與藝術理念

Gunter Grass's Diverse Creations and Artistic Philosophy
指考 99年第 48–51 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Gunter Grass was the winner of the 1999 Nobel Prize in Literature. His talents are revealed in a variety of disciplines: He is not only a novelist, poet and playwright, but also a renowned painter and sculptor. As he himself stresses, his creations are closely related to his unique personal history. His father was a German who joined the Nazi party in World War II, while his mother was Polish. As a result, he constantly suffered contradictory feelings: as a Pole who had been victimized, and as someone guilty of harming the Poles. The torment in his heart led him to denounce the Nazis and his political activism has continued throughout his career. His commitment to the peace movement and the environmental movement as well as his unfailing quest for justice has won him praise as “the conscience of the nation.”

第 2 段In the spring of 1996, he was inspired during a trip to Italy to write a poem with his watercolor brush directly on one of his paintings. Before long, a collection of his “water poems” was born. Painting and literature have become his major forms of creativity. For him, painting is a form of creation with concrete, sensual elements, while writing is a hard and abstract process. When he cannot find words to convey his thoughts, painting helps him find the words to express himself. In this way, Grass not only creates simple depictions of the objects he is fond of in life, such as melons, vegetables, fish, and mushrooms, but also uses them as symbols for mental associations of various kinds. For example, to express the complexity of reality, he sometimes places unrelated objects in the same painting, such as a bird and a housefly, or a mushroom and a nail. Grass has depicted a wide variety of natural scenes, animals and plants, and even human artifacts of the German countryside, portraying them in poems, and allowing words to make the paintings rich in literary value.

題目

48.What caused Grass to feel confused and troubled when he was young?

  1. (A)He was the son of a Nazi and a victimized Pole.
  2. (B)He found himself fighting two opposing political parties.
  3. (C)He was trained to be an artist though he wanted to be a poet.
  4. (D)He was born with so many talents that he couldn’t choose a direction.

49.Why has Grass been praised as “the conscience of the nation”?

  1. (A)He victimized the Poles and criticized the Nazis.
  2. (B)He has been a strong advocate of peace and justice.
  3. (C)He has shown great sympathy for the Poles through his poems.
  4. (D)He joined the Nazi party and showed great loyalty to his country.

50.Why was Grass’s trip to Italy important to him?

  1. (A)He was inspired by a fine arts master in Italy.
  2. (B)He formed a new interest in painting simple objects there.
  3. (C)He developed a new form for creating his poems during the trip.
  4. (D)He found a new way to solve the conflict between the Nazis and the Poles.

51.Which of the following correctly characterizes Grass’s poems, according to the passage?

  1. (A)Most of his poems depict the cruelty of the Nazis.
  2. (B)The theme of his poems won him the Nobel Peace Prize.
  3. (C)The poems on his paintings are often not related to objects in the real world.
  4. (D)The ideas in his poems are expressed more thoroughly with the help of his paintings.

逐段中譯

第 1 段鈞特·葛拉斯是 1999 年諾貝爾文學獎得主。他的才華展現在很多領域：他不只是小說家、詩人和劇作家，也是知名的畫家和雕塑家。他自己一再強調，他的創作和他獨特的個人經歷息息相關。他的父親是德國人，二次大戰時加入納粹黨，母親則是波蘭人。因此，他一直承受著矛盾的感受：一方面自己是受害的波蘭人，另一方面又是傷害波蘭人的罪人。內心的煎熬讓他起而譴責納粹，他的政治行動也持續了整個創作生涯。他投入和平運動和環保運動，始終不懈地追求正義，因而贏得「國家的良心」的美譽。

第 2 段1996 年春天，他在義大利旅行時得到靈感，用水彩筆直接把一首詩寫在他的一幅畫上。不久，一本他的「水彩詩」詩集就誕生了。繪畫和文學成了他主要的創作形式。對他來說，繪畫是一種具體、訴諸感官的創作，寫作則是艱難而抽象的過程。找不到文字表達想法的時候，繪畫能幫他找到表達自己的字句。這樣一來，葛拉斯不只簡單地描繪生活中喜愛的東西，例如甜瓜、蔬菜、魚和蘑菇，也用它們象徵各式各樣的聯想。例如，為了表現現實的複雜，他有時候會把不相干的東西放在同一幅畫裡，像是一隻鳥和一隻家蠅，或是一朵蘑菇和一根釘子。葛拉斯描繪過各種自然景色、動物和植物，甚至德國鄉間的人造器物，再用詩來刻畫它們，讓文字使這些畫作富有文學價值。

第 2 段Painting and literature have become his major forms of creativity.

📌 提醒：不是每一段都有主旨句。開頭鋪陳的段落、承接前一段的延伸段落，還有敘事或描寫的段落，主旨常常藏在字裡行間，找不到一句可以直接畫線。遇到這種段落，與其硬找一句話，不如問自己「這一段在做什麼」（舉例？補充？延續前一段？），再用自己的話歸納出這一段的重點就可以了。

綱要
  1. 葛拉斯是 1999 年諾貝爾文學獎得主，(not only … but also) 不但是小說家、詩人、劇作家，也是知名的畫家和雕塑家；他自己強調，作品和個人身世密切相關。
  2. 他的父親是二次大戰時加入納粹黨的德國人，母親是波蘭人，(As a result) 所以他一直心懷矛盾：既覺得自己是受害的波蘭人，又覺得自己是傷害波蘭人的一方。
  3. 內心的煎熬讓他譴責納粹，一生投入政治運動；他參與和平運動與環保運動，又始終追求正義，所以人稱「國家的良心」。
  4. (Before long) 1996 年春天他在義大利旅行時，用水彩筆直接在畫上寫詩，不久就有了一整本「水詩」，繪畫和文學從此成了他主要的創作形式；對他來說，繪畫具體而感性，(while) 寫作則艱難而抽象，找不到字句時，繪畫能幫他找到。
  5. (In this way) 這樣一來，他畫的瓜果、魚和蘑菇不只是描繪，也成了聯想的象徵，(For example) 例如把鳥和蒼蠅、蘑菇和釘子放進同一幅畫，表達現實的複雜；他也描繪德國鄉間的自然景色、動植物和人造物品，再寫成詩，讓畫作富有文學價值。
