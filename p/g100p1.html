100 學測閱讀測驗：肢體語言的迷思

Myths of Body Language
學測 100年第 41–44 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案與文章結構」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

There is a long-held belief that when meeting someone, the more eye contact we have with the person, the better. The result is an unfortunate tendency for people making initial contact—in a job interview, for example—to stare fixedly at the other individual. However, this behavior is likely to make the interviewer feel very uncomfortable. Most of us are comfortable with eye contact lasting a few seconds. But eye contact which persists longer than that can make us nervous.

Another widely accepted belief is that powerful people in a society—often men—show their dominance over others by touching them in a variety of ways. In fact, research shows that in almost all cases, lower-status people initiate touch. Women also initiate touch more often than men do.

The belief that rapid speech and lying go together is also widespread and enduring. We react strongly—and suspiciously—to fast talk. However, the opposite is a greater cause for suspicion. Speech that is slow, because it is laced with pauses or errors, is a more reliable indicator of lying than the opposite.

題目

41.Which of the following statements is true according to the passage?

  1. (A)Rapid speech without mistakes is a reliable sign of intelligence.
  2. (B)Women often play a more dominant role than men in a community.
  3. (C)Speaking slowly is more often a sign of lying than speaking quickly.
  4. (D)Touching tends to be initiated first by people of higher social positions.

42.What is true about fixing your eyes on a person when you first meet him/her?

  1. (A)Fixing your eyes on the person will make him/her feel at ease.
  2. (B)It is more polite to fix your eyes on him/her as long as you can.
  3. (C)Most people feel uneasy to have eye contact for over a few seconds.
  4. (D)It doesn’t make a difference whether you fix your eyes on him/her or not.

43.Which of the following is NOT discussed in the passage?

  1. (A)Facial expressions.
  2. (B)Physical contact.
  3. (C)Rate of speech.
  4. (D)Eye contact.

44.What is the main idea of the passage?

  1. (A)People have an instinct for interpreting non-verbal communication.
  2. (B)We should not judge the intention of a person by his body language.
  3. (C)A good knowledge of body language is essential for successful communication.
  4. (D)Common beliefs about verbal and non-verbal communication are not always correct.
