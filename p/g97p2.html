97 學測閱讀測驗：冰河國家公園道路的建設與公園合作

Glacier National Park Road Construction and Park Collaboration
學測 97年第 45–48 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段After the creation of the Glacier National Park in Montana, the growing number of park visitors increased the need for roads. Eventually, the demand for a road across the mountains led to the building of the Going-to-the-Sun Road.

第 2 段The construction of the Going-to-the-Sun Road was a huge task. After 11 years of work, the final section of the road was completed in 1932. The road is considered an engineering feat. Even today, visitors to the park marvel at how such a road could have been built. It is one of the most scenic roads in North America. The construction of the road has changed the way visitors experience the Glacier National Park. Visitors now can drive over sections of the park that previously took days of horseback riding to see.

第 3 段Just across the border, in Canada, is the Waterton Lakes National Park. In 1931, members of the Rotary Clubs of Alberta and Montana suggested joining the two parks as a symbol of peace and friendship between the two countries. In 1932, the United States and Canadian governments renamed the parks the Waterton-Glacier International Peace Park, the world’s first. More recently, the parks have received several international honors. They were named as a World Heritage Site in 1995. This international recognition highlights the importance of this area, not just to the United States and Canada, but to the entire world.

題目

45.What made it necessary to build a road through the Glacier National Park?

  1. (A)There were too many parks in Montana.
  2. (B)The park was not sunny enough for visitors.
  3. (C)The existing mountain roads were destroyed.
  4. (D)More visitors were interested in going to the park.

46.How has the Going-to-the-Sun Road influenced the way people experience the Glacier National Park?

  1. (A)The scenery along the road is too beautiful for visitors to drive carefully.
  2. (B)It has become a marvelous experience for people to ride horses on this road.
  3. (C)The road has allowed people to see more of the park in a shorter period of time.
  4. (D)The transportation on the road was so difficult that few people could really enjoy the trip.

47.What does “an engineering feat” mean?

  1. (A)A big success in construction.
  2. (B)A magical building machine.
  3. (C)A great disaster for the travelers.
  4. (D)An enjoyable process for engineers.

48.What is special about the Waterton-Glacier International Peace Park?

  1. (A)It is where the glacier runs to the lake.
  2. (B)It is the first park funded by the whole world.
  3. (C)It is a special park built to protect wild animals.
  4. (D)It is composed of two parks located in two countries.

逐段中譯

第 1 段蒙大拿州的冰河國家公園成立以後，遊客越來越多，對道路的需求也增加了。後來大家需要一條翻越山脈的道路，促成了「奔向太陽之路」的興建。

第 2 段興建奔向太陽之路是一項浩大的工程。經過 11 年的施工，最後一段路在 1932 年完工。這條路公認是一項工程壯舉。直到今天，來公園的遊客仍然驚嘆這樣一條路是怎麼蓋起來的。這是北美洲風景最優美的道路之一。這條路改變了遊客體驗冰河國家公園的方式：公園裡有些地方，以前要騎好幾天的馬才看得到，現在遊客開車就能經過。

第 3 段越過邊界，加拿大那一邊是華特頓湖國家公園。1931 年，亞伯達省和蒙大拿州的扶輪社成員提議把兩座公園結合起來，象徵兩國之間的和平與友誼。1932 年，美國和加拿大政府把這兩座公園重新命名為華特頓冰河國際和平公園，這是世界上第一座國際和平公園。近年來，這兩座公園獲得了幾項國際榮譽，1995 年列為世界遺產。這項國際肯定凸顯出這個地區不只對美國和加拿大重要，對全世界都很重要。
