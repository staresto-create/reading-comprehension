Construction and Significance of Hoover Dam

文章

第 1 段Located in Black Canyon straddling the border between Nevada and Arizona in the southwestern region of the United States, Hoover Dam is named one of the Top 10 Construction Achievements of the 20th century. The dam, constructed between 1931 and 1936, was the largest of its kind at the time. Its construction was the result of a massive effort involving thousands of workers and cost over one hundred lives.

第 2 段Since about 1900, the Black Canyon and nearby Boulder Canyon had been investigated for their potential to support a dam that would control floods, provide irrigation water, and produce hydroelectric power. In 1928, the US Congress authorized the project. The winning bid to build the dam was submitted by Six Companies, Inc. However, such a large concrete structure had never been built before, and some of the techniques were unproven. The extreme summer heat and lack of facilities near the site also presented tremendous difficulties. Nevertheless, Six Companies turned over the finished dam to the federal government on March 1, 1936, more than two years ahead of schedule.

第 3 段The initial design of the dam, which was more concerned with the dam’s functionality than its exterior, was criticized by many as being too plain and unremarkable for a project of such immense scale. So Gordon B. Kaufmann, the architect who was brought in to redesign the exterior, greatly streamlined the design and applied an elegant Art Deco style to the entire project. Allen Tupper True, an American illustrator, was also hired to handle the design and decoration of the walls and floors of the new dam. He integrated into his design the images and colors based on Native American visions of rain, lightning, clouds, and animals, thereby creating symbolic patterns which appear both ancient and modern.

第 4 段Today, Hoover Dam has become a national historic landmark. Standing at more than 725 feet above the Colorado River, the highest concrete dam in the Western Hemisphere continues to draw crowds 85 years after its creation, attracting more than a million visitors a year.

逐段中譯

第 1 段胡佛水壩位於美國西南部，坐落在內華達州和亞利桑那州交界的黑峽谷，名列 20 世紀十大營建成就之一。這座水壩在 1931 年到 1936 年間興建，在當時是同類建築中最大的。興建工程動員了數千名工人，也有超過一百人在工程中喪命。

第 2 段大約從 1900 年起，就有人在評估黑峽谷和附近的博爾德峽谷適不適合興建水壩，用來防洪、提供灌溉用水和水力發電。1928 年，美國國會核准了這項工程，由 Six Companies 公司得標興建。不過，這麼大的混凝土結構以前從來沒有人蓋過，有些施工技術也還沒有經過驗證。再加上夏天酷熱，工地附近又缺乏設施，困難重重。儘管如此，Six Companies 還是在 1936 年 3 月 1 日把完工的水壩交給聯邦政府，比預定時間早了兩年多。

第 3 段水壩最初的設計重視功能，不太講究外觀，很多人批評，以這麼大規模的工程來說，這個設計太平淡、太普通了。於是，建築師 Gordon B. Kaufmann 受邀重新設計外觀，他把造型大幅簡化成流線型，整個工程都採用優雅的裝飾藝術（Art Deco）風格。美國插畫家 Allen Tupper True 也受聘負責新水壩牆面和地板的設計與裝飾。他把美洲原住民對雨、閃電、雲和動物的想像，連同相關的圖像和色彩，融入自己的設計，創造出既古老又現代的象徵圖案。

第 4 段如今，胡佛水壩已經成為國家歷史地標。這座西半球最高的混凝土水壩，高出科羅拉多河 725 英尺以上，完工 85 年後仍然吸引大批人潮，每年有超過一百萬名遊客造訪。

字詞搭配 配對與接法

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
  • straddle the border between A and B橫跨 A 和 B 的邊界第 1 段原文：straddling the border between Nevada and Arizona
  • the largest of its kind同類當中最大的第 1 段原文：the largest of its kind at the time
  • be the result of + N是……的結果第 1 段原文：Its construction was the result of a massive effort
  • cost + 數字 + lives奪走……條人命第 1 段原文：cost over one hundred lives
  • the potential to + V……的潛力第 2 段原文：their potential to support a dam
  • authorize a project核准計畫第 2 段原文：authorized the project
  • submit a bid投標第 2 段原文：The winning bid to build the dam was submitted by
  • present difficulties帶來困難第 2 段原文：presented tremendous difficulties
  • turn over + sth + to + sb把……移交給某人第 2 段原文：turned over the finished dam to the federal government
  • ahead of schedule比預定的時間提早第 2 段原文：more than two years ahead of schedule
  • be concerned with + N重視……、著重在……第 3 段原文：was more concerned with the dam’s functionality
  • criticize + sth + as + adj.批評……太……第 3 段原文：was criticized by many as being too plain
  • bring in + sb請來（專家、人手）第 3 段原文：the architect who was brought in to redesign the exterior
  • integrate A into B把 A 融入 B第 3 段原文：He integrated into his design the images
  • draw crowds吸引人潮第 4 段原文：continues to draw crowds
