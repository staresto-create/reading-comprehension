101 指考閱讀測驗：憤怒鳥在各種媒體的流行

The Spread of Angry Birds across Media
指考 101年第 44–47 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Angry Birds is a video game developed by Finnish computer game developer Rovio Mobile. Inspired primarily by a sketch of stylized wingless birds, the game was first released for Apple’s mobile operating system in December 2009. Since then, over 12 million copies of the game have been purchased from Apple’s App Store.

第 2 段With its fast-growing popularity worldwide, the game and its characters—angry birds and their enemy pigs—have been referenced in television programs throughout the world. The Israeli comedy A Wonderful Country, one of the nation’s most popular TV programs, satirized recent failed Israeli-Palestinian peace attempts by featuring the Angry Birds in peace negotiations with the pigs. Clips of the segment went viral, getting viewers from all around the world. American television hosts Conan O’Brien, Jon Stewart, and Daniel Tosh have referenced the game in comedy sketches on their respective series, Conan, The Daily Show, and Tosh.0. Some of the game’s more notable fans include Prime Minister David Cameron of the United Kingdom, who plays the iPad version of the game, and author Salman Rushdie, who is believed to be “something of a master at Angry Birds.”

第 3 段Angry Birds and its characters have also been featured in advertisements in different forms. In March 2011, the characters began appearing in a series of advertisements for Microsoft’s Bing search engine. In the same year, Nokia projected an advertisement in Austin, Texas that included the game’s characters on a downtown building for its new handset. Later, a T-Mobile advertisement filmed in Spain included a real-life mock-up of the game in a city plaza. Nokia also used the game in Malaysia to promote an attempt to set a world record for the largest number of people playing a single mobile game.

第 4 段Angry Birds has even inspired works of philosophical analogy. A five-part essay entitled “Angry Birds Yoga—How to Eliminate the Green Pigs in Your Life” was written by Giridhari Dasar in Brazil, utilizing the characters and gameplay mechanics to interpret various concepts of yoga philosophy. The piece attracted much media attention for its unique method of philosophical presentation.

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 45 題 詞義題 Vocabulary in context

第 2 段Clips of the segment went viral, getting viewers from all around the world.

viral 原本是「病毒的」，go viral 指影片或訊息像病毒一樣在網路上快速散播、爆紅。後面接著說這段節目吸引了全世界的觀眾（getting viewers from all around the world），所以是 (D) 很受歡迎。(B) 諷刺的，是這段節目的內容，前一句說它諷刺以巴和談失敗，但 went viral 說的是節目傳開了；(A) 明顯的、(C) 刺激的，都和吸引全世界的觀眾接不上。

逐段中譯

第 1 段《憤怒鳥》是芬蘭電腦遊戲開發商 Rovio Mobile 開發的電玩遊戲。這款遊戲主要的靈感，來自一張畫著造型簡化、沒有翅膀的鳥的草圖，2009 年 12 月首先在蘋果的行動作業系統上推出。從那時起，蘋果的 App Store 已經賣出超過 1,200 萬份。

第 2 段隨著在全世界的人氣快速成長，這款遊戲和裡面的角色──憤怒鳥和牠們的敵人綠豬──在世界各地的電視節目中都有人引用。以色列喜劇《美好國度》（A Wonderful Country）是以色列最受歡迎的電視節目之一，節目讓憤怒鳥和豬進行和平談判，藉此諷刺最近以色列和巴勒斯坦和談失敗。這一段節目的片段在網路上瘋傳，吸引了全世界的觀眾。美國電視主持人 Conan O’Brien、Jon Stewart 和 Daniel Tosh，都在各自的節目《Conan》、《The Daily Show》和《Tosh.0》的喜劇短劇裡引用過這款遊戲。這款遊戲比較有名的玩家，包括英國首相 David Cameron，他玩的是 iPad 版；還有作家 Salman Rushdie，據說他「玩憤怒鳥可以說是個高手」。

第 3 段憤怒鳥和裡面的角色，也以各種形式出現在廣告裡。2011 年 3 月，這些角色開始出現在微軟 Bing 搜尋引擎的一系列廣告中。同一年，Nokia 在德州奧斯汀市中心的一棟大樓上，投影了一則有遊戲角色的廣告，宣傳新手機。後來，T-Mobile 在西班牙拍的一則廣告，在一座城市廣場上用實物重現了這款遊戲。Nokia 也在馬來西亞用這款遊戲，宣傳一場挑戰世界紀錄的活動，挑戰同時玩同一款手機遊戲的最多人數。

第 4 段憤怒鳥甚至啟發了用哲學來類比的作品。巴西的 Giridhari Dasar 寫了一篇分成五部分的文章，題目是〈憤怒鳥瑜伽：如何消滅你生命中的綠豬〉，用遊戲的角色和玩法，詮釋瑜伽哲學的各種概念。這篇文章用獨特的方式呈現哲學，吸引了很多媒體的注意。
