92 學測閱讀測驗：電梯操作員的日常

A Day in the Life of an Elevator Operator
學測 92年第 41–43 題3 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案與文章結構」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

I usually go to work by subway, and I get to work by 8:00 A.M. Before I start my job, I put on my uniform and look at myself in the mirror to make sure that I look neat. At 8:30 in the morning, I go on duty. I usually eat lunch from twelve to one and generally take a fifteen-minute break in the morning and in the afternoon. At 4:30 in the afternoon, I go off duty.

I enjoy my job very much. I meet all kinds of people and talk to everyone. Many people ask me questions, and I give them the necessary information. I try to be very helpful. I always call out floors very clearly, and I am constantly on the move. Most men take off their hats in my car, and sometimes I have to tell passengers to put out their cigarettes. Some people smile at me, but others just ignore me. In fact, my life can be described as consisting of a series of “ups” and “downs.”

題目

  1. (A)what the author looks like
  2. (B)what “life” means to the author
  3. (C)what a typical day is like for the author
  4. (D)what kinds of people the author works with

42.We may infer from the passage that the author is a(n) ______.

  1. (A)policeman
  2. (B)driver
  3. (C)floor cleaner
  4. (D)elevator operator

43.The expression “constantly on the move” in the passage refers to the fact that ______.

  1. (A)the author always calls out floors very clearly
  2. (B)the author seldom stays in one place for a long while
  3. (C)the author meets all kinds of people and talks to everyone
  4. (D)the author frequently helps passengers move their baggage

綱要
  1. 描述作者（電梯操作員）的上班準備與工作時間安排。
  2. 說明作者喜歡這份工作，(I meet all kinds of people and talk to everyone) 因能接觸形形色色的人並交談。
  3. (Many people ask me questions, and I give them) 作者會提供必要資訊，並努力提供幫助。
  4. (I always call out floors very clearly, and I am constantly on the move) 強調作者會清晰報樓，且持續移動。
  5. (In fact) 作者說自己的生活就是一連串的「上上下下」，也描述了乘客的舉動，例如脫帽、抽菸。
