103 學測閱讀測驗：自動駕駛汽車的未來

The Future of Driverless Cars
學測 103年第 53–56 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Today the car seems to make periodic leaps in progress. A variety of driver assistance technologies are appearing on new cars. A developing technology called Vehicle-to-Vehicle communication, or V2V, is being tested by automotive manufacturers as a way to help reduce the number of accidents. V2V works by using wireless signals to send information back and forth between cars about their location, speed and direction, so that they keep safe distances from each other. Another new technology being tested is Vehicle-to-Infrastructure communication, or V2I. V2I would allow vehicles to communicate with road signs or traffic signals and provide information to the vehicle about safety issues. V2I could also request traffic information from a traffic management system and access the best possible routes. Both V2V and V2I have the potential to reduce around 80 percent of vehicle crashes on the road.

第 2 段More and more new cars can reverse-park, read traffic signs, maintain a safe distance in steady traffic and brake automatically to avoid crashes. Moreover, a number of firms are creating cars that drive themselves to a chosen destination without a human at the controls. It is predicted that driverless cars will be ready for sale within five years. If and when cars go completely driverless, the benefits will be enormous. Google, which already uses prototypes of such cars to ferry its staff along Californian freeways, once put a blind man in a prototype and filmed him being driven off to buy takeaway hamburgers. If this works, huge numbers of elderly and disabled people can regain their personal mobility. The young will not have to pay crippling motor insurance, because their reckless hands and feet will no longer touch the wheel or the accelerator. People who commute by car will gain hours each day to work, rest, or read a newspaper.

題目

53.Which of the following statements is true about V2V?

  1. (A)V2V communication has been very well developed.
  2. (B)Through V2V, drivers can chat with each other on the road.
  3. (C)V2V is designed to decrease crashes by keeping safe distances.
  4. (D)Through V2V, a car can warn cyclists nearby of its approach.

54.What does “infrastructure” in Vehicle-to-Infrastructure refer to?

  1. (A)Traffic facilities and information systems.
  2. (B)The basic structure of roads and bridges.
  3. (C)Knowledge and regulations about safe driving.
  4. (D)The traffic department of the government.

55.Which of the following is NOT a potential benefit of driverless cars?

  1. (A)The elderly will become more mobile.
  2. (B)“Drivers” can sleep in cars all the way to work.
  3. (C)People can race cars to their heart’s content.
  4. (D)A blind man can get into a car and travel safely.

56.What can be inferred from the passage?

  1. (A)Cars will refuse to start if the driver is drunk.
  2. (B)The future may be a vehicle-accident-free era.
  3. (C)Everyone, including children, can afford a car.
  4. (D)The production of driverless cars is still far away.

每一題的依據句

第 53 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 1 段is being tested by automotive manufacturers as a way to help reduce the number of accidents.

第 1 段so that they keep safe distances from each other.

原文 → 正確選項
reduce the number of accidents → decrease crashes
keep safe distances → keeping safe distances
第 1 段說 V2V 的目的是減少事故的數量（reduce the number of accidents），做法是讓車輛之間互傳資訊、保持安全距離（keep safe distances），選項換成 decrease crashes by keeping safe distances。(A) 原文說 V2V 是發展中的技術（a developing technology），還在測試，不是已經發展得很成熟；(B) V2V 傳送的是位置、速度和方向，不是讓駕駛彼此聊天；(D) 原文講的是車和車之間，沒有提到騎單車的人。
第 54 題 詞義題（指代詞） Vocabulary in context

第 1 段V2I would allow vehicles to communicate with road signs or traffic signals and provide information to the vehicle about safety issues.

第 1 段V2I could also request traffic information from a traffic management system and access the best possible routes.

原文 → 正確選項
road signs or traffic signals → Traffic facilities
traffic management system → information systems
第 1 段說 V2I 讓車輛和道路標誌、交通號誌（road signs or traffic signals）溝通，也能向交通管理系統（traffic management system）要路況資訊。和車輛溝通的這兩類東西，選項概括成 traffic facilities and information systems，所以是 (A)。(B) 道路和橋梁的基本結構是 infrastructure 的一般意思，但這篇裡和車輛溝通的是號誌和系統，不是道路本身；(C) 安全駕駛的知識和法規、(D) 政府的交通部門，文章都沒提。
第 55 題 細節題（未提及） Supporting details

第 2 段once put a blind man in a prototype and filmed him being driven off to buy takeaway hamburgers.

第 2 段If this works, huge numbers of elderly and disabled people can regain their personal mobility.

第 2 段People who commute by car will gain hours each day to work, rest, or read a newspaper.

有提到的三個好處都在第 2 段，選項換了說法：elderly people can regain their personal mobility 是 (A)；讓盲人坐原型車去買漢堡是 (D)；通勤的人每天多出時間休息（rest）是 (B) 在車上一路睡到公司。文章沒說無人車能讓人盡情飆車，原文反而說年輕人莽撞的手腳再也不會碰到方向盤和油門，所以選 (C)。
第 56 題 推論題 Inference

第 1 段Both V2V and V2I have the potential to reduce around 80 percent of vehicle crashes on the road.

第 2 段brake automatically to avoid crashes

第 1 段說 V2V 和 V2I 有潛力減少大約 80% 的車禍，第 2 段說新車已經能自動煞車避免撞車，再加上不需要人操控的無人車。這些技術一起發展下去，可以推論未來可能是沒有車禍的時代，所以是 (B)。(D) 照抄 driverless cars，原文說無人車預計五年內上市，不是還很遙遠；(A) 文章沒有談酒駕；(C) 原文說的是年輕人不必付高額保險費，沒說包括兒童在內每個人都買得起車。

逐段中譯

第 1 段現今汽車似乎每隔一段時間就有大躍進。各式各樣的駕駛輔助技術紛紛出現在新車上。一項發展中的技術叫做車對車通訊（Vehicle-to-Vehicle communication，簡稱 V2V），汽車製造商正在測試這項技術，希望藉此減少事故的數量。V2V 的運作方式是用無線訊號在車輛之間互相傳送位置、速度和方向等資訊，讓車輛彼此保持安全距離。另一項正在測試的新技術是車對基礎設施通訊（Vehicle-to-Infrastructure communication，簡稱 V2I）。V2I 能讓車輛和道路標誌或交通號誌溝通，並且提供車輛安全相關的資訊。V2I 也可以向交通管理系統要求路況資訊，找出最佳路線。V2V 和 V2I 都有潛力減少道路上大約 80% 的車禍。

第 2 段越來越多新車能自動倒車入庫、辨識交通標誌、在車流穩定時保持安全車距，還能自動煞車避免撞車。此外，好幾家公司正在研發不需要人操控、自己開到指定目的地的汽車。據預測，無人駕駛汽車五年內就能上市。如果汽車真有完全無人駕駛的一天，好處會非常大。Google 已經用這種車的原型車在加州的高速公路上接送員工，還曾讓一位盲人坐進原型車，拍下他坐車去買外帶漢堡的過程。如果這行得通，大批年長者和身心障礙者就能重新自由行動。年輕人不必再付高得嚇人的汽車保險費，因為他們莽撞的手腳再也不會碰到方向盤和油門。開車通勤的人每天會多出好幾個小時，可以工作、休息或看報紙。
