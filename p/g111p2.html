111 學測閱讀測驗：安全帽的演進史

The Evolution of the Hard Hat
學測 111年第 39–42 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段A hard hat is a helmet used mostly at worksites to protect the head from injuries due to falling objects. Since its introduction in the early 20th century, the headgear has saved countless lives and is considered the number one safety tool for construction workers.

第 2 段The hard hat was invented in 1919 by Edward W. Bullard, who had just returned from World War I. Before the war, workers used to smear their hats with coal tar for protection of their head. Bullard, having witnessed the life-saving power of the metal helmet in the War, decided to produce a head protection device that was affordable for every worker and lightweight enough to be worn all day long. The Hard Boiled Hat was thus born, using steamed canvas and leather, covered with black paint, and featuring a suspension system to reduce impact. Soon, hard hats became widely used. The headgear was later made mandatory at construction sites in major construction projects, such as the Hoover Dam in 1931 and the Golden Gate Bridge in 1933.

第 3 段Over the past century, hard hats have advanced considerably, evolving from canvas and leather to aluminum, fiberglass, and, eventually, to thermoplastic. Recently, new models have been introduced and accessories added to meet the needs of laborers working on various job sites. For instance, a ventilated hard hat was developed to keep wearers cooler, and see-through face shields were attached to better see the hazards lurking above. Today, attachments include radios, sensors, cameras, and a lot more. A common color code has also been developed for recognizing people and their roles on site. Yellow is used for general laborers and contractors, white (or sometimes black) for supervisors and managers, and green for inspectors and new workers.

第 4 段New products continue to expand the market. Global sales of hard hats totaled USD 2.1 billion in 2016, and are expected to reach USD 3.19 billion in 2025.

逐段中譯

第 1 段安全帽是主要用在工地的頭盔，保護頭部不受掉落物砸傷。安全帽在 20 世紀初問世以來，拯救了無數生命，公認是建築工人的頭號安全裝備。

第 2 段安全帽是 Edward W. Bullard 在 1919 年發明的，他當時剛從第一次世界大戰返鄉。戰前，工人習慣在帽子上塗煤焦油來保護頭部。Bullard 在戰場上親眼看到金屬頭盔救人一命，決定做一種每個工人都買得起、又輕到可以戴一整天的頭部防護裝置。Hard Boiled Hat 就這樣誕生了：用蒸過的帆布和皮革製成，外面塗上黑漆，裡面有減輕撞擊的懸吊系統。不久，安全帽就普及開來。後來在大型工程裡，工地規定一定要戴安全帽，例如 1931 年的胡佛水壩和 1933 年的金門大橋。

第 3 段過去一百年來，安全帽有了長足的進步，材質從帆布和皮革，演變為鋁、玻璃纖維，最後是熱塑性塑膠。近年來，業者推出了新款式，也加上各種配件，滿足在各種工作場所的勞工需求。例如，透氣的安全帽讓配戴的人比較涼爽；加裝透明面罩，則能更清楚看見頭頂上潛藏的危險。如今的配件還有無線電、感測器、攝影機等等。業界也發展出通用的顏色標示，用來辨識工地上的人和他們的職務：黃色代表一般工人和承包商，白色（有時是黑色）代表監工和經理，綠色代表檢查人員和新進工人。

第 4 段新產品持續擴大市場。2016 年全球安全帽銷售額總計 21 億美元，預計 2025 年將達到 31.9 億美元。

字詞搭配 配對與接法

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
  • protect + N + from + N保護……不受……傷害第 1 段原文：to protect the head from injuries
  • save lives拯救性命第 1 段原文：has saved countless lives
  • smear A with B在 A 上塗抹 B第 2 段原文：smear their hats with coal tar
  • be affordable for + sb某人負擔得起第 2 段原文：affordable for every worker
  • be made mandatory成為強制規定第 2 段原文：was later made mandatory at construction sites
  • a construction site工地第 2 段原文：mandatory at construction sites
  • meet the needs of + N滿足……的需求第 3 段原文：to meet the needs of laborers
  • a color code顏色代碼（用顏色分類的規則）第 3 段原文：A common color code
  • on site在現場第 3 段原文：people and their roles on site
  • expand the market擴大市場第 4 段原文：New products continue to expand the market
  • be expected to + V預計會……第 4 段原文：are expected to reach USD 3.19 billion in 2025
