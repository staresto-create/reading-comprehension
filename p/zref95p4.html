The Rise and Fall of Mayan Civilization and Disappearance of Cities

文章

Mayan civilization was a Central American (Mesoamerican) civilization that covered one third of Central America. Mayan civilization is famous for its fully developed written language, striking art, huge architecture, and advanced mathematical and astronomical knowledge. These features came out of the Preclassic Period but reached their highest development in the Classic Period (250 to 900 AD). When it prospered, it was one of the most densely populated and culturally active societies in the world.

Mayan civilization was not one single empire, but rather many separate city-states which had a common cultural background. Strangely, after the Classic Period (900), these cities ceased to be centers of importance. Mayan culture continued and still exists today but in a rural setting. One question that interests scholars is why these cities just disappeared. They were swallowed up by the tropical jungles. Travelers and scholars rediscovered them and have studied them only in the last two hundred years or so. In fact, scholars have debates about what happened, but some factors seem to be part of the explanation of why they disappeared.

Toward the end there was large-scale, continuous warfare among the city-states. The rulers were highly competitive and made fierce wars on one another. Another factor considered is overpopulation. Scholars indicate that the Mayan population reached up to 200 people per square kilometer, among the highest in the pre-industrial world. Overpopulation led to overuse of the tropical forest, the source of their food.

Research from pollen indicates that there was almost no tropical forest left. This led to damage to the environment of the area, further resulting in a lack of water and then drought. Thus warfare, overpopulation, overuse of resources, and destruction of the environment appear to be active factors involved in the disappearance of Mayan cities.

綱要
  1. 馬雅文明涵蓋中美洲三分之一的地區，以文字、藝術、建築和數學、天文知識聞名，在古典期（西元 250 到 900 年）達到高峰，是當時人口最密集、文化最活躍的社會之一。
  2. 馬雅不是單一帝國，(but rather) 而是許多文化背景相同的獨立城邦；(Strangely) 奇怪的是，古典期之後這些城市不再是重要的中心，馬雅文化雖然延續至今，卻只存在鄉間。
  3. 學者想知道這些城市為什麼消失：城市淹沒在熱帶叢林裡，旅人和學者直到最近兩百年左右才重新發現；(In fact) 學者對原因仍有爭論，但有幾個因素似乎可以解釋。
  4. (Toward the end) 馬雅末期，城邦之間戰爭大規模持續不斷；(Another factor) 另一個因素是人口過多，每平方公里多達 200 人，過度利用熱帶森林這個糧食來源。
  5. 花粉研究顯示熱帶森林幾乎消失殆盡，環境遭到破壞，接著缺水、乾旱；(Thus) 因此戰爭、人口過多、資源過度利用和環境破壞，似乎都是馬雅城市消失的原因。
