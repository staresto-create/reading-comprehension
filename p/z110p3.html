110 指考閱讀測驗：獵鷹術的歷史、訓練與文化意涵

History, Training, and Cultural Significance of Falconry
指考 110年第 44–47 題4 題

文章

第 1 段Falcons are powerful birds that may be trained to hunt in cooperation with humans. The art of training falcons as “hunting dogs of the skies” is called falconry. It was developed in the ancient Arabian Desert around 4000 B.C. In the past, falconry mainly involved hunting with falcons to supplement one’s diet. But now, it has become the favorite sport and pastime in the Arabian Peninsula.

第 2 段Falconry includes three major processes: acquiring the falcons, training them, and hunting with them. Every year, falconers begin capturing wild falcons in June and July, the migration season. The best birds to catch are the ones with naturally nurtured hunting skills, aged one or two years. Once the falcons are captured, their heads are immediately covered with a leather hood. This is essential because they imprint as their master whomever they see first after the hood is removed. Right after a bird is caught, the training process starts. It begins by depriving the bird of food to make it easier to tame. The birds are taught to know their own name and respond to their master when called. Arab falconers live with their birds day and night in order to build a strong relationship with them.

第 3 段By the end of October or mid-November, the trained falcons are ready to hunt in the desert. They have excellent vision, 2.6 times greater than that of a human. They are also the fastest divers on the planet. One hunting technique that sets them apart from other animals is that they can be trained to deliver their prey, without killing it first. This is vital in Islamic culture because animals used for food must still be alive to ensure that the meat is halal, that is, properly prepared according to Islamic law.

第 4 段Falcons have played such a crucial role in traditional Islamic cultures that a number of countries in the Middle East have made them their national bird. Falconry has grown so popular that specialist hospitals have been established to take care of these magnificent birds. Falcons are even issued their own passports. They are the only animals in the United Arab Emirates that are legally allowed to travel inside planes, enjoying a level of luxury that some humans can only dream of.

第 2 段The best birds to catch are the ones with naturally nurtured hunting skills, aged one or two years.

原文 → 正確選項
naturally nurtured hunting skills → natural hunting skills
The best birds to catch → most ideal for falconers to catch
第 2 段說最適合捕捉的（The best birds to catch）是一、兩歲、天生就有狩獵本領（naturally nurtured hunting skills）的獵鷹，選項換成 most ideal for falconers to catch，所以是 (D)。(A) 照抄 of food，不給食物是捉到以後的訓練步驟，目的是讓獵鷹容易馴服，不是為了捕捉；(C) 照抄 imprint，原文的 imprint 是獵鷹把第一眼看到的人認作主人，不是把名字印在頭罩上；(B) 照抄 master，原文說鷹獵者日夜和獵鷹住在一起，訓練時並沒有不讓獵鷹看到主人。

逐段中譯

第 1 段獵鷹是強壯有力的鳥，可以訓練來和人類合作打獵。把獵鷹訓練成「天空獵犬」的技藝，稱為鷹獵。鷹獵大約在西元前 4000 年發源於古代的阿拉伯沙漠。過去，鷹獵主要是帶著獵鷹打獵，用獵物補充食物；不過現在，鷹獵已經成為阿拉伯半島最受歡迎的運動和休閒活動。

第 2 段鷹獵包括三個主要過程：取得獵鷹、訓練獵鷹，以及帶著獵鷹打獵。每年六月和七月是獵鷹遷徙的季節，鷹獵者在這時開始捕捉野生獵鷹。最適合捕捉的是一、兩歲，天生就有狩獵本領的獵鷹。獵鷹一捉到，就要立刻用皮製頭罩蓋住牠的頭。這一步非常重要，因為拿掉頭罩以後，獵鷹第一眼看到誰，就會把誰認作主人。獵鷹捉到以後，訓練馬上開始。第一步是先不給牠食物，讓牠比較容易馴服。接著教牠認得自己的名字，聽到主人呼喚就回應。阿拉伯的鷹獵者日夜和他們的獵鷹住在一起，和牠們建立緊密的關係。

第 3 段到了十月底或十一月中，訓練好的獵鷹就可以到沙漠打獵了。獵鷹的視力極佳，是人類的 2.6 倍；牠們也是地球上俯衝速度最快的動物。獵鷹有一項狩獵本領和其他動物不同：經過訓練，牠們可以把獵物帶回來，不先殺死。這在伊斯蘭文化中非常重要，因為當作食物的動物必須還活著，才能確保肉是清真（halal）的，也就是依照伊斯蘭律法妥善處理。

第 4 段獵鷹在傳統伊斯蘭文化中非常重要，好幾個中東國家都把獵鷹定為國鳥。鷹獵越來越受歡迎，甚至有專門照顧這些雄偉鳥類的醫院。獵鷹還能領到自己的護照。在阿拉伯聯合大公國，獵鷹是唯一可以合法搭飛機、待在機艙裡的動物，享受的奢華待遇，有些人只能夢想。
