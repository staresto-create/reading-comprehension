US Federal Education Act ESSA and Assessment Reform

文章

第 1 段In 2015, President Obama of the USA signed the Every Student Succeeds Act (ESSA), replacing the Bush-era No Child Left Behind (NCLB) that had been in effect since 2001. This new Act provides states with more decision-making power regarding curriculum, instruction, and assessment. Below are some big-picture ideas influencing many states as they approach the assessment task.

第 2 段One important idea is flexibility. For years, states have used standardized K-12 assessments, similar to the SAT and ACT for college application, to measure student achievement. They are easy to use, but they fail to give a complete picture of how a student is progressing. Thus, states are rethinking one-size-fits-all standardized assessments and are instead considering personalized, student-centered assessments in schools. Obviously, the task is difficult and time-consuming. Fortunately, modern technology can help solve this dilemma. For instance, computer adaptive assessments can automatically adjust questions based on a student’s performances on the previous questions. This mechanism prevents the computer from giving questions that are obviously too easy or too difficult for the student. It thus allows teachers to quickly assess a student’s level of understanding and provide instant feedback to help in the learning process.

第 3 段Another idea is multi-subject testing. Several states have started to incorporate subjects beyond the traditional math and reading items in their K-12 assessments. All 50 states include tests on science at least twice prior to senior high school, and some are now starting to include social studies, government, or economics. Some states are also moving toward assessing multiple subjects on one test, for example, reading and social studies.

第 4 段A third idea is the emphasis on students’ learning process. In pursuit of a student-centered approach, many states are putting more emphasis on assessments throughout the learning process rather than on traditional end-of-year summative tests. Teachers are encouraged to accumulate data at different points in their students’ learning process. These data together present a more complete picture of a student’s learning.

第 5 段The last idea regards the purpose of assessment. Assessment should be used to inform both teachers’ instruction and students’ learning. Teachers can modify their teaching based on students’ performance on tests; students can identify their own problems and make plans for improvement.

逐段中譯

第 1 段2015 年，美國總統歐巴馬簽署了《每個學生都成功法》（ESSA），取代小布希時代從 2001 年起實施的《有教無類法》（NCLB）。這項新法讓各州在課程、教學和評量上，有更多的決定權。以下是影響許多州規劃評量工作的幾個大方向。

第 2 段一個重要的方向是彈性。多年來，各州都用從幼稚園到 12 年級（K-12）的標準化評量來測量學生的學習成就，就像申請大學用的 SAT 和 ACT 一樣。這些評量容易使用，卻無法完整呈現學生的學習進展。因此，各州正在重新思考這種一體適用的標準化評量，改為考慮在學校採用個人化、以學生為中心的評量。顯然，這項工作既困難又費時。幸好，現代科技可以幫忙解決這個難題。例如，電腦適性測驗可以依照學生前面幾題的表現，自動調整題目。這套機制能避免電腦出明顯太簡單或太難的題目給學生，讓老師很快就能評估學生理解的程度，立即給予回饋，幫助學生學習。

第 3 段另一個方向是多科目測驗。有幾個州已經開始在 K-12 評量中，加入傳統的數學和閱讀以外的科目。全部 50 個州在高中以前，都至少考兩次自然科學，有些州現在也開始加考社會、公民或經濟。有些州還朝著在一份測驗裡同時評量多個科目發展，例如閱讀加上社會。

第 4 段第三個方向是重視學生的學習過程。為了以學生為中心，許多州更重視學習過程中的評量，而不是傳統的學年末總結性測驗。這些州也鼓勵老師在學生學習過程的不同時間點累積資料。這些資料合在一起，可以更完整地呈現學生的學習情形。

第 5 段最後一個方向和評量的目的有關。評量應該同時用來引導老師的教學和學生的學習。老師可以依照學生在測驗中的表現調整教學；學生可以找出自己的問題，擬定改進的計畫。
