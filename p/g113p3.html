113 學測閱讀測驗：「100% Fish」計畫

The "100% Fish" Project
學測 113年第 43–46 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段In 2020, Petur Oddsson, a power station worker in Iceland, was struck by a 60,000-volt current. The electrical shock burned almost half of his body and melted layers of his skin off. Such deep and extensive burns can be fatal. But Oddsson’s life was saved by a creative invention: transplanting codfish skins onto human bodies.

第 2 段A triumph for medical technology, Oddsson’s fish skin transplantation was actually part of the astonishing achievement of “100% Fish,” an ambitious task in promoting environmental efficiency. The Icelandic project, from which this pioneering procedure emerged, strives for making a fundamental change in the marine industry. It aims to encourage full utilization of each fish caught, and to strengthen innovation in seafood products.

第 3 段Reducing waste of fish catch has become a serious issue today, when many countries are faced with food crises. According to a 2003 study, about 60% of a codfish caught in Iceland was lost or wasted during the production process for human consumption. Under the guidance of 100% Fish, however, Icelanders are now using almost 95% of a cod. Cod skin, for example, is made into calcium supplement and energy drinks, and even as material for skin transplantation as in Oddsson’s case. Dried fish heads and spines are exported to West Africa, where they are used as the base of a protein-rich soup. Other groundbreaking products, including Omega-3 capsules, cold virus pretreatment sprays, and dog snacks are made from what was once cod catch detritus.

第 4 段The Icelandic success is accomplished largely through cooperative efforts across various industries. 100% Fish takes the initiative to show seafood companies the importance of collaboration, and facilitate valuable connections between fishing companies and other participants of the project, including academia, start-ups, and research and development teams. By sharing knowledge and information, the different sectors are able to come up with improved processing and handling, through which creation of various innovative products was made possible. The project not only helps Icelanders to get 30% more value from each cod than most developed countries, but also provides an effective model to promote resource efficiency worldwide.

逐段中譯

第 1 段2020 年，冰島電廠員工 Petur Oddsson 觸電，電流高達 6 萬伏特。這次電擊燒傷他將近一半的身體，好幾層皮膚都熔掉了。這麼深、面積又這麼大的燒傷可能致命，但一項創新的發明救了 Oddsson 一命：把鱈魚皮移植到人體上。

第 2 段Oddsson 的魚皮移植是醫療科技的一大勝利，其實也是「100% Fish」計畫驚人成就的一部分；這是一項推動資源有效利用的遠大計畫，這種開創性的療法就是出自這項冰島的計畫。計畫的宗旨是從根本改變漁業，目標是鼓勵充分利用捕到的每一條魚，並加強海鮮產品的創新。

第 3 段如今許多國家面臨糧食危機，減少漁獲的浪費成了一大課題。根據 2003 年的一項研究，冰島捕到的鱈魚在加工成食品的過程中，大約有 60% 流失或浪費掉了。不過在 100% Fish 的帶動下，冰島人現在幾乎能利用一條鱈魚的 95%。舉例來說，鱈魚皮可以做成鈣片和能量飲料，甚至像 Oddsson 的例子那樣，當作皮膚移植的材料。曬乾的魚頭和魚骨外銷到西非，在當地用來熬煮富含蛋白質的湯。其他突破性的產品，包括 Omega-3 膠囊、預防感冒病毒的噴劑和狗零食，原料都是過去當作廢棄物的鱈魚下腳料。

第 4 段冰島的成功，主要靠的是跨產業的合作。100% Fish 主動向海鮮業者說明合作的重要，並促成漁業公司和計畫其他參與者建立寶貴的連結，包括學術界、新創公司和研發團隊。各領域分享知識和資訊，改良了加工和處理的方式，才開發出各種創新的產品。這項計畫不但讓冰島人從每一條鱈魚得到的價值比大多數已開發國家多出 30%，也為全世界推動資源有效利用提供了有效的模式。

字詞搭配 配對與接法

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。

  • extensive burns大面積的燒傷第 1 段
  • be struck by + N讓……擊中第 1 段原文：was struck by a 60,000-volt current
  • make a fundamental change做出根本的改變第 2 段原文：making a fundamental change in the marine industry
  • a triumph for + N……的一大勝利第 2 段原文：A triumph for medical technology
  • strive for + N努力追求……第 2 段原文：strives for making a fundamental change
  • for human consumption供人食用第 3 段
  • under the guidance of sb/sth在……的帶領下第 3 段原文：Under the guidance of 100% Fish
  • be faced with + N面臨……第 3 段原文：are faced with food crises
  • be made into + N做成……第 3 段原文：is made into calcium supplement
  • take the initiative主動（做某事）第 4 段原文：takes the initiative to show
