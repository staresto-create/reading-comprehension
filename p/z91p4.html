91 指考閱讀測驗：原子彈科學家與廣島倖存者

The Atomic Scientist and the Hiroshima Survivor
指考 91年第 62–65 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案與文章結構」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

Boll, a German scientist who was the star of the Manhattan Project, is lecturing at a New York university on the 50th anniversary of the dropping of the atomic bomb on Japan. The speech has become part of his summer routine, self-justifying what was done near the end of World War II. The scientific triumph of his career, in fact, has been paralyzing his life, silently troubling his conscience. He has been haunted with visions of the devastation of the Japanese city, Hiroshima. “Dreams have become nightmares,” he concedes. Then when his wife, an Austrian Jew whose parents were victims of the Nazi death camps, falls seriously ill, she designs a salvation journey for her husband, bringing together Boll and Amai, a woman from Hiroshima who lost her face to the world’s first atomic blast.

Following his speech, Amai, wearing a face reconstructed by the skilled hands of an American surgeon, approaches Boll. Now 56 and a documentary filmmaker, she wants to interview him about his role in making the bomb. Boll looks to Amai to redeem himself for what he has done. And when Amai sees through Boll’s façade to his inner depressed state, she recognizes a shadowy reflection of the despair and exhaustion that possessed the Japanese after the war, and begins to comprehend the price he has paid for victory.

題目

62.Boll ______.

  1. (A)is a professor at the State University of New York
  2. (B)has been enjoying the triumph over the Japanese
  3. (C)was one of the scientists who made atomic bombs
  4. (D)has convinced himself that he had done nothing wrong

63.Amai ______.

  1. (A)is a documentary filmmaker, who was not personally involved in World War II
  2. (B)lost her face in the world’s first atomic bombing and is now wearing a new face
  3. (C)cannot forgive and forget those who have done so much harm to her and her country
  4. (D)sees the despair of the bomb-makers and enjoys the price they have paid for what they did

64.Boll’s wife ______.

  1. (A)will not forgive the Nazis because her parents were killed in the Nazi concentration camps
  2. (B)fails to understand how troubled her husband feels after the destruction of Hiroshima
  3. (C)arranges the meeting for Boll to save him from the recurring nightmares about the atomic bombing
  4. (D)hopes that Amai will help Boll continue his life career as an atomic bomb-maker after her death

65.What is the author of the passage trying to tell the readers?

  1. (A)Killers hardly ever seek forgiveness from their victims.
  2. (B)People can always justify whatever they have done wrong.
  3. (C)Boll and his wife take great pride in the success of the Manhattan Project.
  4. (D)Victory in war will never be gained without paying the price.

綱要
  1. 曼哈頓計畫的德國科學家 Boll 在原爆五十週年時到紐約一所大學演講，替當年的作為辯護。
  2. (in fact) 這項科學成就其實讓他的人生停擺，廣島的慘狀一直糾纏他，美夢成了惡夢。
  3. (Then) 他的妻子是父母死於納粹集中營的奧地利猶太人，她病重時替丈夫安排一趟救贖之旅，讓他和在原爆中毀容的廣島女性 Amai 見面。
  4. (Following) 演講結束後，臉部經美國外科醫師重建、現為紀錄片導演的 Amai 走向 Boll，想訪問他在製造原子彈時扮演的角色。
  5. (And) Amai 看穿 Boll 的外表，看到他和戰後日本人一樣絕望疲憊，開始體會他為勝利付出的代價。
