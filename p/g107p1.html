107 學測閱讀測驗：白宮的歷史

The History of the White House
學測 107年第 41–44 題4 題

文章

第 1 段For more than two hundred years, the White House has stood as a symbol of the United States Presidency, the U.S. government, and the American people. In 1790, President George Washington declared that the federal government would reside in a district “not exceeding ten miles square … on the river Potomac.” As preparations began, a competition was held to find a builder of the “President’s House.” Nine proposals were submitted, and the Irish-born architect James Hoban won the gold medal for his practical and handsome design. Construction began when the first cornerstone was laid in October of 1792. Although President Washington oversaw the construction of the house, he never lived in it. It was not until 1800, when the White House was nearly completed, that its first residents, President John Adams and his wife Abigail, moved in.

第 2 段American presidents can express their individual style in how they decorate the house and in how they receive the public. Thomas Jefferson held the first inaugural open house in 1805; many of those who attended the swearing-in ceremony at the U.S. Capitol simply followed him home. President Jefferson also opened the house for public tours, and it has remained open, except during wartime, ever since. In addition, Jefferson welcomed visitors to annual receptions on New Year’s Day and on the Fourth of July. Abraham Lincoln did the same, but then the inaugural crowds became far too large for the White House to accommodate comfortably, and this also created a security issue. It was not until Grover Cleveland’s first presidency that some effective crowd control measures were implemented to address the problem caused by this practice.

第 3 段At various times in history, the White House has been known as the “President’s Palace,” the “President’s House,” and the “Executive Mansion.” President Theodore Roosevelt officially gave the White House its current name in 1901.

第 44 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段Nine proposals were submitted, and the Irish-born architect James Hoban won the gold medal for his practical and handsome design.

這一題要找和原文不符的敘述。第 1 段說設計白宮的是出生於愛爾蘭的建築師霍本，不是美國總統，所以選 (B)。其他三個選項都和原文相符：第 3 段說白宮曾經叫做總統宮、總統府和行政官邸，是 (A)；第 2 段說除了戰爭期間，白宮一直開放參觀，是 (C)；第 1 段說聯邦政府設在不超過十英里見方的特區，是 (D)。

逐段中譯

第 1 段兩百多年來，白宮一直是美國總統職位、美國政府和美國人民的象徵。1790 年，華盛頓總統宣布聯邦政府將設在波多馬克河畔一個「不超過十英里見方」的特區。籌備工作展開後，政府舉辦了一場比賽，徵求「總統府」的建造者。比賽共收到九件設計提案，出生於愛爾蘭的建築師霍本以實用又美觀的設計贏得金牌。1792 年 10 月，第一塊基石奠下，工程正式開始。華盛頓總統雖然監督了白宮的興建，卻從來沒有住過。一直到 1800 年白宮快要完工時，第一批住戶亞當斯總統和夫人 Abigail 才搬進去。

第 2 段美國總統可以從白宮的布置和接待民眾的方式，展現個人的風格。傑佛遜在 1805 年舉辦了第一次就職開放日；許多在美國國會大廈參加宣誓典禮的民眾，乾脆跟著他一路回到白宮。傑佛遜總統也開放白宮讓民眾參觀，從此以後，除了戰爭期間，白宮一直對外開放。此外，傑佛遜每年元旦和七月四日國慶日也開放白宮，歡迎訪客。林肯也照著這麼做，但是後來就職的人潮多到白宮無法舒適地容納，也造成了安全問題。一直到克里夫蘭第一次擔任總統時，才實施了一些有效的人潮管制措施，解決這個做法帶來的問題。

第 3 段白宮在歷史上不同時期，曾經叫做「總統宮」、「總統府」和「行政官邸」。1901 年，老羅斯福總統正式把它定名為現在的名稱。
