96 指考閱讀測驗：注意力不足/過動症

AD/HD) (Attention Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder
指考 96年第 50–52 題3 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Most parents dread a note or call from school saying that their child’s behavior is “not normal.” If your child’s academic performance and social life is suffering because they don’t pay attention, can’t sit still and act without thinking, it is most likely that they have AD/HD (Attention Deficit/ Hyperactivity Disorder).

第 2 段AD/HD is a neurological disorder which stems not from the home environment, but from biological and genetic causes. Its symptoms typically show up in early childhood. The main characteristics are inattention, hyperactivity and impulsivity. Inattentive children have difficulty focusing on completing a task or learning something new. Hyperactive children always seem to be restless. Often they report that they need to stay busy and may try to do several tasks at once. Impulsive children often do not think before they act. They often blurt out inappropriate comments or have difficulty taking turns in conversation.

第 3 段Most children can be inattentive, hyperactive, or impulsive at times. It is when these behaviors are inappropriate for their age and affect different areas in their lives that the disorder is diagnosed. Depression, anxiety, and learning disabilities may co-exist with AD/HD. Therefore, if a child is suspected of AD/HD, it is very important that he or she be evaluated by a professional.

第 4 段Once your child is diagnosed with AD/HD, it is important to let the school know so that they can provide appropriate academic and social support. Your child’s school should keep this information confidential and it can usually make accommodations in the classroom to fit your child’s learning needs.

題目

50.What is the cause of AD/HD?

  1. (A)Gene problem.
  2. (B)Academic pressure.
  3. (C)Illness in childhood.
  4. (D)Inappropriate home environment.

51.Which of the following are most likely AD/HD patients?

  1. (A)Children who are very lazy.
  2. (B)Children who are very attentive.
  3. (C)Children who act over-cautiously.
  4. (D)Children who have learning difficulties.

52.What is the first thing to do if your child is suspected of AD/HD?

  1. (A)Report it to the school.
  2. (B)Consult a professional.
  3. (C)Wait until the child grows up.
  4. (D)Send the child to a special school.

逐段中譯

第 1 段大多數家長都很怕學校寄來通知或打電話來，說自己孩子的行為「不正常」。如果你的孩子因為不專心、坐不住、不加思考就行動，課業表現和社交生活都受到影響，那麼孩子很可能有注意力不足過動症（AD/HD）。

第 2 段注意力不足過動症是一種神經疾患，原因不是家庭環境，而是生理和遺傳的因素。症狀通常在幼兒期就會出現。主要的特徵是注意力不足、過動和衝動。注意力不足的孩子，很難專心完成一件工作或學習新的東西。過動的孩子好像總是靜不下來，常常說自己需要一直忙著，可能會同時做好幾件事。衝動的孩子常常不加思考就行動，常常脫口說出不恰當的話，或是在對話中很難等別人說完再輪到自己。

第 3 段大部分的孩子有時候都會不專心、過動或衝動。要等到這些行為和年齡不相稱，而且影響到生活的不同方面，才會診斷為這種疾患。憂鬱、焦慮和學習障礙，可能和注意力不足過動症同時出現。因此，如果懷疑孩子有注意力不足過動症，請專業人員評估孩子的狀況非常重要。

第 4 段孩子一旦診斷出注意力不足過動症，就要讓學校知道，學校才能提供適當的課業和社交支援。孩子的學校應該把這項資訊保密，學校通常也能在課堂上做一些調整，配合孩子的學習需求。
