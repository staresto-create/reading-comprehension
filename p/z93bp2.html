93 指考敏督利補考閱讀測驗：台灣地震建築安全問題補考

Building Safety Issues in Taiwan Earthquakes
指考 93年敏督利補考第 50–52 題3 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Many buildings in Taiwan have survived earthquakes, but some appear to have fallen over like toys. This, say experts, is a sign that engineers did not pay enough attention to the foundations, or that the quake virtually turned the soft sediment below into a “liquid”—meaning the apartments should not have been constructed in these locations in the first place. According to Dr. David Petley, of the UK’s Portsmouth University, there are widespread occurrences in Taiwan where building codes are ignored and where development has occurred in locations where it should not have been allowed. Having worked on government-backed risk reduction projects in Taiwan since 1991, Dr. Petley further pointed out that there’s quite a lot of illegal construction work going on in Taiwan as a result of the very rapid development.

第 2 段However, seismologists and engineers say that the government’s use of three building codes formulated to counter the threat of earthquakes may have spared the island from a death toll far higher than that now being revealed. For instance, Walter Mooney, of the US Geological Survey, conducted a research comparing Taiwan’s preparedness for earthquakes with that of Turkey, and found that Taiwan has been doing a good job: Whereas an estimated 65% of buildings in Turkey were illegally constructed, failing many safety tests, Taiwan has been largely successful in enforcing its own laws.

題目

50.This passage was written mainly to ______.

  1. (A)warn Taiwan residents about the danger of rapid urban development
  2. (B)propose solutions to the problems caused by earthquakes in Taiwan
  3. (C)discuss the safety problems of buildings in Taiwan during earthquakes
  4. (D)compare the death tolls in Turkey and Taiwan during recent earthquakes

51.Which of the following is mentioned by Dr. Petley as one factor leading to the collapse of buildings when earthquakes strike Taiwan?

  1. (A)There is too much water underground.
  2. (B)There is a lack of qualified engineers.
  3. (C)Building codes are still waiting approval.
  4. (D)Buildings have been constructed where they shouldn’t have been.

52.The word seismologists in the second paragraph most likely means “experts on ______”.

  1. (A)earthquake studies
  2. (B)weather conditions
  3. (C)government policies
  4. (D)water resources

每一題的依據句

第 50 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 1 段說明台灣有些建築在地震中倒塌的原因：地基沒打好、軟土液化、不遵守建築法規、蓋在不該蓋的地方；第 2 段說政府的防震法規讓死亡人數少了很多，和土耳其相比，台灣執法相當成功。全文討論的是台灣建築在地震中的安全問題，所以是 (C)。(A) 過於狹隘，發展太快只是第 1 段 Petley 提到的原因之一，而且文章不是在警告居民；(D) 過於狹隘，只講第 2 段，而且第 2 段比較的是違法建築的比例，不是死亡人數；(B) 偏離主題，文章分析問題、說明政府已有的法規，並沒有提出解決方法。
錯誤選項：(A) 過於狹隘（只講第 1 段）；(B) 偏離主題；(D) 過於狹隘（只講第 2 段）
第 51 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段development has occurred in locations where it should not have been allowed.

原文 → 正確選項
where it should not have been allowed → where they shouldn’t have been
第 1 段 Petley 博士指出兩件事：台灣普遍不遵守建築法規，而且開發案出現在不該核准開發的地點（locations where it should not have been allowed）。選項把不該核准開發的地點，換成建築物蓋在不該蓋的地方，所以是 (D)。(C) 照抄 building codes，原文說的是有人無視建築法規（ignored），不是還在等核准；(B) 照抄 engineers，說工程師沒有注意地基的是專家（experts），而且原文沒有說工程師人數不夠；(A) 軟土在地震中變成液體，也是專家的說法，不是 Petley 提出的，原文也沒有說地下水太多。
第 52 題 詞義題 Vocabulary in context

第 2 段seismologists and engineers say that the government’s use of three building codes formulated to counter the threat of earthquakes

原文 → 正確選項
earthquakes → earthquake
seismologists 和 engineers 並列，兩種專家評論的都是政府為了因應地震威脅（the threat of earthquakes）而制定的建築法規；第 2 段接著舉美國地質調查局的研究，比較台灣和土耳其的防震準備。全段談的都是地震，seismologists 是研究地震的專家，所以是 (A)。(C) 照抄 government，政府是他們評論的對象，不是他們的專業；(B) 天氣、(D) 水資源，第 2 段都沒有提到。

逐段中譯

第 1 段台灣有許多建築物經得起地震，有些卻像玩具一樣倒了下來。專家說，這表示工程師沒有充分注意地基，要不然就是地震幾乎讓底下的軟土變成了「液體」，也就是說，這些公寓一開始就不該蓋在這些地方。英國樸茨茅斯大學的 David Petley 博士說，台灣普遍有不遵守建築法規的情形，也有不少開發案出現在根本不該核准開發的地點。Petley 博士從 1991 年起就在台灣參與政府支持的減災計畫，他也指出，台灣發展得太快，因此有不少違法的營建工程。

第 2 段然而，地震學家和工程師說，政府為了因應地震的威脅，制定了三套建築法規，可能因此讓台灣避免了更慘重的傷亡，否則死亡人數會遠遠超過目前公布的數字。舉例來說，美國地質調查局的 Walter Mooney 做了一項研究，比較台灣和土耳其的防震準備，發現台灣做得很好：土耳其估計有 65% 的建築是違法興建，通不過許多安全檢驗；台灣則大致成功落實了自己的法律。

綱要
  1. 台灣許多建築經得起地震，(but) 有些卻像玩具一樣倒下，專家認為原因是地基沒打好，或是軟土在地震中液化。
