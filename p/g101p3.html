101 學測閱讀測驗：餐叉的演進史

The Evolution of the Fork
學測 101年第 49–52 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Forks trace their origins back to the ancient Greeks. Forks at that time were fairly large with two tines that aided in the carving of meat in the kitchen. The tines prevented meat from twisting or moving during carving and allowed food to slide off more easily than it would with a knife.

第 2 段By the 7th century A.D., royal courts of the Middle East began to use forks at the table for dining. From the 10th through the 13th centuries, forks were fairly common among the wealthy in Byzantium. In the 11th century, a Byzantine wife brought forks to Italy; however, they were not widely adopted there until the 16th century. Then in 1533, forks were brought from Italy to France. The French were also slow to accept forks, for using them was thought to be awkward.

第 3 段In 1608, forks were brought to England by Thomas Coryate, who saw them during his travels in Italy. The English first ridiculed forks as being unnecessary. “Why should a person need a fork when God had given him hands?” they asked. Slowly, however, forks came to be adopted by the wealthy as a symbol of their social status. They were prized possessions made of expensive materials intended to impress guests. By the mid 1600s, eating with forks was considered fashionable among the wealthy British.

第 4 段Early table forks were modeled after kitchen forks, but small pieces of food often fell through the two tines or slipped off easily. In late 17th century France, larger forks with four curved tines were developed. The additional tines made diners less likely to drop food, and the curved tines served as a scoop so people did not have to constantly switch to a spoon while eating. By the early 19th century, four-tined forks had also been developed in Germany and England and slowly began to spread to America.

逐段中譯

第 1 段叉子的起源可以追溯到古希臘人。當時的叉子相當大，有兩根叉齒，在廚房裡用來幫忙切肉。切肉時，叉齒能讓肉不會扭動或移動，食物從叉子上滑下來，也比從刀子上滑下來容易。

第 2 段到了西元七世紀，中東的宮廷開始在餐桌上用叉子吃飯。十到十三世紀，叉子在拜占庭的富人之間相當普遍。十一世紀時，一位拜占庭籍的妻子把叉子帶到義大利；不過，直到十六世紀，義大利人才廣泛使用叉子。接著在 1533 年，叉子從義大利傳到法國。法國人也很慢才接受叉子，因為他們認為用叉子很彆扭。

第 3 段1608 年，Thomas Coryate 把叉子帶到英國，他是在義大利旅行時看到叉子的。英國人起初嘲笑叉子是多餘的東西。他們問：「上帝都給了人一雙手，人為什麼還需要叉子？」不過，富人慢慢用起叉子，把叉子當作社會地位的象徵。這些叉子是珍貴的財產，用昂貴的材料做成，為的是讓客人留下深刻的印象。到了 1600 年代中期，用叉子吃飯在英國富人之間已經是一種時尚。

第 4 段早期的餐叉是照著廚房用的叉子做的，但小塊食物常常從兩根叉齒之間掉下去，或是很容易滑落。十七世紀末，法國發展出比較大、有四根彎曲叉齒的叉子。多了叉齒，用餐的人比較不容易讓食物掉下來；彎曲的叉齒還可以當杓子用，吃東西時就不必一直換成湯匙。到了十九世紀初，德國和英國也發展出四齒叉，並且慢慢傳到美國。
