93 學測閱讀測驗：博物館古物歸還爭議

Debate over Returning Ancient Artifacts to Museums
學測 93年第 45–47 題3 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段In 1993, the Metropolitan Museum of Art reluctantly handed over 363 pieces of gold, silver, precious stones, paintings, and sculptures back to Turkey after a court case. Following increasing calls for the return of artistic objects that were removed decades or centuries ago, some of the world’s leading museums have signed a declaration that they will not hand back the ancient artifacts to their countries of origin. They say people all over the world have only been able to fully appreciate ancient civilizations because these museums have provided access to these artistic objects. The ancient civilizations would not be so deeply admired today if these ancient artifacts were not so widely available to an international public in major museums throughout Europe and America. For example, Egyptian culture would not have become so well-known if the museums had not put Egyptian mummies on show.

第 2 段The British Museum has not signed the declaration, but says it fully supports it. Over the recent years, it has faced growing pressure to hand back the Elgin Marbles, sculptures taken from the Parthenon in Athens, Greece, in the 19th century. But the British Museum has said that the Museum is the best possible place for them. “They must remain here if the museum is to continue to achieve its aim, which is to show the world to the world,” said the director of the museum.

題目

45.What reason do the major museums provide for not handing back the ancient objects?

  1. (A)Only in the leading museums can the objects be fully appreciated by the world.
  2. (B)It is better for those objects to remain at a certain place than to be moved around.
  3. (C)They need those well-known ancient objects to attract people from all over the world.
  4. (D)Ancient civilizations can only be admired if they are removed from their home countries.

46.Which of the following is NOT mentioned as one of the “countries of origin” in the article?

  1. (A)Turkey.
  2. (B)Britain.
  3. (C)Egypt.
  4. (D)Greece.

47.What does “the world” mean in “show the world to the world”?

  1. (A)The global village.
  2. (B)The leading museums.
  3. (C)The ancient civilizations.
  4. (D)The international public.

每一題的依據句

第 45 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段They say people all over the world have only been able to fully appreciate ancient civilizations because these museums have provided access to these artistic objects.

原文 → 正確選項
fully appreciate → fully appreciated
第 1 段說這些博物館的理由是：全世界的人能充分欣賞（fully appreciate）古代文明，只因為（only…because）博物館讓大家看得到這些藝術品。選項把 only 移到句首、改成倒裝句 Only in the leading museums can…，主詞也從人換成文物，句型變了，意思相同，所以是 (A)。(D) 照抄 admired，原文說的是文物在各大博物館展出，古代文明才這麼受推崇，沒有說非得離開原屬國才能受推崇；(C) 照抄 well-known，well-known 在原文是說埃及文化因此出名，不是博物館需要有名的文物來吸引人；(B) 照抄第 2 段的 remain，那是大英博物館館長說雕塑要留在館裡才能達成宗旨，不是說文物留在一處比搬來搬去好。
第 46 題 細節題（未提及） Supporting details

第 1 段handed over 363 pieces of gold, silver, precious stones, paintings, and sculptures back to Turkey

第 1 段Egyptian culture would not have become so well-known if the museums had not put Egyptian mummies on show.

第 2 段sculptures taken from the Parthenon in Athens, Greece, in the 19th century.

countries of origin 是文物原本所屬的國家。第 1 段大都會藝術博物館把文物歸還給 (A) 土耳其；埃及木乃伊來自 (C) 埃及；第 2 段埃爾金石雕是從 (D) 希臘雅典的帕德嫩神殿拿走的。英國只以大英博物館的身分出現，是收藏文物的一方，不是文物的原屬國，所以選 (B)。
第 47 題 詞義題 Vocabulary in context

第 2 段which is to show the world to the world,

第 1 段They say people all over the world have only been able to fully appreciate ancient civilizations because these museums have provided access to these artistic objects.

show A to B 是把 A 展示給 B 看。第 1 段說博物館讓全世界的人（people all over the world）看得到這些藝術品，因而能欣賞古代文明。展示出來的第一個 the world，是各地的古代文明，所以是 (C)；展示的對象，也就是第二個 the world，才是全世界的人。(D) 照抄第 1 段的 international public，那是第二個 the world 的意思，把兩個 the world 弄反了；(B) 照抄 leading museums，博物館是做展示的一方；(A) 地球村，原文沒有提到。

逐段中譯

第 1 段1993 年，大都會藝術博物館在一場官司之後，不情願地把 363 件金器、銀器、寶石、畫作和雕塑歸還給土耳其。數十年或幾百年前從原地帶走的藝術品，要求歸還的呼聲愈來愈高；面對這樣的情勢，世界上一些頂尖的博物館簽署了一份聲明，表示不會把古代文物歸還給文物的原屬國。這些博物館說，全世界的人之所以能夠充分欣賞古代文明，只因為博物館讓大家看得到這些藝術品。要不是歐洲和美洲各大博物館把這些古代文物廣泛地展示給世界各地的大眾，這些古代文明今天不會這麼受人推崇。例如，要不是博物館展出埃及的木乃伊，埃及文化不會變得這麼有名。

第 2 段大英博物館沒有簽署這份聲明，但表示完全支持。近年來，大英博物館面臨愈來愈大的壓力，要求歸還埃爾金石雕；這批雕塑是 19 世紀從希臘雅典的帕德嫩神殿拿走的。但是大英博物館表示，館方就是這批雕塑最好的歸宿。館長說：「博物館要繼續達成它的宗旨，也就是把世界展示給世界看，這批雕塑就必須留在這裡。」

全文中心思想與段落主旨句

兩者都是　全文中心思想　段落主旨句
藍底標示全文中心思想，底線標示段落主旨句，一句同時是兩者，就用藍底加底線。下面依段落列出這些句子。

第 1 段Following increasing calls for the return of artistic objects that were removed decades or centuries ago, some of the world’s leading museums have signed a declaration that they will not hand back the ancient artifacts to their countries of origin.

第 2 段The British Museum has not signed the declaration, but says it fully supports it.

📌 提醒：不是每一段都有一句明講的主旨句。開頭鋪陳的段落、承接前一段的延伸段落，還有敘事或描寫的段落，主旨常常藏在字裡行間，找不到一句可以直接畫線。遇到這種段落，與其硬找一句話，不如問自己「這一段在做什麼」（舉例？補充？延續前一段？），再用自己的話歸納出這一段的重點就可以了。
