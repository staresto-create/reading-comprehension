85 學測閱讀測驗：大腦兩半球與語言學習

Brain Hemispheres and Language Learning
學測 85年第 47–50 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段The brain, which weighs less than 2.2 pounds, is perhaps the most complicated organ in our bodies. Although scientists have not been able to solve all the mysteries of this amazing organ, they have made some progress. They have found that certain parts of the brain are responsible for learning, memory, and language.

第 2 段Recent studies indicate that the two halves of the brain—the right hemisphere and the left hemisphere — play extremely important roles in learning and communicating. The left hemisphere deals with rules, lists of information, and short-term memory. In contrast, the right hemisphere deals with feelings, colors, and long-term memory. Scientists recognize the importance of both hemispheres in the learning of all sorts, including language learning.

第 3 段Scientists now relate left and right hemispheres to the way different individuals learn languages. They believe that some learners use one half of their brains more than the other half. Left-brained learners usually concentrate on memorizing rules and lists. They use logic, definition, and repetition to learn. Right-brained learners look for a general picture and concentrate on relating new information to what they already know. They use associations and intuition to learn. Most people fall into one of these types. If teachers know whether their students are left-brained or right-brained, they can help them learn better.

題目

47.Mary is a typical left-brained learner. Which of the following descriptions does NOT fit her?

  1. (A)She thinks in a very logical way.
  2. (B)She is very artistic and romantic.
  3. (C)She often scores A in sentence-combining tests.
  4. (D)She is very good at math.

48.What is the most significant implication that teachers can draw from the above information? They should

  1. (A)encourage students to memorize rules.
  2. (B)show students how to use logic to solve problems
  3. (C)urge students to relate new information to what they already know.
  4. (D)try to find out the best learning styles for individual students.

49.Which of the following statements is TRUE?

  1. (A)Damage of one hemisphere does not always cause complete loss of speech.
  2. (B)Remembering one's way home involves short-term memory.
  3. (C)Language learning takes place only in one hemisphere.
  4. (D)Scientists have solved all the mysteries of the brain.

50.What is the best title of this passage?

  1. (A)The Short-term and Long-term Memory
  2. (B)Discoveries in the Field of Hemispheres
  3. (C)A Recent Scientific Discovery
  4. (D)The Amazing Human Brain

每一題的依據句

第 47 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 2 段In contrast, the right hemisphere deals with feelings, colors, and long-term memory.

第 3 段They use logic, definition, and repetition to learn.

第 3 段說左腦型學習者專注於背規則和清單，用邏輯、定義和反覆練習來學習；第 2 段說處理感受、色彩的是右半球。富有藝術氣息又浪漫，靠的是感受和色彩，比較像右腦型，所以不符合 Mary 的是 (B)。其他三個選項都符合左腦型：思考很有邏輯，是 (A)；句子合併測驗要照規則組合句子，常拿 A，是 (C)；擅長數學要靠邏輯，是 (D)。
第 48 題 推論題 Inference

第 3 段If teachers know whether their students are left-brained or right-brained, they can help them learn better.

第 3 段最後說，如果老師知道學生是左腦型還是右腦型，就能幫助他們學得更好。老師要先找出每個學生適合的學習方式，所以是 (D)。(A) 照抄 rules，背規則是左腦型學習者的方法；(B) 照抄 logic，用邏輯也是左腦型的方法；(C) 照抄 new information to what they already know，連結新舊資訊是右腦型的方法。這三個選項都只適合其中一型的學生。
第 49 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 2 段Scientists recognize the importance of both hemispheres in the learning of all sorts, including language learning.

第 2 段說兩個半球對各種學習都很重要，語言學習也包括在內。語言既然不只靠一個半球，一邊受損時，另一邊還能發揮作用，不一定會完全失去說話能力，所以是 (A)。(C) 照抄 language learning，原文說的正好相反，語言學習要靠兩個半球；(B) 照抄 short-term memory，回家的路要長久記住，屬於長期記憶；(D) 照抄第 1 段的 mysteries，原文說科學家還沒解開所有謎團。
第 50 題 主旨題 Main idea

依據句：全文要讀完整篇才能判斷，不在某一句裡。

第 1 段說大腦也許是身體裡最複雜的器官，稱它為 amazing organ，接著第 2、3 段介紹左右半球的分工，以及左右腦和學語言方式的關係，全文談的都是人類的大腦，所以是 (D)。(A) 短期和長期記憶只在第 2 段提到一句，過於狹隘；(B) 左右半球的研究發現是第 2、3 段的內容，沒有涵蓋第 1 段，過於狹隘；(C) 只說是一項最新的科學發現，沒有點出大腦，太過廣泛。
錯誤選項：(A) 過於狹隘（只講第 2 段）；(B) 過於狹隘（只講第 2、3 段）；(C) 太過廣泛

逐段中譯

第 1 段大腦重不到 2.2 磅，也許是我們身體裡最複雜的器官。科學家雖然還沒能解開這個神奇器官的所有謎團，但已經有一些進展。他們發現，大腦的某些部位負責學習、記憶和語言。

第 2 段最近的研究指出，大腦的兩半，也就是右半球和左半球，在學習和溝通上扮演極為重要的角色。左半球處理規則、一連串的資訊和短期記憶。相反地，右半球處理感受、色彩和長期記憶。科學家認為兩個半球對各種學習都很重要，語言學習也包括在內。

第 3 段科學家現在把左右半球和每個人學語言的方式連在一起。他們認為，有些學習者用其中一半大腦用得比另一半多。左腦型的學習者通常專注於背規則和清單，用邏輯、定義和反覆練習來學習。右腦型的學習者會先看整體的樣貌，專注於把新資訊和已經知道的東西連結起來，靠聯想和直覺來學習。大多數人都屬於這兩型的其中一種。如果老師知道學生是左腦型還是右腦型，就能幫助他們學得更好。

字詞搭配 配對與接法

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
  • make progress有進展第 1 段原文：they have made some progress
  • be responsible for + N負責……第 1 段原文：certain parts of the brain are responsible for learning, memory, and language
  • play an important role in + N在……扮演重要角色第 2 段原文：play extremely important roles in learning and communicating
  • deal with + N處理……第 2 段原文：The left hemisphere deals with rules
  • short-term memory短期記憶第 2 段
  • relate A to B把 A 和 B 連在一起第 3 段原文：relate left and right hemispheres to the way different individuals learn languages
  • concentrate on + V-ing專注在……第 3 段原文：concentrate on memorizing rules and lists
  • fall into + N屬於（某一類）第 3 段原文：Most people fall into one of these types
