101 學測閱讀測驗：如何拍攝動物

How to Photograph Animals
學測 101年第 53–56 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Animals are a favorite subject of many photographers. Cats, dogs, and other pets top the list, followed by zoo animals. However, because it’s hard to get them to sit still and “perform on command,” some professional photographers refuse to photograph pets.

第 2 段One way to get an appealing portrait of a cat or dog is to hold a biscuit or treat above the camera. The animal’s longing look toward the food will be captured by the camera, but the treat won’t appear in the picture because it’s out of the camera’s range. When you show the picture to your friends afterwards, they’ll be impressed by your pet’s loving expression.

第 3 段If you are using fast film, you can take some good, quick shots of a pet by simply snapping a picture right after calling its name. You’ll get a different expression from your pet using this technique. Depending on your pet’s mood, the picture will capture an interested, curious expression or possibly a look of annoyance, especially if you’ve awakened it from a nap.

第 4 段Taking pictures of zoo animals requires a little more patience. After all, you can’t wake up a lion! You may have to wait for a while until the animal does something interesting or moves into a position for you to get a good shot. When photographing zoo animals, don’t get too close to the cages, and never tap on the glass or throw things between the bars of a cage. Concentrate on shooting some good pictures, and always respect the animals you are photographing.

題目

53.Why do some professional photographers NOT like to take pictures of pets?

  1. (A)Pets may not follow orders.
  2. (B)Pets don’t want to be bothered.
  3. (C)Pets may not like photographers.
  4. (D)Pets seldom change their expressions.

54.What is the use of a biscuit in taking pictures of a pet?

  1. (A)To capture a cute look.
  2. (B)To create a special atmosphere.
  3. (C)To arouse the appetite of the pet.
  4. (D)To keep the pet from looking at the camera.

55.What is the advantage of calling your pet’s name when taking a shot of it?

  1. (A)To help your pet look its best.
  2. (B)To make sure that your pet sits still.
  3. (C)To keep your pet awake for a while.
  4. (D)To catch a different expression of your pet.

56.In what way is photographing zoo animals different from photographing pets?

  1. (A)You need to have fast film.
  2. (B)You need special equipment.
  3. (C)You need to stay close to the animals.
  4. (D)You need more time to watch and wait.

第 53 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段However, because it’s hard to get them to sit still and “perform on command,” some professional photographers refuse to photograph pets.

原文 → 正確選項
perform on command → follow orders
第 1 段說很難讓動物乖乖坐好、聽命令表演（perform on command），所以有些專業攝影師拒絕替寵物拍照，選項換成 may not follow orders。(B) 第 3 段說叫名字時可能吵醒寵物、拍到不耐煩的表情，那是一個拍照技巧可能遇到的情況，不是攝影師不拍寵物的原因；(D) 第 3 段說叫名字就能拍到寵物不同的表情，和選項正好相反；(C) 寵物不喜歡攝影師，文章沒提。
第 54 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 2 段One way to get an appealing portrait of a cat or dog is to hold a biscuit or treat above the camera.

第 2 段The animal’s longing look toward the food will be captured by the camera,

原文 → 正確選項
appealing → cute
longing look → a cute look
第 2 段一開頭就說，要拍出討人喜愛（appealing）的照片，方法是把餅乾或零食舉在相機上方，相機會拍下寵物望著食物的渴望眼神（longing look）。用餅乾的目的是拍到可愛的表情，所以是 (A)。(C) 引起寵物的食慾只是手段，拍下那個眼神才是目的；(D) 照抄 camera，零食舉在相機上方，寵物看零食時視線正好朝著鏡頭附近，不是要讓寵物別看相機；(B) 營造特別的氣氛，文章沒提。
第 55 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 3 段You’ll get a different expression from your pet using this technique.

原文 → 正確選項
get a different expression → catch a different expression
第 3 段說叫寵物的名字後馬上按快門，用這個技巧可以拍到寵物不同的表情（get a different expression），選項把 get 換成 catch。(C) 照抄 awake，原文說的是可能把午睡的寵物吵醒，拍到不耐煩的表情，不是要讓寵物保持清醒；(B) 第 1 段說讓寵物乖乖坐好（sit still）正是很難做到的事，叫名字不是為了這個；(A) 原文說可能拍到一臉不耐煩，不一定是最好看的樣子。
第 56 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 4 段Taking pictures of zoo animals requires a little more patience.

第 4 段You may have to wait for a while until the animal does something interesting or moves into a position for you to get a good shot.

原文 → 正確選項
wait for a while → watch and wait
第 4 段說拍動物園的動物需要多一點耐心，可能得等一陣子（wait for a while），等動物做出有趣的動作，選項換成 more time to watch and wait。(A) 照抄第 3 段的 fast film，高感光度底片是拍寵物時用的；(C) 照抄 close，原文說的正好相反，不要太靠近籠子；(B) 特殊器材文章沒提。

逐段中譯

第 1 段動物是許多攝影師最喜歡拍的題材。貓、狗和其他寵物排第一，其次是動物園的動物。不過，因為很難讓動物乖乖坐好、「聽命令表演」，有些專業攝影師拒絕替寵物拍照。

第 2 段要替貓或狗拍出討人喜愛的照片，有一個方法是把餅乾或零食舉在相機上方。相機會拍下寵物望著食物的渴望眼神，但零食不會出現在照片裡，因為零食在相機的拍攝範圍之外。事後你把照片拿給朋友看，朋友會對你家寵物充滿愛意的表情印象深刻。

第 3 段如果你用的是高感光度底片，只要叫寵物的名字，然後馬上按下快門，就能迅速拍出幾張好照片。用這個技巧可以拍到寵物不同的表情。依寵物當時的心情而定，照片可能拍到感興趣、好奇的表情，也可能是一臉不耐煩，尤其是你把寵物從午睡中吵醒的時候。

第 4 段拍動物園的動物需要多一點耐心。畢竟，你總不能把獅子叫醒吧！你可能得等一陣子，等動物做出有趣的動作，或是移動到好拍的位置。拍動物園的動物時，不要太靠近籠子，也絕對不要敲玻璃，或把東西丟進籠子的欄杆之間。專心拍出好照片，而且永遠要尊重你拍攝的動物。

綱要
  1. 動物是熱門攝影題材，(However, because) 但動物難以聽令，有些專業攝影師拒拍寵物。
  2. 方法一：把零食舉在相機上方，拍下寵物渴望的眼神。
  3. 方法二：用高感光底片，叫寵物名字後立刻按快門，捕捉不同表情。
  4. 拍動物園動物需要更多耐心，(After all) 畢竟不能叫醒獅子。
  5. 提醒拍攝時不要靠籠子太近、不要敲玻璃或丟東西，要尊重動物。
