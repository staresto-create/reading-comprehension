Comparison of Green Anole and Chameleon, and Anole's Research Value
指考 95課綱參考試卷（98年施測）第 40–43 題4 題

文章

第 1 段The green anole lizard is usually sold in pet shops as a chameleon, by which name it has been known to generations of American children. The true chameleon and the green anole are actually different animals, but they do have much in common. They are both lizards. Most live in trees or bushes, subsisting mainly on insects. Both can change color, although the anole’s ability to do so is considerably more limited than the chameleon’s. This is the trait that has made chameleons and anoles popular as pets. However, the anole’s color change, in contrast to the chameleon’s, is not, as many people think, related to the color of the background. Instead, it is determined by factors such as light and temperature or by emotions such as fright, triumph, or defeat. The chameleon is an animal of the Old World, whereas the anole is found in the warmer regions of North and South America. The chameleon lays from two to forty eggs at a time; the anole only a single egg. Recently, biologists have found that the anole is an excellent subject for laboratory studies of the interaction between behavior and hormones. The particular value of green anoles as experimental animals is that they are abundant and, under appropriate conditions, they will establish the same social system and behavior in the laboratory that they display in their natural environment.

逐段中譯

第 1 段綠變色蜥在寵物店裡通常當作變色龍來賣，好幾代的美國孩子都知道它叫變色龍。真正的變色龍和綠變色蜥其實是不同的動物，不過兩者確實有很多共同點。牠們都是蜥蜴，大多住在樹上或灌木叢裡，主要靠吃昆蟲維生。兩者都會變色，只是綠變色蜥變色的能力比變色龍有限得多。正是這個特點，讓變色龍和綠變色蜥成了受歡迎的寵物。不過，和變色龍不同的是，綠變色蜥變色並不像很多人以為的，和背景的顏色有關，而是取決於光線和溫度等因素，或是害怕、得意、挫敗等情緒。變色龍是舊大陸的動物，綠變色蜥則分布在南北美洲較溫暖的地區。變色龍一次產下兩顆到四十顆蛋，綠變色蜥一次只產一顆。最近，生物學家發現，綠變色蜥很適合在實驗室裡研究行為和荷爾蒙之間的交互作用。綠變色蜥作為實驗動物的特別價值，在於數量很多，而且只要條件合適，牠們在實驗室裡會建立起和在自然環境中一樣的社會體系和行為。
