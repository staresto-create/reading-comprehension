93 指考敏督利補考閱讀測驗：語言實驗室的發展與應用補考

Development and Application of Language Laboratories
指考 93年敏督利補考第 53–56 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段The best-known technological aid in language teaching is undoubtedly the language laboratory—a room, usually divided into booths, where students can listen individually to tape recordings of foreign language material, and where they may record and play back their own responses, while being monitored by a teacher.

第 2 段When these laboratories were first introduced, they were heralded as a technique that would improve the rate and quality of language learning. They would take the burden of repetitive drills away from the teacher, provide more opportunities for learners to practice listening and speaking, and enable them to develop at their own rates and monitor their own progress. Many schools were quick to install expensive laboratory equipment. However, within a few years, it became apparent that there would be no breakthrough. The expected improvements did not materialize, and the popularity of the “language lab” showed a marked decline.

第 3 段There were several reasons for the language lab’s failure to live up to expectations. The taped materials were often poorly designed, leading to student frustration and boredom. The published programs failed to reflect the kind of work the student was doing in class. Few modern languages staff had received training in materials design or laboratory use. And it proved difficult to maintain the equipment once it had been installed.

第 4 段Today, the strengths and limitations of the laboratory are better realized, and the vastly increased potential of modern electronic hardware has led to a certain revival. There is now considerable interest in language learning laboratories, which contain much more than the traditional systems—in particular, the introduction of interactive computational aids and video materials has proved to be extremely popular.

第 5 段It is now clear that, when used properly, laboratories can provide a valuable extra dimension to language teaching. For example, the taped material can provide a variety of authentic and well-recorded models for the training of listening comprehension. And laboratories can be used as resource centers, or libraries, giving learners extra opportunities to practice at their chosen level.

逐段中譯

第 1 段語言教學中最有名的科技輔助工具，無疑是語言教室。語言教室通常隔成一間間小隔間，學生可以在裡面各自聽外語教材的錄音帶，也可以錄下自己的回答再播放出來，老師則在一旁監聽。

第 2 段語言教室剛推出時，大家大力宣揚語言教室是能提升語言學習速度和品質的技術。語言教室可以減輕老師帶反覆練習的負擔，讓學習者有更多機會練習聽和說，也讓他們能按照自己的速度進步，並自己檢視學習進度。許多學校很快就裝設了昂貴的語言教室設備。然而，不到幾年，大家就發現不會有什麼突破。預期的效果沒有出現，「語言教室」也明顯不再那麼受歡迎。

第 3 段語言教室沒有達到預期，原因有好幾個。錄音教材往往設計不良，讓學生感到挫折、無聊。市面上的教材和學生在課堂上學的內容脫節。很少現代語言科的老師受過教材設計或語言教室使用的訓練。而且設備裝好以後，維護起來很困難。

第 4 段如今，大家比較了解語言教室的優點和限制，加上現代電子硬體的功能大幅提升，語言教室某種程度上又重新興起。現在大家對語言學習教室很有興趣，這種教室的設備比傳統的系統豐富得多，特別是互動式電腦輔助工具和影音教材的引進，已經證明非常受歡迎。

第 5 段現在大家都很清楚，只要使用得當，語言教室能替語言教學增加一個寶貴的面向。例如，錄音教材可以提供各種道地、錄音品質好的示範，用來訓練聽力；語言教室也可以當作資源中心或圖書館，讓學習者按照自己選擇的程度，有更多機會練習。
