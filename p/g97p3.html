97 學測閱讀測驗：冰雕藝術的挑戰與應用

Challenges and Applications of Ice Sculpting
學測 97年第 49–52 題4 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開最後的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Ice sculpting is a difficult process. First, ice must be carefully selected so that it is suitable for sculpting. Its ideal material is pure, clean water with high clarity. It should also have the minimum amount of air bubbles. Perfectly clear ice blocks weighing 140 kg and measuring 100 cm × 50 cm × 25 cm are available from the Clinebell Company in Colorado. Much larger clear blocks are produced in Europe and Canada or harvested from a frozen river in Sweden. These large ice blocks are used for large ice sculpting events and for building ice hotels.

第 2 段Another difficulty in the process of ice sculpting is time control. The temperature of the environment affects how quickly the piece must be completed to avoid the effects of melting. If the sculpting does not take place in a cold environment, then the sculptor must work quickly to finish his piece. The tools used for sculpting also affect when the task can be accomplished. Some sculptures can be completed in as little as ten minutes if power tools are used. Ice sculptors also use razor-sharp chisels that are specifically designed for cutting ice. The best ice chisels are made in Japan, a country that, along with China, has a long tradition of magnificent ice sculptures.

第 3 段Ice sculptures are used as decorations in some cuisines, especially in Asia. When holding a dinner party, some large restaurants or hotels will use an ice sculpture to decorate the table. For example, in a wedding banquet it is common to see a pair of ice-sculpted swans that represent the union of the new couple.

49.What kind of ice is ideal for sculpting?

  1. (A)Ice from ice hotels.
  2. (B)Ice from clean water.
  3. (C)Ice with lots of bubbles in it.
  4. (D)Ice weighing over 100 kilograms.

50.Why is ice sculpting difficult?

  1. (A)It is hard to control the size and shape of the ice.
  2. (B)The right theme for ice sculpting is not easy to find.
  3. (C)The appropriate tools are only available in some countries.
  4. (D)It is not easy to find the right kind of ice and work environment.

51.What is paragraph 3 mainly about?

  1. (A)The uses of ice sculptures.
  2. (B)The places where ice is sculpted.
  3. (C)The quality of ice sculptures.
  4. (D)The origin of ice sculpting parties.

52.Which of the following statements is true about the process of sculpting ice?

  1. (A)It takes more time to carve with razor-sharp chisels.
  2. (B)It can be finished in 10 minutes if the right tools are used.
  3. (C)Larger blocks of ice from Sweden are easier to handle for sculptors.
  4. (D)The carver must work fast in a cold environment to avoid catching cold.

每一題的依據句

第 49 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段Its ideal material is pure, clean water with high clarity.

原文 → 正確選項
clean water → clean water
第 1 段說冰雕理想的材料是純淨、乾淨（clean water）、透明度高的水，所以是 (B)。(C) 照抄 bubbles，原文說氣泡要越少越好，正好相反；(A) 照抄 ice hotels，冰旅館是用大冰塊蓋的，不是冰塊的來源；(D) 照抄 100，100 公分是冰塊的長度，每塊重 140 公斤只是 Clinebell 公司的規格，原文沒說超過 100 公斤才理想。
第 50 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段First, ice must be carefully selected so that it is suitable for sculpting.

第 2 段Another difficulty in the process of ice sculpting is time control.

第 2 段If the sculpting does not take place in a cold environment, then the sculptor must work quickly to finish his piece.

原文 → 正確選項
carefully selected → find the right kind of ice
第 1 段說第一個困難是冰塊必須仔細挑選（carefully selected）；第 2 段說另一個困難是控制時間，不在寒冷的環境裡就得加快動作。選項把兩段合起來，說找到合適的冰塊和工作環境都不容易，所以是 (D)。(A) 照抄 control，要控制的是時間，不是冰塊的大小和形狀；(C) 照抄 tools，原文說工具會影響完成的時間，最好的冰鑿是日本製的，沒說只有某些國家買得到；(B) 雕刻的主題，原文沒有提到。
第 51 題 主旨題 Main idea

第 3 段Ice sculptures are used as decorations in some cuisines, especially in Asia.

原文 → 正確選項
used as decorations → uses
第 3 段第一句是主題句：冰雕在某些料理中用來當作裝飾（used as decorations），接著舉晚宴的餐桌和婚宴上的冰雕天鵝為例，所以是 (A) 冰雕的用途。(B) 第 3 段提到的餐廳和飯店是擺放冰雕的地方，不是雕冰的地方；(C) 冰塊的品質是第 1 段的內容；(D) 冰雕派對的起源，原文沒有提到。
第 52 題 細節題（何者為是） Supporting details

第 2 段Some sculptures can be completed in as little as ten minutes if power tools are used.

原文 → 正確選項
ten minutes → 10 minutes
power tools → right tools
第 2 段說如果使用電動工具（power tools），有些作品只要十分鐘就能完成，選項換成 the right tools，所以是 (B)。(D) 照抄 cold environment，原文說不在寒冷的環境裡才要加快動作，而且是為了避免冰融化，不是怕感冒；(A) 照抄 razor-sharp chisels，原文只說冰雕師會用這種鑿子，沒說用它比較費時；(C) 照抄第 1 段的 Sweden，瑞典的大冰塊用在大型活動和冰旅館，原文沒說比較容易處理。

逐段中譯

第 1 段冰雕是一個困難的過程。首先，冰塊必須仔細挑選，才適合雕刻。理想的材料是純淨、乾淨、透明度高的水，氣泡也要越少越好。科羅拉多州的 Clinebell 公司供應完全透明的冰塊，每塊重 140 公斤，尺寸是 100 公分 × 50 公分 × 25 公分。更大的透明冰塊則在歐洲和加拿大生產，或是從瑞典一條結冰的河上採集。這些大冰塊用在大型冰雕活動，也用來蓋冰旅館。

第 2 段冰雕過程中的另一個困難是控制時間。環境的溫度會影響作品必須多快完成，才不會融化。如果不是在寒冷的環境裡雕刻，雕刻師就必須加快動作，才能完成作品。雕刻用的工具也會影響多久能完成。如果使用電動工具，有些作品只要十分鐘就能完成。冰雕師也會使用專門用來切冰、像剃刀一樣鋒利的鑿子。最好的冰鑿是日本製的；日本和中國一樣，都有製作壯觀冰雕的悠久傳統。

第 3 段在某些料理中，冰雕用來當作裝飾，在亞洲尤其如此。舉辦晚宴時，有些大型餐廳或飯店會用冰雕裝飾餐桌。例如在婚宴上，常常可以看到一對冰雕的天鵝，代表新人的結合。

綱要
  1. (First) 闡述冰雕的第一個難點：冰塊的選擇，(Its ideal material is) 理想材料要求高純度、高透明度且氣泡最少。
  2. 介紹冰塊的來源：科羅拉多州的 Clinebell 公司生產的標準塊，以及歐洲、加拿大生產或從瑞典結冰的河上採集的更大冰塊。
  3. (Another difficulty) 闡述冰雕的第二個難點：時間控制，環境溫度影響融化速度，(If … then) 若不在寒冷環境，雕塑家必須快速完成作品。
  4. (The tools used … also affect) 提及工具對完成時間的影響，(Some sculptures can be completed in as little as … if) 使用電動工具可快速完成，並介紹日本的冰雕鑿子與傳統。
