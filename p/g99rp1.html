The History and Culture of the Ocarina
學測 99課綱（102年施測）第 41–44 題4 題

文章

第 1 段The ocarina, a flute-like musical instrument, has been around since the Stone Age. Ocarinas have been found in Africa, Asia, Europe, and South America. Most of those found were made from clay while some were made of bone, wood, and other materials. Because they produce sounds similar to bird calls, ocarinas have been credited with magical powers.

第 2 段One variation of the ocarina in Chinese culture is the xun, which has a history of more than 7,000 years. It is made of earthenware clay and often comes in the shape of an egg. At various times in Chinese music history, the instrument has played an important role in the imperial orchestra for religious worship and palace entertainment.

第 3 段Ocarinas were also found to serve various purposes in other cultures. For example, the Mayans of South America, like the Chinese, also used ocarinas for special ceremonies. From there European travelers brought this South American “whistle” back, and it was later modified to play the Western scale. In the 1800s, Italy saw the greatest development of the ocarina, where musicians formed groups to play ocarinas of various sizes. During World War II the ocarina was introduced to the United States and playing it became a pastime for soldiers.

第 4 段In the 20th century, the ocarina was popular among the general public, and the application of ocarina music was extended to video games. In 1999, Nintendo released Zelda: the Ocarina of Time, the most popular video game of all time. The game features a character named Link who plays songs on an ocarina in order to go to different areas of the game.

逐段中譯

第 1 段陶笛是一種類似長笛的樂器，從石器時代就已經存在。非洲、亞洲、歐洲和南美洲都發現過陶笛。發現的陶笛大多是用黏土做的，也有一些是用骨頭、木頭和其他材料做成。陶笛發出的聲音很像鳥叫，所以人們認為陶笛有神奇的魔力。

第 2 段陶笛在中國文化裡的一種變化型是塤，有七千多年的歷史。塤是用陶土燒製而成，常常做成蛋的形狀。在中國音樂史上的不同時期，塤曾經在宮廷樂隊裡扮演重要的角色，用於宗教祭祀和宮廷娛樂。

第 3 段在其他文化裡，陶笛也有各種用途。例如南美洲的馬雅人和中國人一樣，也在特殊的儀式上使用陶笛。後來歐洲的旅人把這種南美洲的「哨子」帶回歐洲，經過改良，陶笛就能吹奏西方音階。1800 年代，陶笛在義大利發展得最好，樂手組成樂團，吹奏各種尺寸的陶笛。第二次世界大戰期間，陶笛傳入美國，吹陶笛成為士兵的消遣。

第 4 段二十世紀時，陶笛在一般大眾之間很受歡迎，陶笛音樂的應用也延伸到電玩遊戲。1999 年，任天堂推出《薩爾達傳說：時之笛》，這是有史以來最受歡迎的電玩遊戲。遊戲裡有一個叫做 Link 的角色，他用陶笛吹奏樂曲，好前往遊戲裡的不同區域。

字詞搭配 配對與接法

「字詞搭配」是要整組記的說法，分兩種：一種是哪兩個字配在一起，例如 pose a serious risk（造成嚴重的風險）、in unison（齊聲），換成意思相近的字就不自然；另一種是這個字後面要接什麼，寫成公式，例如 be resistant to + N（對……有抵抗力）。公式裡的 + N 表示接名詞，+ V 接原形動詞，+ V-ing 接動名詞，sb 指人，sth 指事物，「時間」「地方」這類中文表示那裡要填的東西。名稱不必背，看得出這一條寫的是「配對」還是「接法」就夠了；記住搭配和接法，這個字你才用得出來，不會只是看得懂。一看就懂的組合和專有名詞不列。每一條寫的是查字典時的基本形，文中的寫法不同時，下面附上原文。
  • be around存在第 1 段原文：has been around since the Stone Age
  • be made from + N由……製成第 1 段原文：Most of those found were made from clay
  • be credited with + N一般認為具有……第 1 段原文：ocarinas have been credited with magical powers
  • come in the shape of + N形狀像……第 2 段原文：often comes in the shape of an egg
  • play an important role in + N在……扮演重要角色第 2 段原文：the instrument has played an important role in the imperial orchestra
  • serve a purpose發揮作用、有某種用途第 3 段原文：Ocarinas were also found to serve various purposes
  • 時間／地方 + see + N某時、某地出現……第 3 段原文：Italy saw the greatest development of the ocarina
  • be introduced to + 地方傳入……第 3 段原文：the ocarina was introduced to the United States
  • a pastime for + sb某人的消遣第 3 段原文：a pastime for soldiers
  • be popular among + N受……歡迎第 4 段原文：was popular among the general public
