84 學測閱讀測驗：電腦駭客與網路犯罪

Computer Hacking and Cybercrime
學測 84年第 41–43 題3 題

先讀文章、作答下方的題目，再展開後面的「看答案、依據句與逐段中譯」和「這一篇的生字表」。也可以到互動練習線上作答，交卷後自動批改。

文章

第 1 段Breaking into the computer codes (known as 'hacking') of banks, large companies or even government departments is the latest game for super-intelligent teenagers. One young hacker said, "Hacking is just intellectual. It's your brain against the computer. It's like climbing Mount Everest—it's something you have to do. You don't even need a very expensive computer—but you must understand everything about all kinds of computers."

第 2 段At the moment most of these games are just fun for young people. But how long will it be before criminal gangs, like the Mafia, start to use computer experts to help them in their crimes? Future bank robbers will not need explosives to blow open bank safes—all they will need is someone who can break computer codes. Already in Britain people say that computer crimes are costing companies between 500 million pounds and 2.5 billion pounds a year.

題目

41.The preceding passage refers to the word 'hacking' in the context of

  1. (A)climbing Mount Everest.
  2. (B)a game for young individuals.
  3. (C)breaking into computer codes.
  4. (D)physically dismantling a solid object.

42.According to the passage, breaking into computer codes of banks, large companies, and government departments is

  1. (A)a widespread criminal practice.
  2. (B)a game for computer literate individuals.
  3. (C)being studied by the Mafia for fun.
  4. (D)soon to replace coding bank safes.

43.Based on the passage, in order to break into computer codes, one needs

  1. (A)an elaborate and expensive multi-media computer.
  2. (B)a group of computer science students.
  3. (C)the help and financial support from the Mafia.
  4. (D)a thorough understanding of a variety of computers.

第 43 題 細節題 Supporting details

第 1 段but you must understand everything about all kinds of computers

原文 → 正確選項
all kinds of computers → a variety of computers
第 1 段的年輕駭客說，一定要了解各種電腦的一切（understand everything about all kinds of computers），選項換成 a thorough understanding of a variety of computers，所以是 (D)。(A) 照抄 expensive，原文說的正好相反，不需要很貴的電腦；(C) 照抄第 2 段的 Mafia，黑手黨是可能找電腦專家幫忙的犯罪集團，不是駭客需要的支援；(B) 原文沒有提到一群念電腦科學的學生。

逐段中譯

第 1 段破解銀行、大企業，甚至政府部門的電腦密碼（也就是所謂的「駭客入侵」），是超級聰明的青少年最新的遊戲。一位年輕的駭客說：「當駭客純粹是動腦的事，是你的頭腦和電腦對決。就像爬聖母峰一樣，是你非做不可的事。你甚至不需要很貴的電腦，但你一定要了解各種電腦的一切。」

第 2 段目前，這些遊戲大多只是年輕人的消遣。可是，再過多久，像黑手黨這樣的犯罪集團就會開始找電腦專家幫他們犯罪呢？未來的銀行搶匪不需要用炸藥炸開銀行金庫，他們只需要一個能破解電腦密碼的人。在英國，已經有人說電腦犯罪每年讓企業損失 5 億到 25 億英鎊。
