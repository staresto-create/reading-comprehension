列舉：項目有先後，還是只是並列

109 學測　第 49–52 題　The Grades and Storage of Olive Oil

這一篇介紹橄欖油。你會自己點、自己驗證：先找出列舉標記，再用換位測試分辨兩種列舉，接著看每一段在做什麼，最後做一題篇章結構。

共四步，做完會出現重點整理。

已接續上次的進度。

先看全文（3 段）

第 1 段Probably the most widely used oil in cooking, olive oil is made by crushing olives with stone or steel blades to release the oil. Extra virgin oil, the finest grade, is made from the first pressing of the olives and has a very low acidity rate (under 1%). To be qualified as “extra virgin,” the oil must also pass both an official test in a laboratory and a sensory evaluation by a trained tasting panel. It must be free from impurity, while exhibiting some fruitiness and creating a pleasant bitter flavor along with a peppery sensation in the mouth. Second on the ranking is virgin olive oil. It is also a first pressing, but has a slightly higher acidity level (under 2%). Lastly, pure olive oil is the cheapest olive oil. It is refined to remove its impurities and blended to improve flavor, and is recommended for all-purpose cooking.

第 2 段Packaging and storage also have an impact on the quality of olive oil. （第四步的空格，做完再顯示） Moisture and oxygen promote oxidation and therefore limit its shelf life. Plastic containers usually do not provide adequate protection from light, heat, or moisture; additionally, small particles in plastics can leak into the oil, which further reduces its quality.

第 3 段Olive oil tasters have long adopted descriptive terms such as “pure” and “light” to evaluate the quality of olive oil. Recently, however, the International Olive Council (IOC) is seeking input on test methods to improve the transparency of the testing process and boost the accuracy of the results. A redefinition of the term “olive oil” is also proposed to make a clear distinction between virgin olive oil and pure olive oil. Industry members of the IOC hope to promote the trade of olive oil by initiating these changes.

第一步

先找出列舉標記

列舉是一項一項往下講。下面七張字卡來自文章，有四張是在帶出一個項目。把這四張找出來。點錯會變灰，還可以再點。

列舉標記不一定是轉折詞。the finest grade 用最高級告訴你這一項排第一，沒有 First 也看得出來；後面的 Second on the ranking 和 Lastly 再帶出第二項、第三項；additionally 也是帶出後面的項目。

therefore 和 however 也是轉折詞，但 therefore 表示因果，however 表示前後相反，都不是再加一項。Probably the most widely used oil 描述的是橄欖油整體，不是其中一項。

第二步

自己驗證：換位測試

方法：標記留在原位，只把兩個項目對調，看意思變不變。例如「冠軍是小美，亞軍是小明」，兩人對調，名次就變了；意思變了，表示項目有先後，沒變就是沒有先後的並列。

下面哪一組，對調順序之後意思會變？

Extra virgin oil, the finest grade, … Second on the ranking is virgin olive oil. … Lastly, pure olive oil is the cheapest olive oil.第 1 段
Plastic containers usually do not provide adequate protection …; additionally, small particles in plastics can leak into the oil, which further reduces its quality.第 2 段

列舉有兩種：項目有先後，和沒有先後的並列。

第 1 段的三種等級是排名：the finest grade、Second on the ranking、the cheapest，從等級最高排到最便宜的一級。標記留在原位、把項目對調，意思就不一樣了。第 2 段的 additionally 只是再加一個缺點，先講哪一個都可以。第 2 段保存時要避開的幾個不利條件、第 3 段想做的兩件事，也都是並列。

要分辨是哪一種，不能只看 Second、Lastly，因為這兩個字只告訴你講到第幾項；要看內容字：有沒有 finest、ranking、cheapest 這類表示排名的字。

第三步

每一段在做什麼

這篇文章用列舉來組織，但不是每一段都在列舉。下面三段，各選一個說法。選對會鎖定，選錯會變灰，還可以再選。

第 1 段：Extra virgin oil, the finest grade … Lastly, pure olive oil …
第 2 段：Packaging and storage also have an impact … additionally …
第 3 段：Olive oil tasters have long adopted … Recently, however, the International Olive Council …

前兩段各列舉一組，第 3 段轉到最新的動向。第 1 段排出三種等級，第 2 段列出影響品質的包裝與保存條件，這兩段各自有一串項目。第 3 段先說品評師一向用 pure、light 這類字評定品質，再用 however 轉到最近的改變：IOC 想改進檢驗方法，也提議重新定義 olive oil。這一段雖然也列了兩件事，但功能是交代最新的動向。

第四步

把一句話放回去

本題庫自編，仿篇章結構題

下面把第 2 段第二句抽掉了。四個句子，只有一句放得進去。前三步練的東西，這一題都用得上。

Packaging and storage also have an impact on the quality of olive oil. （空格） Moisture and oxygen promote oxidation and therefore limit its shelf life. Plastic containers usually do not provide adequate protection from light, heat, or moisture; additionally, small particles in plastics can leak into the oil, which further reduces its quality.第 2 段

答案是 (B)，三個線索要一起看。

空格要提供列舉的第一項。空格前一句只說包裝和保存會影響品質，沒有說是哪些條件；後面兩句才講到濕氣、氧氣和塑膠容器。所以空格最適合先講出第一個條件。

後面的字呼應前面。最後一句說塑膠容器擋不住 light, heat, or moisture：light 和 heat 呼應空格裡的 direct sunlight、dark 和 16-18°C，moisture 呼應空格後面的 Moisture and oxygen。

內容不能互相矛盾。(A) 說可以放在陽光直射的廚房檯面，和最後一句要擋住 light、heat 相反。(C) 的 This oxidation 要指前面已經講過的 oxidation，但 oxidation 要到空格下一句才第一次出現，指涉順序不對。(D) 說塑膠容器最安全，和最後一句 do not provide adequate protection 直接相反。

重點整理

  1. 列舉的第一項不一定有 First。要靠 the finest 這類排名的字，或是後面出現的 Second 來判斷。
  2. 列舉有兩種：項目有先後（排名、步驟），和沒有先後的並列。Second、Lastly 只告訴你講到第幾項；順序有沒有意義，要看 ranking、finest、cheapest 這類內容字。換位測試可以自己驗證。
  3. therefore 表示因果，however 表示前後相反，都不是再加一項。
  4. 整篇用列舉來組織，但不是每一段都在列舉。要看轉折詞，判斷列舉在哪裡結束。
